    Humanoids

    Surena IV

    Also known as Sorena IV

    n., humanoid. University of Tehran, Center of Advanced Systems and Technologies, Iran, 2019. Research platform.

    Iranian full size humanoid with 43 degrees of freedom built to a low cost design that replaces force sensors with predictive foot contact sensing.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of Tehran, Center of Advanced Systems and Technologies
    CountryIran
    First shown2019
    StatusResearch platform
    Height1.7 m
    Mass68 kg
    Degrees of freedom43
    ActuationBrushless direct current motors with harmonic drive gears
    SensorsPredictive foot contact encoders, cameras, inertial unit
    AutonomyOnboard walking control with vision based manipulation

    Surena IV stands 170 cm tall and weighs 68 kg, with seven degree of freedom arms, six degree of freedom hands, six degree of freedom legs, a two joint waist and a three joint neck driven by brushless motors through harmonic drives. Instead of costly six axis foot sensors it uses encoders at the foot corners to predict ground contact about 1.5 cm before touchdown, which lets it walk over unknown ground with small steps and slopes. The University of Tehran team unveiled it in December 2019 and has demonstrated grasping objects of varying stiffness, visual object tracking, writing and using a drill.

    Of note

    • Fourth generation of the Iranian Surena programme, which began in 2008
    • Uses low cost predictive foot sensors in place of six axis force sensors
    • The IEEE included it among the world's notable robots after assessing its performance
