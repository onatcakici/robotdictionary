    Underwater and marine

    ENDURANCE

    n., underwater robot. Stone Aerospace.

    Autonomous underwater vehicle built by Stone Aerospace to chart Antarctic underwater terrain in 3D, recording its geochemistry and biology.

    Measures and facts
    MakerStone Aerospace

    Of note

    • A reworked second version of the DEPTHX vehicle
    • Substantially reconfigured for Antarctic conditions
