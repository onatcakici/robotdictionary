    Underwater and marine

    REMUS

    Also known as Remote Environmental Monitoring UnitS

    n., underwater robot. Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution. In production.

    Family of low cost autonomous underwater vehicles from the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, later built by Hydroid, used for seabed mapping, surveys and naval mine countermeasures.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWoods Hole Oceanographic Institution
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Controllable from an ordinary laptop computer
    • Huntington Ingalls Industries bought Hydroid in March 2020
    • Name stands for Remote Environmental Monitoring UnitS
