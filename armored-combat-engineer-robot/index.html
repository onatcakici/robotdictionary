    Wheeled machines

    Armored Combat Engineer Robot

    n., wheeled machine. Mesa Robotics.

    Tracked military robot from Mesa Robotics, about as big as a small bulldozer with a weight of 2.25 tons, used to clear obstacles and explosives and haul cargo.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMesa Robotics

    Of note

    • Top speed of 6.3 mph on its tracks
    • Can also tow disabled vehicles and help disarm landmines
