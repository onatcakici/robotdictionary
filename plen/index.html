    Humanoids

    PLEN

    n., humanoid.

    Tabletop humanoid toy robot steered from a Bluetooth phone that can ride a skateboard or roller skates and lift, kick and toss small items.

    Measures and facts

    Of note

    • Controlled remotely from a Bluetooth enabled phone
    • Has no sensors; every movement is remote controlled
    • Can stand back up after falling over
