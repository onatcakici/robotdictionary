    Quadrupeds

    CyberDog 2

    n., quadruped. Xiaomi, China, 2023. In production.

    A doberman sized successor that learns tricks from demonstration.

    Measures and facts
    MakerXiaomi
    CountryChina
    First shown2023
    StatusIn production
    Massapprox. 9 kg

    CyberDog 2 shrank Xiaomi's robot dog to doberman scale and taught it tricks by demonstration, sold nearly at cost to grow a developer ecosystem.
