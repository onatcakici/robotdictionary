    Wheeled machines

    MATILDA

    Also known as Mesa Associate's Tactical Integrated Light-Force Deployment Assembly

    n., wheeled machine. Mesa Robotics. In production.

    Remotely operated scouting and surveillance robot made by Mesa Robotics, sold in versions such as Urban Warrior, Block II and Scout.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMesa Robotics
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Options include a manipulator arm, weapon mount, sensor mount and fiber optic reel
    • The basic system includes the platform, control unit and battery charger
    • Name stands for Mesa Associates' Tactical Integrated Light-Force Deployment Assembly
