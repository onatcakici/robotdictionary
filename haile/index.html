    Haile

    n., robot. Georgia Institute of Technology, 2006. Research platform.

    Robotic percussionist from the Georgia Institute of Technology that listens to live music through a microphone and plays its own rhythm on a real drum.

    Measures and facts
    MakerGeorgia Institute of Technology
    First shown2006
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Designed in 2006 by Georgia Tech professor Gil Weinberg with student Scott Driscoll
    • Billed as the first robot to make acoustic percussion rather than play through speakers
    • Switches between leading and following roles based on what it hears
