    Wheelbarrow

    n., wheeled machine. United Kingdom, 1972.

    Remote controlled bomb disposal robot created in 1972 for British Army teams and used in Iraq, Northern Ireland and mainland Britain.

    Measures and facts
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    First shown1972

    Of note

    • Designed in 1972 for British Army bomb disposal teams
    • Deployed in Northern Ireland, mainland Britain and Iraq
    • More than 400 have been destroyed during operations
