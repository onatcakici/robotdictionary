    Humanoids

    TEO

    Also known as RH-2

    n., humanoid. Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, RoboticsLab, Spain, 2012. Research platform.

    Full size Spanish research humanoid used to study household tasks such as ironing, folding laundry and opening doors.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversidad Carlos III de Madrid, RoboticsLab
    CountrySpain
    First shown2012
    StatusResearch platform
    Degrees of freedom24
    ActuationElectric motors with harmonic drive gears
    SensorsMonocular camera, RGB-D sensor, six axis force torque sensors at wrists and ankles
    AutonomyDistributed control across three onboard computers on the YARP middleware

    TEO has 24 degrees of freedom, with six degree of freedom arms ending in three finger grippers and six degree of freedom legs, and runs distributed control across three onboard computers linked by the YARP middleware. Its sensing includes a monocular camera, an RGB-D sensor and six axis force torque sensors at the wrists and ankles for compliant contact with objects and doors. The RoboticsLab at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid built it in 2012 as successor to its RH-1 humanoid, published the software openly and has also tested it with a fuel cell power system.

    Of note

    • Demonstrated ironing and laundry folding as household task research
    • Successor to the RH-1 humanoid at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid
