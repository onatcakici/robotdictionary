    Philip K. Dick Android

    n., android. Hanson Robotics, Hong Kong, 2005.

    Humanlike android modeled on the science fiction author Philip K. Dick, built by Hanson Robotics in 2005 and able to converse using a model trained on his writings.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHanson Robotics
    CountryHong Kong
    First shown2005

    Of note

    • First shown publicly at Wired NextFest in 2005
