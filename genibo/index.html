    Quadrupeds

    Genibo

    n., quadruped. Dasarobot, South Korea, 2006. Discontinued.

    Korean entertainment robot dog styled as a bull terrier, sold as a home pet with voice command control.

    Measures and facts
    MakerDasarobot
    CountrySouth Korea
    First shown2006
    StatusDiscontinued
    ActuationElectric servo actuators
    PowerRechargeable battery with charging station
    SensorsColour camera, touch sensors on head, back and flanks, microphones, tilt sensor
    AutonomyOnboard personality and voice command software

    Genibo walked on four servo driven legs and carried a camera, touch sensors and microphones in a glossy plastic body shaped after a bull terrier. Onboard software gave it a personality that changed with handling, and it recognised a set of spoken commands and could be taught simple tricks. Dasarobot showed a prototype in 2006 and sold the robot from 2008 at around fifteen hundred US dollars, with later versions adding English language software.

    Of note

    • One of very few consumer walking robot pets to reach market after Sony ended AIBO
    • Sold internationally from 2008 at about 1,500 US dollars
