    Humanoids

    WABOT-2

    n., humanoid. Waseda University, Kato Laboratory, Japan, 1984. Retired.

    Keyboard playing humanoid from Waseda University that read printed sheet music with a camera eye and performed on an electronic organ.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWaseda University, Kato Laboratory
    CountryJapan
    First shown1984
    StatusRetired
    ActuationAbout 50 joints driven by electric motors
    SensorsCamera eye for reading printed scores, microphone
    AutonomyDistributed control across roughly 80 microprocessors

    WABOT-2 was built as a specialist musician rather than a general purpose machine, with ten fingers, two feet and about fifty joints. A camera in its head read ordinary sheet music, and a distributed network of roughly eighty microprocessors coordinated fingers, feet and speech so it could play organ pieces of moderate difficulty and accompany a singer. It performed in public at Expo '85 in Tsukuba.

    Of note

    • Read printed scores and played an electronic organ
    • Performed at Expo '85 in Tsukuba
