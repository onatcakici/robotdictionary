    Rovers

    MAGPIE

    n., rover. ispace EUROPE S.A., Luxembourg. In development.

    Lunar rover being developed by ispace for the European Space Agency to look for water ice near the Moon's south pole.

    Measures and facts
    Makerispace EUROPE S.A.
    CountryLuxembourg
    StatusIn development

    Of note

    • Planned as the European Space Agency's first lunar rover mission
    • Due to launch in 2029 aboard ispace Mission 4
