    Medical and surgical

    Ability Hand

    n., medical robot. PSYONIC, United States, 2021. In production.

    Fast, impact resistant bionic hand that sends touch feedback from its fingertips back to the wearer.

    Measures and facts
    MakerPSYONIC
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2021
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Described by its maker as the first commercially available bionic hand with multi touch feedback
    • Grew out of research at the University of Illinois

    Sources
