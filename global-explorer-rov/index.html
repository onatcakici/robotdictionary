    Underwater and marine

    Global Explorer ROV

    n., underwater robot. Deep Sea Systems International, United States.

    Deep water remotely operated vehicle made by Chris Nicholson of Deep Sea Systems International and used on research and survey dives deeper than 2,700 m.

    Measures and facts
    MakerDeep Sea Systems International
    CountryUnited States

    Of note

    • Has made many dives below 9,000 feet (2,700 m)
    • Used on expeditions for National Geographic and NOAA
    • Its builder, Deep Sea Systems International, is based in Falmouth, Massachusetts
