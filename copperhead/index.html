    Underwater and marine

    Copperhead

    n., underwater robot. Anduril.

    Family of autonomous underwater vehicles from Anduril, offered in two size classes, each with a utility version and a single use munition version.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAnduril

    Of note

    • Copperhead-100M is similar in size and capability to the Mark 54 lightweight torpedo
    • Copperhead-500M is comparable to the Mark 48 heavyweight torpedo
    • Both are much lighter and cheaper to build than those torpedoes
