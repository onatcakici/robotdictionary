    OhmniCare

    n., wheeled machine. OhmniLabs, United States. In production.

    Telepresence robot for clinics and care homes with a tall touchscreen and four cameras, driven by a remote clinician from a browser.

    Measures and facts
    MakerOhmniLabs
    CountryUnited States
    StatusIn production
    Height1.51 m
    Top speed0.89 m/s (3.2 km/h)
    Runtime8 h
    ActuationFour wheel base with 30 W brushless motors and six inch wheels
    PowerRechargeable battery with up to 18 hours of standby
    SensorsFour cameras including 4K front, rear and downward views
    AutonomyRemote driving with assisted docking

    OhmniCare is a four wheel telepresence robot carrying a 21.5 inch touchscreen at about eye level for a seated patient. Front, rear and downward cameras give the remote driver a full view of the room and floor, and six inch wheels handle carpet edges and thresholds up to about 13 mm. It is used for remote consultations, family visits in care homes and specialist rounds. It replaced the original Ohmni telepresence robot, which OhmniLabs discontinued in 2024.

    Of note

    • Replaced the original Ohmni telepresence robot, discontinued in 2024
    • Carries a 21.5 inch high definition touchscreen
