    Wheeled machines

    Phantom

    n., wheeled machine. Spetstechnoexport, Ukraine, 2016.

    Ukrainian military unmanned ground vehicle from Spetstechnoexport, intended for scouting, fire support, carrying ammunition and evacuating wounded soldiers.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSpetstechnoexport
    CountryUkraine
    First shown2016

    Of note

    • Designed in 2016 for Ukroboronprom
    • Can also be used for repair work
