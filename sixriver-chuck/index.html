    Wheeled machines

    Chuck

    n., wheeled machine. 6 River Systems, United States, 2016. In production.

    Cart shaped collaborative robot that leads warehouse pickers along a batched pick path and carries up to about 91 kg of totes.

    Measures and facts
    Maker6 River Systems
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2016
    StatusIn production
    Payload91 kg
    ActuationDifferential drive cart with modular multi level tote shelving
    PowerRechargeable battery with opportunity charging
    SensorsLidar, cameras and contact bumpers
    AutonomyMap based navigation with batch optimised pick paths and worker prompts

    Chuck is a wheeled picking cart that drives itself to each location and cues the associate with lights, a display and voice prompts. Modular shelving gives up to six levels of tote space, and navigation uses lidar and cameras against a site map without floor infrastructure. 6 River Systems was founded in 2015 by former Kiva Systems executives, and Chuck fleets are used in retail, apparel and third party logistics warehouses.

    Of note

    • Founded in 2015 by former Kiva Systems executives
    • Shopify acquired 6 River Systems in 2019 and Ocado took over the business in 2023
    • Configurations offer up to six shelf levels of workspace
