    Arms and workcells

    Blue Jay

    Also known as Amazon Blue Jay

    n., robot arm. Amazon Robotics, United States, 2025. Discontinued.

    Amazon workcell in which several robot arms work over one station at once, picking, stowing and consolidating items.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAmazon Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2025
    StatusDiscontinued

    Of note

    • Unveiled in October 2025
    • Reported to have been shelved about six months after launch
