    Quadrupeds

    Mini Pupper 2

    n., quadruped. MangDang, Hong Kong, 2022. In production.

    Small open source quadruped kit built around a Raspberry Pi and ROS 2, aimed at students learning navigation and mapping.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMangDang
    CountryHong Kong
    First shown2022
    StatusIn production
    Degrees of freedom12
    ActuationServo actuators, three per leg
    PowerOnboard lithium battery
    SensorsCamera, optional two dimensional lidar, inertial measurement unit
    AutonomyROS 2 stack with mapping and navigation on a Raspberry Pi

    Mini Pupper 2 descends from the Stanford Pupper design and ships as a kit with twelve servo driven joints, a Raspberry Pi compute module, a small display face and an optional lidar. Its software runs ROS 2 with simultaneous localisation and mapping and navigation examples, plus interfaces for vision and voice, so it can carry a full robotics course on cheap hardware. MangDang funded it on Kickstarter in late 2022 and sells both assembled and self build versions.

    Of note

    • Crowdfunded in late 2022 and supplied as an open source ROS 2 teaching kit
    • Derived from the openly published Stanford Pupper design
