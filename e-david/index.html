    Arms and workcells

    e-David

    n., workcell. University of Konstanz, Germany, 2009. Research platform.

    Painting robot developed at the University of Konstanz that works out brushstrokes from a source image and paints it onto canvas.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of Konstanz
    CountryGermany
    First shown2009
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Project began in 2009 with an adapted one armed welding robot
    • Swaps between brushes and pens and measures its distance to the canvas with a sensor
    • Paints using predefined stroke candidates or dynamically generated strokes
