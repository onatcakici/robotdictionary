    CubeStormer 3

    n., robot. 2014.

    Rubik's Cube solving robot made from Lego Mindstorms and a Samsung Galaxy S4 that set a world record of 3.253 seconds in March 2014.

    Measures and facts
    First shown2014

    Of note

    • Solved a Rubik's Cube in 3.253 seconds on 15 March 2014 in Birmingham
    • Beat the previous record of 5.270 seconds, held by CubeStormer II
    • Built over 18 months by David Gilday and Mike Dobson
