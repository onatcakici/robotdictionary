    Underwater and marine

    CURV-21

    n., underwater robot. United States. In service.

    Remotely operated underwater vehicle of the United States Navy, built for deep ocean salvage work at depths of up to 20,000 feet (6,100 m).

    Measures and facts
    CountryUnited States
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • Maximum working depth: 20,000 feet (6,100 m)
