    Underwater and marine

    Dive-LD

    n., underwater robot. Anduril Industries, United States.

    Large diameter autonomous underwater vehicle made by Anduril Industries for defence and commercial work at sea, able to reach 6,000 metres on missions lasting up to 10 days.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAnduril Industries
    CountryUnited States

    Of note

    • Weighs about three tons
    • Operates at depths of up to 6,000 metres
    • Modular design lets it be quickly fitted with different payloads
