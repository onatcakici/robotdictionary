    Wheeled machines

    Knightscope K5

    n., wheeled machine. Knightscope, United States, 2013. In production.

    Outdoor autonomous security patrol robot that films, listens and reads licence plates while covering parking lots and campuses at pedestrian speed.

    Measures and facts
    MakerKnightscope
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2013
    StatusIn production
    Height1.59 m
    Mass180.5 kg
    Top speed1.33 m/s (4.8 km/h)
    ActuationElectric wheeled drive inside a sealed weatherproof shell
    PowerBattery electric with autonomous return to a charging pad
    Sensors360 degree high definition cameras, thermal imaging, lidar, automatic licence plate recognition and two way audio
    AutonomyAutonomous patrol of mapped outdoor routes with alerts sent to human operators

    The K5 is a tall bullet shaped wheeled robot that patrols outdoor ground at walking pace, streaming 360 degree video, thermal imagery and audio to a security operations centre. It reads licence plates, detects people and devices, and can broadcast a spoken warning or trigger strobes. Navigation is autonomous along mapped patrol routes, and the machine returns to a charging pad by itself. Knightscope leases the robots to car parks, corporate campuses, hospitals and public agencies in the United States.

    Knightscope's K5 became an accidental celebrity through misadventure: bullied by drunks, drowned in a fountain, and immortalised in memes, all while quietly logging patrol miles for malls and hospitals across America.

    Of note

    • Patrols parking structures, corporate campuses and public spaces across the United States
    • The New York Police Department trialled a Knightscope robot on patrol duty
    • Knightscope also sells the four wheeled K7 for larger outdoor perimeters
