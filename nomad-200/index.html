    Wheeled machines

    Nomad 200

    n., wheeled machine. Nomadic Technologies. Discontinued.

    Commercial mobile robot made by Nomadic Technologies in the 1990s, with a choice of sensors, chiefly for robotics lab courses and research and development.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNomadic Technologies
    StatusDiscontinued

    Of note

    • Also known as the N200
    • Manufactured in the 1990s by Nomadic Technologies, Inc.
