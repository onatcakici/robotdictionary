    Kota the Triceratops

    n., robot. Hasbro and Playskool. Discontinued.

    Animatronic toy dinosaur in the form of a Triceratops that Hasbro and Playskool produced for children from 3 to 6 years old.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHasbro and Playskool
    StatusDiscontinued

    Of note

    • Recommended age range of 3 to 6 years
