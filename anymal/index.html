    Quadrupeds

    ANYmal

    n., quadruped. ANYbotics, Switzerland, 2016. In production.

    The industrial inspection quadruped patrolling offshore platforms and plants, offered in explosion proof form.

    Measures and facts
    MakerANYbotics
    CountrySwitzerland
    First shown2016
    StatusIn production
    Massapprox. 50 kg
    Enduranceapprox. 90 to 120 min

    ANYmal grew from ETH Zürich's legged robotics lab into the standard inspection quadruped of European heavy industry, reading gauges and sniffing for gas on offshore platforms. An explosion proof variant is certified for petrochemical sites.
