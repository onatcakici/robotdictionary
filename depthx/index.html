    Underwater and marine

    DEPTHX

    n., underwater robot. Stone Aerospace, United States. Research platform.

    Autonomous underwater vehicle that Stone Aerospace of Austin, Texas, built to map and sample flooded sinkholes in northern Mexico, as a NASA funded step toward exploring Europa.

    Measures and facts
    MakerStone Aerospace
    CountryUnited States
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • First of three NASA funded vehicles built by Stone Aerospace
    • Built to gather water samples and cores from sinkhole walls
    • Name stands for Deep Phreatic Thermal Explorer
