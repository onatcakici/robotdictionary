    Quadrupeds

    Unitree Go2

    n., quadruped. Unitree Robotics, China, 2023. In production.

    Fifteen kilogram consumer and developer quadruped with a hemispherical lidar, sold from well under two thousand US dollars.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUnitree Robotics
    CountryChina
    First shown2023
    StatusIn production
    Height0.4 m
    Length0.7 m
    Mass15 kg
    Top speed3.7 m/s (13.3 km/h)
    Degrees of freedom12
    ActuationIn house brushless joint motors, three per leg
    Power8,000 mAh swappable lithium battery
    SensorsHemispherical lidar with full 360 degree horizontal coverage, wide angle camera, foot force sensors on education models
    AutonomyOnboard obstacle avoidance and person following, developer interface on education versions
    Paceapprox. 3.7 m/s
    Pricefrom approx. $1,600

    Go2 replaced the Go1 with a stiffer body, higher torque joints and a lidar giving full 360 degree coverage around the robot. Consumer versions walk, follow a person and stream video to a phone app, while education versions expose joint level control for research and take extra onboard computing. Trot speed runs from 2.5 metres per second on the base model up to 3.7 on the education version, and its low price made it the most widely sold quadruped of its generation.

    The Go2 put a lidar equipped walking robot under the price of a used scooter and sold in volumes no legged machine had seen. It is the default embodiment for quadruped research code worldwide.

    Of note

    • Launched in 2023 from about 1,600 US dollars, among the cheapest dynamic quadrupeds sold
    • Offered in consumer, professional and education variants of the same platform
