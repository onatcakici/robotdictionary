    Humanoids

    Erica

    n., android. Ishiguro Laboratories, Japan, 2015. Research platform.

    A conversational android of studied realism; once hired as a television news anchor.

    Measures and facts
    MakerIshiguro Laboratories
    CountryJapan
    First shown2015
    StatusResearch platform

    Erica was designed as the most humanlike conversational android of her generation and was announced as a Japanese television news anchor in 2018; she has since been cast in a Hollywood film project.

    1. Ishiguro Laboratories website, geminoid.jp
