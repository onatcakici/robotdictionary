    Discovery

    n., underwater robot. Specialist Machine Development, United Kingdom.

    Remotely operated underwater vehicle built in Britain by Specialist Machine Development for use in China; little has been published about it.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSpecialist Machine Development
    CountryUnited Kingdom
