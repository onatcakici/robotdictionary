    Walking Truck

    Also known as GE Quadruped, Cybernetic Walking Machine

    n., legged robot. General Electric, United States, 1965. Retired.

    Hydraulic four legged vehicle from General Electric that an operator drove by moving his own arms and legs inside a force feedback harness.

    Built for the US Army by Ralph Mosher at General Electric, the Walking Truck was a rideable quadruped meant to carry loads over ground that wheels could not cross. Each leg had three hydraulically powered joints, and the driver commanded them through a master slave linkage that fed contact forces back to his own hands and feet. Operators could walk the machine, step over obstacles and lift heavy objects with a leg, but the control scheme was so tiring that the project ended after trials.

    Of note

    • Built under the US Army Cybernetic Anthropomorphous Machine programme
    • Could lift loads of several hundred kilograms with a single leg
    • Preserved at the US Army Transportation Museum at Fort Eustis
