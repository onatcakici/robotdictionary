    Samsung SGR-A1

    n., robot. Samsung Techwin and Korea University, South Korea.

    Autonomous sentry gun developed by Samsung Techwin and Korea University to help South Korean soldiers guard the Korean Demilitarized Zone.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSamsung Techwin and Korea University
    CountrySouth Korea

    Of note

    • Often cited as the first such unit combining surveillance, tracking, firing and voice recognition
    • Reportedly deployed, though numbers are unknown because the project is classified
