    CIMON

    Also known as Crew Interactive Mobile companion, Pilot Study with the Crew Interactive MObile companioN (Cimon), Mobile Companion

    n., robot. Airbus Commercial Aircraft, Germany. In service.

    Head shaped robot with artificial intelligence, built by Airbus in Germany as a crew companion aboard the International Space Station.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAirbus Commercial Aircraft
    CountryGermany
    StatusIn service
    Mass5 kg
    Width32 cm

    Of note

    • Name stands for Crew Interactive Mobile companion
    • Weighs 5 kg and measures 32 cm across
