    Wheeled machines

    Marvin

    Also known as Mobile Autonomous Robot Vehicle for Indoor Navigation

    n., wheeled machine. University of Kaiserslautern, Germany. Research platform.

    Indoor mobile research robot from the Robotics Lab at the University of Kaiserslautern, using laser scanners, ultrasonic sensors and a camera to navigate and map rooms.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of Kaiserslautern
    CountryGermany
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Name stands for Mobile Autonomous Robot Vehicle for Indoor Navigation
    • Uses a differential drive and a behaviour based control system
    • Has a stereo microphone system to locate sound sources
