    Underwater and marine

    Goldfish class ROUV

    n., underwater robot. Shenyang Institute of Automation, China. In service.

    Family of light Chinese remotely operated underwater vehicles from the Shenyang Institute of Automation, operated by the People's Liberation Army Navy and by civilian agencies.

    Measures and facts
    MakerShenyang Institute of Automation
    CountryChina
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • Developed by the Shenyang Institute of Automation of the Chinese Academy of Sciences
    • In service with the Chinese navy and civilian government agencies
