    Quadrupeds

    RaiBo

    n., quadruped. KAIST, South Korea, 2022. Research platform.

    Learning controlled quadruped from KAIST able to run across a sandy beach, terrain that defeats most legged robots.

    Measures and facts
    MakerKAIST
    CountrySouth Korea
    First shown2022
    StatusResearch platform
    Top speed3 m/s (10.8 km/h)
    Degrees of freedom12
    ActuationElectric joint actuators with torque control
    PowerOnboard battery
    SensorsInertial measurement unit and joint sensing only
    AutonomyReinforcement learning policy trained in simulation with deformable terrain models

    RaiBo was built in Jemin Hwangbo's laboratory to test controllers trained entirely in simulation, including a physics model of how feet sink into granular ground. A recurrent neural network policy infers ground properties from proprioception as the robot moves, so no terrain sensing or retuning is needed between surfaces. It ran at just over three metres per second on a beach and crossed grass, running tracks and soft mats without modification, work published in Science Robotics in 2023.

    Of note

    • Ran at 3.03 m/s on a sandy beach with no change to its controller
    • Its terrain adaptive policy was trained purely in simulation and transferred without tuning
