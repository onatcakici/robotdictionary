    Humanoids

    Autonomous Shipboard Humanoid

    n., humanoid. United States Naval Research Laboratory, United States. Research platform.

    Humanoid robot developed at the United States Naval Research Laboratory with Virginia Tech and the University of Pennsylvania to move around ships, climb ladders and fight fires.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUnited States Naval Research Laboratory
    CountryUnited States
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Meant to respond to emergencies and follow visual commands from people
    • Already able to walk, among other abilities
