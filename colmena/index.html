    Rovers

    Colmena

    n., rover. Universidad Nacional Autonoma de Mexico, Mexico, 2024. Lost.

    Swarm of five palm sized Mexican micro robots intended to self organise on the lunar regolith, lost with the Peregrine lander.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversidad Nacional Autonoma de Mexico
    CountryMexico
    First shown2024
    StatusLost
    ActuationSmall wheeled micro robots designed to work as a group
    PowerSolar cells on each robot
    SensorsRegolith and plasma sensors with position tracking
    AutonomyCooperative swarm behaviour rather than individual teleoperation
    Mass608 g

    Colmena was developed by the space instrumentation laboratory at UNAM as Mexico's first lunar mission, with five wheeled robots about twelve centimetres across. Released from a rail on the Astrobotic Peregrine lander, they were to drive together, link into a shared electrical network and measure regolith and plasma conditions. The mission ended when Peregrine suffered a propellant leak in January 2024 and re entered the atmosphere.

    Of note

    • Mexico's first lunar payload
    • Five robots designed to operate as a cooperative swarm
