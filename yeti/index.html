    Rovers

    Yeti

    n., rover. Dartmouth College, United States. Research platform.

    Four wheeled polar survey robot that tows radar over Antarctic and Greenland ice to find crevasses and buried structures.

    Measures and facts
    MakerDartmouth College
    CountryUnited States
    StatusResearch platform
    ActuationFour wheel drive on a lightweight snow chassis
    PowerRechargeable battery pack
    SensorsGround penetrating radar with satellite navigation for survey grids
    AutonomyAutonomous waypoint following along programmed survey lines

    Yeti was built at Dartmouth's Thayer School of Engineering as a small battery powered vehicle, light enough to cross snow bridges that would swallow a tractor. Driving programmed grid patterns under satellite navigation, it carries ground penetrating radar to detect crevasses along traverse routes and to map buried infrastructure, including surveys at the South Pole and on the Greenland ice sheet. Because it is unmanned and expendable, it can be sent ahead of human convoys into hazardous terrain.

    Of note

    • Surveys crevasse fields ahead of crewed polar traverses
    • Used to map buried structures at the South Pole station
