    Wheeled machines

    Uran-9

    n., wheeled machine. Rostec, Russia. In production.

    Russian tracked unmanned combat vehicle from JSC 766 UPTK, offered abroad through Rosoboronexport, that scouts and gives fire support from a distance.

    Measures and facts
    MakerRostec
    CountryRussia
    StatusIn production
    Mass12 tonne

    Of note

    • Weighs 12 tonnes
    • Intended to support combat, reconnaissance and counterterrorism units
