Robot Dictionary. Wheeled machines

CLOi ServeBot

n., wheeled machine.

LG, South Korea, 2020. LG's shelf-carrying restaurant and building courier line. In production.

CLOi ServeBot was introduced by LG of South Korea and first filed in this dictionary under the year 2020. It belongs to the wheeled class, machines that trade legs for range and endurance. It is offered commercially and remains in production at the time of this volume.

See also Q9, CLOi GuideBot.
