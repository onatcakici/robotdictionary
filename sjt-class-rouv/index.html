    Underwater and marine

    SJT class ROUV

    n., underwater robot. Shenyang Institute of Automation and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China.

    Chinese family of remotely operated underwater vehicles created as a joint project of the Shenyang Institute of Automation and the underwater engineering institute of Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

    Measures and facts
    MakerShenyang Institute of Automation and Shanghai Jiao Tong University
    CountryChina

    Of note

    • General designer was Zhu Jimao, a Shanghai Jiao Tong University professor
    • Zhu Jimao also designed the earlier Type 7103 DSRV
    • Experience from the series fed into many later Chinese ROUVs
