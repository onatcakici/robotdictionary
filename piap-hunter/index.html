    PIAP HUNTeR

    Also known as HUNTeR, PIAP Hunter

    n., wheeled machine. Industrial Research Institute for Automation and Measurements, Poland, 2017.

    Heavy hybrid unmanned ground vehicle developed in Poland by the Industrial Research Institute for Automation and Measurements, weighing 4000 kg and 4.7 m long.
