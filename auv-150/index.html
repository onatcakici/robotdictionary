    Underwater and marine

    AUV-150

    n., underwater robot. Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute, India. In development.

    Indian autonomous underwater vehicle under development by scientists at the Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute in Durgapur, West Bengal, with support from IIT Kharagpur.

    Measures and facts
    MakerCentral Mechanical Engineering Research Institute
    CountryIndia
    StatusIn development
