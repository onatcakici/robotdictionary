    Gatik autonomous box truck

    n., wheeled machine. Gatik, United States, 2019. In service.

    Retrofitted box truck that drives fixed middle mile freight routes between warehouses and stores, running driverless on some routes in the United States.

    Measures and facts
    MakerGatik
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2019
    StatusIn service
    ActuationAutonomy kit retrofitted to medium duty commercial box trucks
    PowerConventional truck powertrain
    SensorsLidar, radar and cameras
    AutonomyLevel 4 autonomy constrained to fixed, pre validated middle mile routes with remote monitoring

    Gatik takes production class box trucks and fits them with a retrofit autonomy kit of lidar, radar, cameras and compute. The trucks run short repeatable middle mile routes between distribution centres, dark stores and retail outlets, which lets the company validate a fixed operating domain closely. Some routes run fully driverless with no person in the cab, while others keep a safety driver. Customers include grocery and consumer goods shippers in the United States and Canada.

    Of note

    • Made fully driverless commercial box truck deliveries for Walmart in Bentonville, Arkansas, from 2021
    • Runs middle mile freight for Sam's Club and hauls for Georgia-Pacific through KBX
    • Reported hundreds of additional trucks planned for United States roads in 2026
