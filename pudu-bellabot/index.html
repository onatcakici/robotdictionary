    Wheeled machines

    BellaBot

    n., wheeled machine. Pudu Robotics, China, 2019. In production.

    Four tray delivery robot with a cat like animated face, used in restaurants and hotels to carry dishes to tables and clear them away.

    Measures and facts
    MakerPudu Robotics
    CountryChina
    First shown2019
    StatusIn production
    Height1.29 m
    Mass55 kg
    Payload40 kg
    Top speed1.2 m/s (4.3 km/h)
    Runtime13 h
    PowerRechargeable battery with a charging dock
    SensorsLidar, three depth cameras and tray load sensors
    AutonomyLaser or visual SLAM with automatic route planning and obstacle avoidance
    Width53.44 cm

    BellaBot is a chest high wheeled service robot with four removable trays, each rated for 10 kg, and a display face that reacts when guests take a plate. It localises with laser SLAM or visual SLAM, so it can work in rooms with high ceilings where markers are impractical, and three depth cameras watch for obstacles at different heights. Pudu Robotics of Shenzhen sells it worldwide for casual dining, hotpot restaurants, hotels and hospitals.

    Pudu's cat faced BellaBot purrs when petted and has served in tens of thousands of restaurants, becoming the face of China's service robot export wave.

    Of note

    • Carries up to 10 kg on each of four trays
    • Pudu Robotics was founded in Shenzhen in 2016
    • Offers laser positioning with no ceiling height restriction
