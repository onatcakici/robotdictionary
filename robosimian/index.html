    RoboSimian

    n., legged robot. NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, United States, 2013. Retired.

    Four limbed ape like robot from NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory built for the DARPA Robotics Challenge, where it finished fifth in the 2015 finals.
