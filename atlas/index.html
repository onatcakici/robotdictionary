Atlas

Also known as Electric Atlas

Boston Dynamics' full-size humanoid, rebuilt in 2024 as an all-electric machine and now being productised for Hyundai factory work.

The original Atlas was hydraulically actuated and served from 2013 as a testbed for dynamic balance, parkour and box handling; Boston Dynamics retired it in April 2024. Its all-electric replacement uses continuous-rotation joints that give it a range of motion no human has, and the version shown at CES 2026 stands about 1.9 metres, carries 56 degrees of freedom, reaches 2.3 metres, lifts 110 pounds, holds an IP67 rating and swaps its own battery after roughly four hours. Control combines Boston Dynamics' motion stack with Google DeepMind Gemini Robotics models learned from teleoperated demonstrations. Neither version has been sold, and Hyundai plans to put it to work at its Georgia vehicle plant.

Of note

  • Hydraulic Atlas retired in April 2024 after eleven years of public demonstrations
  • Electric Atlas made its first live public appearance at CES 2026
  • Slated for factory work at a Hyundai plant from 2028
