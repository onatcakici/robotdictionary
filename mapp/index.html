MAPP

Also known as Mobile Autonomous Prospecting Platform

Commercial prospecting rover carrying a cellular network demonstration to the Moon, stranded aboard the IM-2 lander after it tipped over.

MakerLunar Outpost
CountryUnited States
Launched2025
StatusLost
Payload15 kg
ActuationFour wheel drive platform with payload deck
PowerSolar panels with battery
SensorsNavigation cameras with customer payload interfaces
AutonomySemi autonomous driving under ground supervision

Lunar Outpost built MAPP as a small four wheeled platform to carry customer payloads across the lunar surface, including a Nokia Bell Labs cellular network node, MIT's AstroAnt robot and cameras for a regolith sampling demonstration. It rode Intuitive Machines' Athena lander to a landing near the lunar south pole on 6 March 2025. The lander came to rest on its side with too little power, so MAPP was never released.

Of note

  • Carried the first cellular network node sent to the Moon
  • Never deployed because the IM-2 lander toppled
