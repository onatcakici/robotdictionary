Double 3

Self balancing telepresence robot with a raised screen that lets a remote user drive through an office and talk with people face to face.

MakerDouble Robotics
CountryUnited States
StatusIn production
Runtime4 h
ActuationTwo wheel self balancing drive with remotely adjustable height from about 1.19 to 1.52 m
PowerLithium battery with about two hours to recharge
SensorsTwo stereo depth cameras, five ultrasonic rangefinders, wheel encoders and an inertial unit
AutonomyAssisted driving with click to drive path planning and obstacle avoidance

Double 3 balances on two wheels with retractable kickstands and can raise or lower its screen between about 1.19 and 1.52 m from the browser interface. Two wide angle cameras, stereo depth sensors and five ultrasonic rangefinders support click to drive control, where the remote user picks a spot and the robot handles the path and obstacles. Onboard computing runs the vision work so the remote side only needs a browser. It is used by remote workers, distributed teams and students who cannot attend in person.

Of note

  • The first Double shipped in 2013 using an iPad as its head and screen
  • Backwards compatible with the Double 2 robot base
  • Carries two 13 megapixel cameras and six beamforming microphones

Maker: www.doublerobotics.com
