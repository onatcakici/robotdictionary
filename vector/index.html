    Wheeled machines

    Vector

    n., wheeled machine. Anki (Digital Dream Labs), United States, 2018. In production.

    A palm sized desk companion with big eyes and a cloud brain, revived by its fans.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAnki (Digital Dream Labs)
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2018
    StatusIn production

    Anki's Vector packed personality into a palm size body just before its maker collapsed; fans and Digital Dream Labs revived its cloud brain, a rare robot resurrection by community demand.
