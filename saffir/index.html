    Humanoids

    SAFFiR

    Also known as Shipboard Autonomous Firefighting Robot

    n., humanoid. Virginia Tech with the US Naval Research Laboratory, United States, 2014. Prototype.

    Bipedal firefighting robot developed for the US Navy that walked a decommissioned ship's compartments and aimed a hose at a live fire.

    Measures and facts
    MakerVirginia Tech with the US Naval Research Laboratory
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2014
    StatusPrototype
    Height1.78 m
    Mass65 kg
    ActuationLinear series elastic actuators with titanium springs
    SensorsInfrared stereo vision, rotating lidar, six axis force and torque sensors in the ankles
    AutonomySemi autonomous task execution with a human supervisor in the loop

    SAFFiR is 1.78 m tall and weighs about 65 kg, with legs driven by linear series elastic actuators and whole body momentum control so it can stay upright on a moving deck and step over door sills. It navigates smoke using infrared stereo vision and a rotating laser scanner, with a human supervisor able to take over any task. In November 2014 the Office of Naval Research tested it aboard the ex-USS Shadwell, where it walked uneven flooring, located overheated equipment by thermal imaging and suppressed a fire with a hose.

    Of note

    • Fought a live fire aboard the ex-USS Shadwell in November 2014
    • Funded by the Office of Naval Research with the Naval Research Laboratory
