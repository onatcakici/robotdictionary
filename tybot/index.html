    Wheeled machines

    TyBot

    n., wheeled machine. Advanced Construction Robotics, United States. In service.

    Self propelled gantry robot that rolls along a bridge deck rebar mat and ties intersections automatically without needing a building information model.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAdvanced Construction Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    StatusIn service
    ActuationSelf propelled wheeled gantry spanning the deck with a robotic rebar tying head
    SensorsMachine vision that locates rebar intersections without a pre loaded digital model
    AutonomyAutonomously locates and ties intersections across the mat with one operator supervising

    TyBot is a wheeled gantry that spans a bridge deck and rolls along rails or the deck edges above a laid out mat of reinforcing bar. Machine vision finds each bar intersection on the fly, so it needs no digital model of the deck, and a tying head then wraps and twists a tie at each crossing. One operator sets it up and supervises while the robot works the mat, removing a physically punishing manual task. It is used on highway bridge deck construction.

    Of note

    • Used on highway bridge deck projects across the United States
    • Removes a repetitive stooped labour task associated with worker injury
    • The same company builds IronBot, which lifts and places reinforcing bar
