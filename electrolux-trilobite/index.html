    Wheeled machines

    Trilobite

    n., wheeled machine. Electrolux, Sweden, 2001. Discontinued.

    The first mass market robot vacuum cleaner, a disc shaped machine that sensed rooms with ultrasound and returned to its dock to recharge.

    Measures and facts
    MakerElectrolux
    CountrySweden
    First shown2001
    StatusDiscontinued
    Height0.13 m
    Length0.35 m
    Runtime1 h
    ActuationTwo drive wheels with casters and a removable roller brush
    PowerRechargeable battery with a docking station
    SensorsUltrasonic transducers, with infrared cliff sensors on the version 2.0
    AutonomyUltrasonic ranging with wall following and automatic return to the charging dock

    The Trilobite used ultrasonic transducers to range walls and furniture, following walls and covering open floor while avoiding contact within about an inch. It reached shops in 2001, ahead of the first Roomba, after a prototype was demonstrated on BBC television in 1996. A version 2.0 arrived in 2004 with added infrared sensors to detect stairs and ledges. Electrolux later dropped the product and it is no longer sold or supported.

    Of note

    • Demonstrated as a prototype on BBC Tomorrow's World in 1996
    • Reached the market in 2001, before the first Roomba
    • A version 2.0 followed in 2004 before the line was dropped
