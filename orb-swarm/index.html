    Orb Swarm

    n., robot. 2007.

    Kinetic art piece made up of six partly autonomous spherical robots, built in 2007 by engineers and artists to imitate natural swarms and human dance.

    Measures and facts
    First shown2007

    Of note

    • Consists of six spherical robots
    • Created in 2007 by a team including Michael Prados, Jon Foote and Lee Sonko
    • Nearly all of its hardware and software is open source
