    Sea Hunter

    Also known as USV Sea Hunter

    n., marine robot. Leidos and Christensen Shipyards, United States, 2016. Prototype.

    Experimental uncrewed trimaran of the US Navy, created by Leidos under DARPA's ACTUV antisubmarine programme and launched in 2016.

    Measures and facts
    MakerLeidos and Christensen Shipyards
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2016
    StatusPrototype
    Mass145 tonne
    Length40.2 m

    Of note

    • Christened in Portland, Oregon, on 7 April 2016
    • Christensen Shipyards handled construction as subcontractor
    • Measures 40.2 m long and weighs 145 tonnes
