    Wheeled machines

    LOVOT

    n., wheeled machine. GROOVE X, Japan, 2019. In production.

    A warm bodied companion robot engineered solely to be loved.

    Measures and facts
    MakerGROOVE X
    CountryJapan
    First shown2019
    StatusIn production

    GROOVE X spent a hundred million dollars making LOVOT do nothing useful on purpose: it is warm to the touch, begs to be held, and exists to be loved, and Japanese buyers treat it as family.
