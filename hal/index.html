    HAL

    Also known as Hybrid Assistive Limb, HAL 5, The great robot

    n., exoskeleton. University of Tsukuba and Cyberdyne Inc., Japan, 2004.

    Powered exoskeleton suit with a soft body, known in full as the Hybrid Assistive Limb, built by Japan's University of Tsukuba and Cyberdyne to boost its wearers' physical abilities.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of Tsukuba and Cyberdyne Inc.
    CountryJapan
    First shown2004

    Of note

    • HAL 3 supports leg function only
    • HAL 5 is a full body version covering arms, legs and torso
