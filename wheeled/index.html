    Root n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States. Coding robot that drives on whiteboards, drawing and erasing as it runs a program, developed at Harvard's Wyss Institute and later sold by iRobot. In production.

    Rovio n., wheeled machine. WowWee. Wireless networked robot made by WowWee that carries a webcam, letting its user watch the camera feed and drive it remotely over the internet.

    ROVVER X n., wheeled machine. Envirosight, United States. Six wheel drive tethered sewer inspection crawler with a pan and tilt camera, driven by a technician from a surface reel and controller. In production.

    Roxo n., wheeled machine. FedEx, United States, 2019. FedEx sidewalk delivery robot built on DEKA balancing wheel technology that could climb curbs and stairs, tested in several cities and cancelled in 2022. Retired.

    RP-VITA n., wheeled machine. InTouch Health, United States, 2012. Hospital telemedicine robot on an autonomous wheeled base, letting a specialist examine patients remotely without a nurse steering the machine. Discontinued.

    RVR n., wheeled machine. Sphero, United States, 2019. Programmable tracked robot from Sphero with a port for adding single board computers and sensors. In production.

    S

    S8 MaxV Ultra n., wheeled machine. Roborock, China, 2024. The flagship of the robot vacuum arms race, with a self emptying, self washing dock. In production.

    Saros Z70 n., wheeled machine. Roborock, China, 2025. Robot vacuum with a folding five axis arm that lifts small objects such as socks out of its path before it cleans. In production.

    SCITOS G5 n., wheeled machine. MetraLabs, Germany, 2007. A German service robot base that has guided museum visitors for years. In production.

    Scooba n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2005. Floor washing robot from iRobot that sprayed, scrubbed and squeegeed hard floors. Discontinued.

    Scooptram ST18 n., wheeled machine. Epiroc, Sweden. Articulated underground loader rated at eighteen tonnes that can be run remotely from surface or drive itself between loading and dumping points. In production.

    Scribbler n., wheeled machine. Parallax, Inc., United States. Small, inexpensive programmable educational robot from Parallax with several sensor systems, able to navigate on its own and respond to its surroundings.

    Scrubber 50 n., wheeled machine. Gausium, China, 2020. Commercial scrubbing robot the size of a walk behind machine that cleans roughly 500 to 1,300 square metres an hour without a driver. In production.

    Scythe M.52 n., wheeled machine. Scythe Robotics, United States, 2021. Commercial grade autonomous zero turn mower with a fifty two inch deck that mows areas an operator has driven around once. In production.

    Seekur n., wheeled machine. Adept MobileRobots, United States, 2008. An all weather omnidirectional research truck of the pre-AMR era. Retired.

    Serve Gen3 n., wheeled machine. Serve Robotics, United States, 2024. Third generation four wheeled sidewalk delivery robot that carries restaurant orders on United States city sidewalks under Level 4 autonomy with remote assistance. In service.

    Servi n., wheeled machine. Bear Robotics, United States, 2020. Tray carrying restaurant robot that brings food from the kitchen to tables and returns dirty dishes along a mapped route. In production.

    Servo7 n., wheeled machine. Servo7, Netherlands. Robot that unloads loose cartons from shipping containers, used by logistics firms including DHL and PostNL.

    Shakey n., wheeled machine. SRI International, United States, 1966. The first mobile robot able to reason about its own actions, a wheeled machine that pushed blocks around laboratory rooms under program control. Retired.

    SkyFood n., wheeled machine. TracFtech, Brazil. Autonomous delivery robot from the Brazilian robotics company TracFtech that doubles as a mobile digital advertising display.

    Skypod n., wheeled machine. Exotec, France, 2018. Warehouse robot that drives across the floor and then climbs the face of the racking to retrieve bins stored up to 14 metres high. In production.

    Slicer n., wheeled machine. Remote controlled combat robot that competed in the Dutch version of Robot Wars, the television series in which teams fight their machines in an arena.

    Solix n., wheeled machine. Solinftec, Brazil, 2022. Solar powered four wheeled scouting and spraying robot that inspects individual plants and applies herbicide only where weeds are found in row crops. In production.

    Somatic n., wheeled machine. SOMATIC, United States. Mobile robot that cleans restrooms in office buildings on its own.

    Sphero n., wheeled machine. Sphero, United States, 2011. A programmable rolling ball that carried coding lessons into a million classrooms. In production.

    Spykee n., wheeled machine. Meccano. Toy robot from Meccano carrying a webcam, microphone and speaker that its owner can drive around the home from a computer, whether nearby or remotely through the internet.

    Stanford Cart n., wheeled machine. Stanford University, United States, 1979. A camera cart that crossed a chair filled room in five hours, founding visual navigation. Retired.

    Starship delivery robot n., wheeled machine. Starship Technologies, Estonia and United States, 2015. Six wheeled sidewalk robot that carries groceries and meals a few kilometres from a hub to a customer, navigating autonomously with remote human oversight. In service.

    STR-1 n., wheeled machine. VNII Transmash, Soviet Union, 1986. Radio controlled clearing robot derived from Lunokhod chassis work, used on the reactor building roofs at Chernobyl in 1986. Retired.

    Stretch n., wheeled machine. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2021. A box moving mobile arm unloading trucks one carton per second at DHL. In production.

    SUMMIT-XL n., wheeled machine. Robotnik, Spain, 2012. A Spanish research AMR base common in European labs and EU projects. In production.

    SwarmBot n., wheeled machine. SwarmFarm Robotics, Australia. Lightweight autonomous farm platform that carries third party implements for spraying, mowing and seeding, with several machines working one paddock together. In production.

    SwiftBot n., wheeled machine. Pudu Robotics, China, 2022. A premium server robot that reads gestures and dodges crowds. In production.

    Synphony n., wheeled machine. Synphony, United States. Strawberry harvesting robot for California farms, paired with field analytics.

    T

    T-52 Enryu n., wheeled machine. Tmsuk, Japan. Rescue robot produced by the Japanese robotics company Tmsuk for use in rescue work.

    T7AMR n., wheeled machine. Tennant Company, United States, 2018. Robotic ride on floor scrubber for retail and industrial floors that staff can drive manually or leave to clean a taught route alone. In production.

    Tally n., wheeled machine. Simbe, United States, 2015. Slim retail robot that patrols store aisles photographing shelves to find out of stock items, misplaced products and wrong price labels. In production.

    Taobotics n., wheeled machine. Taobotics, China. Autonomous robots for retail stores from a company based in Shenzhen.

    Taurob Inspector n., wheeled machine. Taurob GmbH, Austria. Mobile robot certified under ATEX rules, made by the Austrian firm Taurob for offshore oil and gas sites and derived from its Taurob Tracker.

    Taurob Tracker n., wheeled machine. Taurob GmbH, Austria. Austrian remote controlled ground robot made by Taurob, first developed for fire brigade reconnaissance and now also used by armed forces, universities and oil and gas companies. In production.

    Teleo n., wheeled machine. Teleo, United States. Retrofit that turns dozers, trucks and loaders into supervised autonomous machines so one operator can run several from a control room; the company was acquired.

    temi n., wheeled machine. Temi, Israel, 2019. A telepresence and assistant robot that follows its owner from room to room. In production.

    Tennibot Rover n., wheeled machine. Tennibot. Autonomous robot from the company Tennibot that gathers balls by itself, removing the need for a person to collect them.

    Tertill n., wheeled machine. Franklin Robotics, United States, 2017. A solar garden weeding puck from a Roomba co inventor. In production.

    Thorvald n., wheeled machine. Saga Robotics, Norway. Modular battery electric field platform best known for carrying ultraviolet lamps that kill mildew on strawberries and grapes during night passes. In service.

    Thymio n., wheeled machine. Mobsya, Switzerland, 2011. A friendly white classroom robot teaching programming to children across Europe. In production.

    TIAGo n., wheeled machine. PAL Robotics, Spain, 2015. Wheeled robot with a lifting torso and one arm from PAL Robotics, widely used as a research platform for home and care tasks. In production.

    TiLR n., wheeled machine. RoboDynamics Corporation. Telepresence robot from RoboDynamics Corporation that stands in for a remote user at another location, reducing the need for business travel.

    TinyLineMarker Pro n., wheeled machine. TinyMobileRobots, Denmark. Battery electric marking robot that sprays sports field lines and construction layout marks to satellite coordinates loaded from a drawing. In production.

    Topo n., wheeled machine. Nolan Bushnell. Series of personal robots produced by Androbot in the 1980s for homes and schools, programmed wirelessly from an Apple II computer. Discontinued.

    Trilobite n., wheeled machine. Electrolux, Sweden, 2001. The first mass market robot vacuum cleaner, a disc shaped machine that sensed rooms with ultrasound and returned to its dock to recharge. Discontinued.

    TUG T3 n., wheeled machine. Aethon, United States, 2004. Hospital courier robot that tows carts of medication, meals, linen and waste, calling elevators and automatic doors over the building network. In production.

    TurtleBot n., wheeled machine. Willow Garage, 2010. Personal robot kit that runs open source software, first developed at Willow Garage in November 2010 by Melonee Wise and Tully Foote.

    TurtleBot 4 n., wheeled machine. Clearpath and Open Robotics, Canada, 2022. The standard issue teaching robot of the ROS curriculum. In production.

    TurtleBot3 n., wheeled machine. ROBOTIS, South Korea, 2017. Small open source research and teaching robot with a spinning laser scanner, used in university courses and labs to learn mapping and navigation. In production.

    TyBot n., wheeled machine. Advanced Construction Robotics, United States. Self propelled gantry robot that rolls along a bridge deck rebar mat and ties intersections automatically without needing a building information model. In service.

    U

    Ulysses n., wheeled machine. Greece. Bomb detecting robot used at Athens International Airport Eleftherios Venizelos in Greece, the country's largest international airport.

    UVD Robot n., wheeled machine. Blue Ocean Robotics, Denmark, 2018. Mobile ultraviolet disinfection robot that drives itself around hospital rooms and exposes surfaces to short wavelength light after manual cleaning. In production.

    V

    Vector n., wheeled machine. Anki (Digital Dream Labs), United States, 2018. A palm sized desk companion with big eyes and a cloud brain, revived by its fans. In production.

    Vitirover n., wheeled machine. Vitirover, France. Small solar powered grazing robot that trims grass between vine rows and along railway verges, working inside a geofenced plot for days unattended. In production.

    W

    W3 n., wheeled machine. Keenon Robotics, China, 2022. A hotel and building courier that rides lifts and passes through doors on its own. In production.

    Warthog n., wheeled machine. Clearpath Robotics, Canada, 2016. An amphibious heavyweight research vehicle for mines, farms and shorelines. In production.

    Whiz n., wheeled machine. SoftBank Robotics, Japan, 2019. A vacuum sweeper trained by walking it once, then set loose on office carpets. In production.

    X

    XAG R150 n., wheeled machine. XAG, China. Compact wheeled farm vehicle that sprays orchards and carries loads along mapped routes, controlled from a phone and positioned by satellite reference stations. In production.

    Xiaomanlv n., wheeled machine. Cainiao, Alibaba Group, China, 2020. Low speed autonomous parcel van that delivers packages on Chinese university campuses and residential districts, developed by Alibaba and operated by its Cainiao logistics arm. In service.

    XV-11 n., wheeled machine. Neato Robotics, United States, 2010. The first consumer robot vacuum with a spinning laser scanner, which let it map a room and clean in straight overlapping rows. Discontinued.

    Y

    Yarbo n., wheeled machine. Yarbo, China, 2023. A modular yard robot that swaps between snow blowing, mowing and leaf blowing bodies. In production.

    youBot n., wheeled machine. KUKA, Germany. Small omnidirectional base with a five axis arm, sold by KUKA to universities for research and teaching. Discontinued.

    Z

    Zenbo n., wheeled machine. ASUS, Taiwan, 2016. A rolling home assistant that read bedtime stories in Taiwanese households. Discontinued.
