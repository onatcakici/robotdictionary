    Arms and workcells

    5DX

    n., workcell. Agilent Technologies, United States. Discontinued.

    Automated inspection robot, made by Hewlett Packard and later Agilent Technologies, that took 3D radiographic images of assembled circuit boards to check the solder.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAgilent Technologies
    CountryUnited States
    StatusDiscontinued

    Of note

    • Production passed to Agilent when Hewlett Packard split in 1999
    • Showed solder thickness in 8 bit grayscale using laminography
    • Gave process feedback to surface mount assembly lines and caught defects
