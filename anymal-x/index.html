    ANYmal X

    n., quadruped. ANYbotics, Switzerland, 2023. In production.

    The explosion proof ANYmal certified for oil, gas and chemical plants.

    Measures and facts
    MakerANYbotics
    CountrySwitzerland
    First shown2023
    StatusIn production

    ANYmal X carries its certification like armour: the first explosion proof legged robot, cleared for the gas soaked zones of chemical and energy plants where a spark is catastrophe.
