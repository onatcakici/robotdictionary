    Hexbug

    n., toy robot. Spin Master, 2007. In production.

    Brand of robotic insect toys from Spin Master that borrows from BEAM robotics, its first models imitating six legged arthropods.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSpin Master
    First shown2007
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • First test marketed in the United States through RadioShack
    • Name refers to its six sided packaging, not its number of legs
