    Wheeled machines

    Thorvald

    n., wheeled machine. Saga Robotics, Norway. In service.

    Modular battery electric field platform best known for carrying ultraviolet lamps that kill mildew on strawberries and grapes during night passes.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSaga Robotics
    CountryNorway
    StatusIn service
    ActuationModular four wheel drive electric platform with reconfigurable track width and clearance
    PowerBattery electric drive
    SensorsLidar, cameras and satellite positioning
    AutonomyAutonomous row navigation with remote monitoring, working unattended at night

    Thorvald is a modular four wheel drive electric platform whose track width and clearance can be rebuilt for different crops, from strawberry tunnels to vineyards. Its signature application is a ultraviolet C light module that is driven over crops at night to suppress powdery mildew, reducing fungicide use. Navigation uses lidar, cameras and satellite positioning, with autonomous row following and remote supervision. Saga Robotics runs it as a treatment service on farms in Norway, the United Kingdom and the United States.

    Of note

    • Ultraviolet C treatment module suppresses powdery mildew on strawberries and grapes without fungicide
    • Originated in research at the Norwegian University of Life Sciences
    • Offered as a service on commercial berry and vineyard operations in Europe and the United States
