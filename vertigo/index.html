    VertiGo

    n., robot. ETH Zurich and Disney Research Zurich, Switzerland. Research platform.

    Remote controlled wall climbing robot on wheels, developed by ETH Zurich and Disney Research Zurich, that uses two fully tilting propellers to push itself onto and up walls.

    Measures and facts
    MakerETH Zurich and Disney Research Zurich
    CountrySwitzerland
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Its propellers can tilt through a full 360 degrees
    • Built on a carbon fiber baseplate with 3D printed joints
    • Can roll across the floor and then move onto a vertical wall
