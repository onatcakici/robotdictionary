    Molecubes

    n., robot. Cornell University. Research platform.

    Modular robot system from Cornell University made of cubes with two rotating halves that can be joined together into many different shapes.

    Measures and facts
    MakerCornell University
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Designed by Hod Lipson, Victor Zykov and Stathis Mytilinaios
    • One half of each cube holds a microprocessor, the other a motor
