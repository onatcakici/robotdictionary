    Skypod

    n., wheeled machine. Exotec, France, 2018. In production.

    Warehouse robot that drives across the floor and then climbs the face of the racking to retrieve bins stored up to 14 metres high.

    Measures and facts
    MakerExotec
    CountryFrance
    First shown2018
    StatusIn production
    Payload30 kg
    Top speed4 m/s (14.4 km/h)
    ActuationFour wheel drive chassis that grips rack rails and climbs vertically
    PowerRechargeable battery with automatic charging
    SensorsOnboard cameras and laser sensing
    AutonomyOnboard navigation coordinated by Exotec fleet software

    A Skypod is a four wheel drive robot that travels on the floor at up to 4 m per second, then grips rails on a rack upright and climbs vertically to reach a bin. Each robot carries one bin of up to 30 kg and delivers it to a picking station, so throughput scales by adding robots rather than rebuilding the rack. Onboard navigation and Exotec fleet software coordinate the movement, and the robots can drive under racking to change aisles. Systems are installed for grocers, sporting goods retailers, apparel brands and manufacturers.

    Of note

    • Climbs racking to about 14 m to reach stored bins
    • Named customers include Carrefour, Decathlon and Gap
    • Throughput is scaled by adding robots to an existing rack
