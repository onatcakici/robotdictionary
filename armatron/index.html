    Arms and workcells

    Armatron

    n., robot arm. Tomy, 1984.

    Joystick controlled toy robot arm from Tomy, sold through Radio Shack stores in the United States from 1984, with no automatic functions.

    Measures and facts
    MakerTomy
    First shown1984

    Of note

    • Six degrees of freedom, including a gripper with two fingers
    • Wrist and shoulder could rotate without limit
    • Looked like a 1980s industrial robot but was fully manual
