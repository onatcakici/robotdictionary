    Underwater and marine

    Mini Rover ROV

    n., underwater robot. Deep Sea Systems International, United States, 1983.

    Tethered underwater remotely operated vehicle created by Chris Nicholson of Deep Sea Systems International, introduced in 1983 as the first compact and inexpensive ROV.

    Measures and facts
    MakerDeep Sea Systems International
    CountryUnited States
    First shown1983

    Of note

    • Launched at $26,850 when the next cheapest ROV cost $100,000
    • First unit built in Nicholson's garage in Falmouth, Massachusetts
    • 26 inches long and 55 pounds, small enough to travel as airline luggage
