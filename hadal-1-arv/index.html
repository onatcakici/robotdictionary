    Underwater and marine

    Hadal 1 ARV

    n., hybrid underwater vehicle. Shenyang Institute of Automation, China.

    Chinese hybrid unmanned underwater vehicle from the Shenyang Institute of Automation that can work either autonomously or under remote control, succeeding the Hadal ARV.

    Measures and facts
    MakerShenyang Institute of Automation
    CountryChina

    Of note

    • Can operate as an autonomous vehicle or a remotely operated one
    • Chief designer is Tang Yuan-Gui
    • Chinese counterpart to the Nereus hybrid underwater vehicle
