    Humanoids

    Myon

    n., humanoid. Humboldt University of Berlin, Neurorobotics Research Laboratory, Germany, 2010. Research platform.

    Modular humanoid from Berlin whose limbs can be detached and keep working, built for research on distributed neural style control.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHumboldt University of Berlin, Neurorobotics Research Laboratory
    CountryGermany
    First shown2010
    StatusResearch platform
    Height1.25 m
    Mass15 kg
    ActuationModular electric actuator units, one set per body part
    AutonomyDistributed neural style control across per limb processing nodes

    Myon is about 1.25 m tall and weighs roughly 15 kg, with each body part carrying its own power supply, processing and actuation so a single arm or leg still functions once removed. Control runs across a distributed network of small processing nodes rather than one central computer, which suits experiments in sensorimotor learning and self exploration. The robot took a singing and acting role in the opera My Square Lady at the Komische Oper Berlin in 2015.

    Of note

    • Limbs can be removed and continue operating on their own power
    • Performed in the opera My Square Lady at the Komische Oper Berlin
