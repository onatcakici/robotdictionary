    Humanoids

    Robosapien V2

    n., humanoid.

    Second generation humanoid toy robot in Mark Tilden's Robosapien line, about 56 cm tall and able to speak a set of recorded phrases.

    Measures and facts

    Of note

    • Nearly twice the size of the original Robosapien
    • Replaced the original's caveman grunts with prerecorded speech
    • Has infrared, colour, grip, touch and sonic sensors
