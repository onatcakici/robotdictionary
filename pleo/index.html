    Quadrupeds

    Pleo

    n., quadruped. Innvo Labs.

    Animatronic toy dinosaur modelled on a baby Camarasaurus about a week old, designed by Caleb Chung, who helped create the Furby, and first sold by Ugobe.

    Measures and facts
    MakerInnvo Labs

    Of note

    • Designed by Caleb Chung, one of the Furby's creators
    • First sold by Ugobe and now made by Innvo Labs
    • Its large body and head leave room to hide sensors and motors
