    Rovers

    Rosalind Franklin

    Also known as ExoMars rover

    n., rover. Airbus Defence and Space for the European Space Agency, Europe. Prototype.

    European ExoMars rover built to drill two metres below the surface of Oxia Planum in search of preserved organic biosignatures.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAirbus Defence and Space for the European Space Agency
    CountryEurope
    StatusPrototype
    Mass310 kg
    ActuationSix wheel flexible chassis with wheel walking locomotion
    PowerSolar arrays with radioisotope heater units for night survival
    SensorsPanCam, ISEM, CLUPI, WISDOM radar, Ma_MISS borehole spectrometer, MOMA, MicrOmega, Raman spectrometer
    AutonomyOnboard autonomous navigation for long single sol traverses
    Height2 m
    Width2.5 m

    The Rosalind Franklin rover is completed flight hardware, assembled by Airbus in Stevenage and held in Turin after the 2022 launch was cancelled. It carries a two metre drill, the Mars Organic Molecule Analyser and a six wheel flexible chassis that can wheel walk out of soft ground, and NASA is supplying the launch, braking engines and radioisotope heater units. ESA now plans a 2028 launch on a Falcon Heavy with landing at Oxia Planum in 2030.

    Of note

    • Designed to drill two metres deep, far deeper than any previous Mars rover
    • Flight model is built and awaiting a 2028 launch
    • Named after the chemist whose X ray work underpinned DNA structure
