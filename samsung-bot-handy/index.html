    Wheeled machines

    Bot Handy

    Also known as Samsung Bot Handy

    n., wheeled machine. Samsung Electronics, South Korea, 2021. Announced, not yet in service.

    Concept home robot from Samsung, a wheeled column with one arm meant to load dishwashers, set tables and pour drinks.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSamsung Electronics
    CountrySouth Korea
    First shown2021
    StatusAnnounced, not yet in service

    Of note

    • Shown at CES in January 2021 alongside Bot Care
    • Uses a camera and AI to recognise objects and judge how hard to grip them

    Sources

    1. Samsung's latest home robots can do chores and nag you to stop working, Engadget, engadget.com
    2. Samsung's Bot Handy can put away dishes, set a table, and pour drinks for you, TechSpot, techspot.com
