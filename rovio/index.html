    Wheeled machines

    Rovio

    n., wheeled machine. WowWee.

    Wireless networked robot made by WowWee that carries a webcam, letting its user watch the camera feed and drive it remotely over the internet.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWowWee

    Of note

    • Can be controlled from a PC, Mac, smartphone, PDA or PSP
