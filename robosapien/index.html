    Humanoids

    Robosapien

    n., humanoid. WowWee, 2004.

    Biomorphic toy robot created by Mark Tilden and made by WowWee, released in 2004 with preset moves and infrared remote control.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWowWee
    First shown2004

    Of note

    • Released in 2004
    • Sold more than 1.5 million units from April to December 2004
    • Named Toy of the Year by the Toy Retailers Association
