    Wheeled machines

    TiLR

    Also known as Telepresence internet-connected Low cost Robot

    n., wheeled machine. RoboDynamics Corporation.

    Telepresence robot from RoboDynamics Corporation that stands in for a remote user at another location, reducing the need for business travel.

    Measures and facts
    MakerRoboDynamics Corporation

    Of note

    • Described as the first enterprise grade telepresence robot
