    Rovers

    Idefix

    n., rover. CNES and German Aerospace Center, France, 2026. In service.

    Small rover built by France's CNES and Germany's DLR with help from JAXA, carried by the MMX spacecraft to set down on and explore the Martian moon Phobos.

    Measures and facts
    MakerCNES and German Aerospace Center
    CountryFrance
    First shown2026
    StatusIn service
    Height23 cm
    Mass23.47 kg
    Length41 cm
    Width37 cm

    Of note

    • Lifted off aboard MMX on 19 October 2026
    • Mass 23.47 kg; 41 cm long, 37 cm wide and 23 cm high
    • Meant to survey Phobos ahead of MMX's own landing and sample collection
