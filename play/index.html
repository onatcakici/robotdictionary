    Robodle

    No. 3

    Name today's robot from its shadow. You have eight guesses, and each one tells you how close you came.

      Your guessSectionCountryMakerYearStatus

      How to play

      Every day there is one robot from the dictionary to find. Type a name and pick it from the list. Each guess is compared with the answer.

      • Green means the same.
      • Yellow means close: a country in the same part of the world, a year within five, or a maker that shares a name.
      • Plain means not it.

      Arrows on the year point toward the answer. After five misses you are told the first letter. A new robot arrives at midnight, your time. Your results stay in this browser.
