    Humanoids

    Figure 03

    n., humanoid. Figure AI, United States, 2025. In pilot deployments.

    Figure's third machine, built for the Helix vision language action model and scaled to dozens of units at customer sites.

    Measures and facts
    MakerFigure AI
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2025
    StatusIn pilot deployments
    Heightapprox. 168 cm
    DeploymentsBMW; multiple pilots
    Business modelrobots as a service
    Mass61 kg

    Figure 03 was designed around Helix, the company's vision language action model, with a home safe soft exterior and camera equipped palms. Dozens of units were reported working a BMW site by 2026, and a Series C in late 2025 valued Figure at thirty nine billion dollars.
