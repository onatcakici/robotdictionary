    Rovers

    Bridget

    n., rover. Airbus Defence and Space, United Kingdom. Research platform.

    Airbus Mars rover testbed used in the Stevenage Mars Yard and in Atacama Desert trials of European rover autonomy software.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAirbus Defence and Space
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    StatusResearch platform
    ActuationSix wheels in three bogies with steering
    PowerBattery electric with cabled supply during yard tests
    SensorsStereo navigation cameras with mast mounted instruments
    AutonomyTestbed for European autonomous navigation software

    Bridget is a six wheeled development rover roughly the size of the ExoMars vehicle, used by Airbus in Stevenage to try out chassis, navigation and instrument concepts. In ESA's SAFER campaign it was deployed to the Atacama Desert in Chile and operated as though on Mars, testing autonomous navigation, ground penetrating radar and drilling operations planned from Europe. It continues to serve as a mobile platform for European rover autonomy and instrument development.

    Of note

    • Operated in the Atacama Desert during ESA's SAFER Mars analogue trial
    • Long running testbed for European rover autonomy
