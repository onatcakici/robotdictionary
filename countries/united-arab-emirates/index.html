    Robots of United Arab Emirates

    2 entries built in United Arab Emirates, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    Ibn Sina Robot n., android. Al Ain University, United Arab Emirates, 2009. Android robot made in 2009 by Nikolaos Mavridis and David Hanson in the United Arab Emirates, described as the first android able to converse in Arabic.

    Rovers

    Rashid n., rover. Mohammed bin Rashid Space Centre, United Arab Emirates, 2022. Emirati micro rover built to study lunar dust and plasma, lost when the ispace Hakuto-R lander crashed in April 2023. Lost.
