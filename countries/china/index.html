    Rovers

    Yutu n., rover. China Academy of Space Technology, China, 2013. China's first lunar rover, landed in Mare Imbrium by Chang'e 3, which lost mobility after one lunar day but kept transmitting for years. Retired.

    Yutu-2 n., rover. China Academy of Space Technology, China, 2018. Chinese far side lunar rover delivered by Chang'e 4, the longest operating rover on the Moon, with 1,613 metres logged by September 2025. In service.

    Zhurong n., rover. China Academy of Space Technology, China, 2020. China's first Mars rover, which explored southern Utopia Planitia for a year and found evidence of water altered soils and buried shorelines. Lost.

    Medical and surgical

    GD01 n., exoskeleton. Unitree Robotics, China. Large powered exoskeleton made by the Chinese company Unitree Robotics, standing 2.7 m tall with a mass of 500 kg.

    Underwater and marine

    8A4 class ROUV n., underwater robot. Shenyang Institute of Automation, China. Remotely operated underwater vehicle of the work class type, developed by China's Shenyang Institute of Automation for jobs ranging from oil platform servicing to salvage and rescue.

    Explorer AUV n., underwater robot. China, 1994. Chinese autonomous underwater vehicle that entered service in November 1994 and supplied the experience behind many later Chinese designs.

    Hadal ARV n., underwater robot. Shenyang Institute of Automation, China. Chinese hybrid unmanned underwater vehicle from the Shenyang Institute of Automation that can work either autonomously or under remote control, much like Nereus.

    Sea Dragon class ROV n., underwater robot. China. Series of Chinese work class remotely operated vehicles for oil platform servicing, salvage and rescue, all derived from the original Sea Dragon. In service.

    SJT class ROUV n., underwater robot. Shenyang Institute of Automation and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China. Chinese family of remotely operated underwater vehicles created as a joint project of the Shenyang Institute of Automation and the underwater engineering institute of Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

    Submerged Dragon 1 n., underwater robot. China. Chinese autonomous underwater vehicle, an uncrewed craft that operates beneath the sea surface under its own onboard control.
