    Robots of France

    26 entries built in France, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    Calvin 40 n., humanoid. Wandercraft, France, 2025. An industrial humanoid grown from a decade of self balancing exoskeleton engineering. Announced, not yet in service.

    Mirokai n., humanoid. Enchanted Tools, France, 2023. Character styled service robot that balances on a single ball, trialled in Paris hospitals for carrying samples, linens and small deliveries. Prototype.

    NAO n., humanoid. Aldebaran, France, 2008. Small programmable biped sold in large numbers to schools, universities and research labs, and long the standard platform for RoboCup soccer leagues. Discontinued.

    Pepper n., humanoid. Aldebaran, France, 2014. Wheeled social humanoid used for greeting and information duties in shops, banks and airports, with about 17,000 units sold before production ended. Discontinued.

    Poppy n., humanoid. Inria Flowers Laboratory, France, 2013. Open source 3D printed humanoid from Inria used for developmental robotics experiments and widely adopted in schools and universities. Research platform.

    RABBIT n., humanoid. CNRS ROBEA project consortium, France, 2002. Underactuated planar biped built by a French research consortium to test analytical control theory for walking and running without feet. Retired.

    Reachy 2 n., humanoid. Pollen Robotics, France, 2024. Open source humanoid torso on an omnidirectional base, sold to research and teleoperation customers and now built under Hugging Face ownership. In production.

    Wheeled machines

    Air-Cobot n., wheeled machine. France. Wheeled collaborative robot from a French research project, built to check aircraft during maintenance while working alongside human staff. Prototype.

    Buddy n., wheeled machine. Blue Frog Robotics, France, 2018. A French emotional companion robot aimed at classrooms and eldercare. In pilot deployments.

    Colossus n., wheeled machine. France. French robot built for firefighting, meant to help fire services by taking on fire suppression work.

    GSF AutoVacC 6 n., wheeled machine. Robosoft, France, 1990. French prototype industrial cleaning robot of the AutoVacC type, built by Robosoft in 1990. Prototype.

    Hilare n., wheeled machine. Laboratory for Analysis and Architecture of Systems, France, 1977. French mobile robot built in 1977 by the Laboratory for Analysis and Architecture of Systems, its name an acronym for heuristics integrated into software and automation in an evolving robot.

    Oz n., wheeled machine. Naio Technologies, France, 2015. Small electric tool carrier that weeds, sows and marks rows in market garden beds, driving itself between crop rows using satellite guidance. In production.

    Skypod n., wheeled machine. Exotec, France, 2018. Warehouse robot that drives across the floor and then climbs the face of the racking to retrieve bins stored up to 14 metres high. In production.

    SYRANO n., wheeled machine. France. French military robot, named for a robotic system for target acquisition and neutralization, that became the first battlefield robot in operational use with the French armed forces. In service.

    Vitirover n., wheeled machine. Vitirover, France. Small solar powered grazing robot that trims grass between vine rows and along railway verges, working inside a geofenced plot for days unattended. In production.

    Arms and workcells

    Three finger prehensile robot n., workcell. France, 1990. Gripping unit with three fingers, dating from 1990, designed to fit the end of assembly robots in the car industry and lent by the Laboratoire de robotique de Paris.

    Rovers

    Idefix n., rover. CNES and German Aerospace Center, France, 2026. Small rover built by France's CNES and Germany's DLR with help from JAXA, carried by the MMX spacecraft to set down on and explore the Martian moon Phobos. In service.

    Medical and surgical

    Atalante n., exoskeleton. Wandercraft, France. Self balancing exoskeleton from Wandercraft that lets people with paralysis walk without crutches. In production.

    Maestro n., medical robot. Moon Surgical, France. Pair of robotic arms from Moon Surgical that hold the camera and retract tissue for the surgeon during keyhole surgery. In production.

    ROSA n., medical robot. Zimmer Biomet, France. Robotic surgical assistant first developed by Medtech in France for brain and spine procedures, later used for knee replacement. In production.

    Underwater and marine

    Épaulard n., underwater robot. France. Uncrewed underwater vehicle from France, operated remotely and belonging to the Ifremer institute, for work beneath the sea surface.

    Jessiko n., marine robot. Robotswim, France. Small autonomous robotic fish, 22 cm long, created by the French startup Robotswim, based in Palaiseau near Paris.

    Vaimos n., marine robot. IFREMER and École nationale supérieure de techniques avancées Bretagne, France. French autonomous sailing boat carrying onboard instruments for measuring the ocean surface, developed by IFREMER and ENSTA Bretagne.

    Other forms

    Digesting Duck n., automaton. Jacques de Vaucanson, France. Mechanical duck built by Jacques de Vaucanson that seemed to eat grain and excrete it, although the droppings were stored in advance and nothing was actually digested.

    Squirrel Robot n., robot. LIST, France, 1990. Inspection robot built in France in 1990 by LIST to get into hazardous parts of nuclear plants and send back reports on conditions there.
