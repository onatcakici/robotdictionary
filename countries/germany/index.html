    Robots of Germany

    48 entries built in Germany, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    4NE-1 n., humanoid. Neura Robotics, Germany, 2023. Europe's leading commercial humanoid effort, built on Neura's cognitive robot stack. Research platform.

    Agile ONE n., humanoid. Agile Robots, Germany, 2025. Humanoid robot introduced in 2025 by Agile Robots, a robotics company from Germany.

    ARMAR-6 n., humanoid. Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany, 2018. A collaborative wheeled humanoid that hands tools to technicians and learns from demonstration. Research platform.

    ARMAR-III n., humanoid. Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany, 2006. Kitchen oriented humanoid from Karlsruhe with 43 degrees of freedom on a holonomic wheeled base, used for household manipulation research. Research platform.

    GARMI n., humanoid. TU Munich, Germany, 2021. A wheeled geriatric care humanoid doubling as a telemedicine avatar. Research platform.

    LOLA n., humanoid. Technical University of Munich, Germany, 2010. Munich research biped built for fast walking, with 25 actuated joints and a light structure that reached 3.34 km/h in experiments. Research platform.

    MiPA n., humanoid. Neura Robotics, Germany, 2024. A wheeled household and care humanoid intended for serial production in Europe. In pilot deployments.

    Myon n., humanoid. Humboldt University of Berlin, Neurorobotics Research Laboratory, Germany, 2010. Modular humanoid from Berlin whose limbs can be detached and keep working, built for research on distributed neural style control. Research platform.

    Rollin' Justin n., humanoid. German Aerospace Center DLR, Germany, 2008. Wheeled humanoid from DLR with torque controlled arms and hands, commanded from the International Space Station in orbital telerobotics experiments. Research platform.

    TORO n., humanoid. German Aerospace Center DLR, Germany, 2013. Torque controlled humanoid from DLR assembled from lightweight robot arm joint modules to test compliant whole body balancing and walking. Research platform.

    Quadrupeds

    Arachno-Bot n., legged robot. Fraunhofer Institute of Manufacturing Engineering and Automation, Germany, 2011. Eight legged spider inspired robot developed in 2011 at the Fraunhofer Institute of Manufacturing Engineering and Automation in Stuttgart to survey areas too dangerous for people. Research platform.

    BISAM n., quadruped. FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik, Germany, 1997. Mammal like four legged walking machine from Karlsruhe, built to study adaptive gait control with an articulated trunk. Retired.

    LAURON n., legged robot. FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik, Germany, 1994. Six legged walking robot modelled on the stick insect Carausius morosus, developed over several generations by the German research centre FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik. Research platform.

    Momaro n., legged robot. University of Bonn, Germany, 2015. Centaur like robot from the University of Bonn with four legs ending in wheels and two arms, built for the DARPA Robotics Challenge. Retired.

    Solo 12 n., quadruped. Open Dynamic Robot Initiative, Germany, 2020. Open hardware torque controlled quadruped weighing a few kilograms, designed so any laboratory could build one from printed parts. Research platform.

    Solo 8 n., quadruped. Open Dynamic Robot Initiative, Germany, 2019. A fully open hardware research quadruped reproducible on a lab budget. Research platform.

    Wheeled machines

    AMOR n., wheeled machine. University of Siegen, Germany. Autonomous robot built for outdoor operation at the University of Siegen in Germany, working without direct human control. Research platform.

    ARTOS n., wheeled machine. University of Kaiserslautern, Germany. Autonomous robot for transport and service tasks, developed at the University of Kaiserslautern in Germany.

    Care-O-bot 4 n., wheeled machine. Fraunhofer IPA, Germany, 2015. A gentleman butler service robot with a bowing torso and tray hands. Research platform.

    KMR iiwa n., wheeled machine. KUKA, Germany. Omnidirectional mobile platform carrying a seven axis sensitive arm, used to move and load parts and samples in laboratories and factories. In production.

    Marvin n., wheeled machine. University of Kaiserslautern, Germany. Indoor mobile research robot from the Robotics Lab at the University of Kaiserslautern, using laser scanners, ultrasonic sensors and a camera to navigate and map rooms. Research platform.

    RAVON n., wheeled machine. University of Kaiserslautern, Germany. Off road research vehicle from the Robotics Research Lab at the University of Kaiserslautern, used to test behaviour based navigation and localisation on uneven outdoor ground. Research platform.

    RHINO n., wheeled machine. University of Bonn, Germany, 1997. Research tour guide robot that led museum visitors for six days in 1997, among the first robots to work unattended in a public crowd. Retired.

    Robotino n., wheeled machine. Festo Didactic, Germany. Omnidirectional three wheeled training robot used in classrooms and competitions, able to move in any direction without turning first. Research platform.

    SCITOS G5 n., wheeled machine. MetraLabs, Germany, 2007. A German service robot base that has guided museum visitors for years. In production.

    TeleMAX n., wheeled machine. Rheinmetall AG, Germany. Robot product model offered by Rheinmetall AG, a manufacturer based in Germany.

    youBot n., wheeled machine. KUKA, Germany. Small omnidirectional base with a five axis arm, sold by KUKA to universities for research and teaching. Discontinued.

    Arms and workcells

    CA-1 n., workcell. Circus SE, Germany, 2025. Autonomous robotic cooking system introduced in 2025 by the German company Circus SE.

    CA-M n., workcell. Circus SE, Germany, 2025. Autonomous provisioning system for military operations, introduced in 2025 by the German company Circus SE.

    e-David n., workcell. University of Konstanz, Germany, 2009. Painting robot developed at the University of Konstanz that works out brushstrokes from a source image and paints it onto canvas. Research platform.

    InLoop n., workcell. InLoop Robotics, Germany. Robot arms that pick and pack online orders, with remote operators stepping in when a grasp fails.

    KR 1000 titan n., robot arm. KUKA, Germany, 2007. Heavy duty arm from KUKA rated for loads of about one tonne, used to move engine blocks, stone and glass. In production.

    KR AGILUS n., robot arm. KUKA, Germany. Family of small, fast six axis arms from KUKA used in electronics and packaging lines. In production.

    LBR iiwa n., robot arm. KUKA, Germany, 2013. Seven axis lightweight arm from KUKA with torque sensors in every joint, developed from DLR research arms. In production.

    Panda n., robot arm. Franka Emika, Germany, 2017. Seven axis arm with torque sensing in every joint, sold at a low price and widely adopted by research laboratories. Discontinued.

    Rovers

    Audi Lunar Quattro n., rover. Planetary Transportation Systems and Audi AG, Germany, 2015. Small German lunar rover developed in 2015 by engineers of the PTScientists team, with backing from Audi and partners in several countries.

    Lightweight Rover Unit n., rover. DLR Institute of Robotics and Mechatronics, Germany, 2017. German four wheeled research rover used in Mount Etna campaigns to test autonomous lunar style survey and sample collection. Research platform.

    MASCOT n., rover. DLR and CNES, Germany, 2018. Shoebox sized lander carried by Hayabusa2 that used an internal swinging arm to hop across asteroid Ryugu in 2018. Mission completed.

    SherpaTT n., rover. DFKI Robotics Innovation Center, Germany, 2016. Hybrid wheeled leg rover with actively articulated suspension, field tested in the Utah desert and in Morocco for Mars analogue work. Research platform.

    Medical and surgical

    Michelangelo Hand n., medical robot. Ottobock and Advanced Arm Dynamics, Germany, 2010. Robotic prosthetic hand with full articulation made by the German company Ottobock with its American partner Advanced Arm Dynamics, the first prosthesis with an electronically driven thumb. In production.

    Underwater and marine

    SeaFox n., marine robot. Atlas Elektronik, Germany. German remotely operated underwater vehicle from Atlas Elektronik that finds and destroys seabed and moored sea mines, used by eleven navies. In service.

    Other forms

    Carl n., robot. University of Kaiserslautern, Germany. Robot in the form of a compliant robotic leg, built at the University of Kaiserslautern in Germany.

    CIMON n., robot. Airbus Commercial Aircraft, Germany. Head shaped robot with artificial intelligence, built by Airbus in Germany as a crew companion aboard the International Space Station. In service.

    Compressorhead n., animatronic figure. Germany, 2013. Robot rock cover band whose members are animatronic machines, created by Berlin based artist Frank Barnes and several collaborators.

    IceMole n., robot. FH Aachen, Germany, 2010. Autonomous, steerable ice probe developed at FH Aachen in Germany that melts and screws its way through ice for polar, glacier and space research. Research platform.

    Navel n., robot. navel robotics, Germany. Social robot developed by the German company navel robotics for use in care facilities.

    Robin n., robot. University of Kaiserslautern, Germany. Research robot for studying interaction between robots and humans, developed at the University of Kaiserslautern in Germany.

    Tradinno n., animatronic figure. Germany. Huge animatronic dragon that walks and breathes fire, built to play the dragon in the Drachenstich play in Furth im Wald, Germany. In service.
