    Robots of Brazil

    4 entries built in Brazil, grouped by section.

    Wheeled machines

    SkyFood n., wheeled machine. TracFtech, Brazil. Autonomous delivery robot from the Brazilian robotics company TracFtech that doubles as a mobile digital advertising display.

    Solix n., wheeled machine. Solinftec, Brazil, 2022. Solar powered four wheeled scouting and spraying robot that inspects individual plants and applies herbicide only where weeds are found in row crops. In production.

    Arms and workcells

    Kanguera n., robot hand. University of São Paulo, Brazil, 2007. Research robot hand from the University of São Paulo that models the kinematics of the human hand to help develop better humanlike grippers and manipulators. Research platform.

    RoboTurb n., workcell. Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina, Brazil, 1998. Welding robot developed at the Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina in Brazil to repair turbine blades, built as a redundant robot mounted on a flexible rail.
