    Robots of Spain

    9 entries built in Spain, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    ARI n., humanoid. PAL Robotics, Spain, 2020. A wheeled social humanoid built for reception desks and human robot interaction studies. In production.

    REEM-C n., humanoid. PAL Robotics, Spain, 2013. Adult size biped from PAL Robotics with fully articulated hands, sold as a research platform for walking, manipulation and human robot interaction work. Research platform.

    TALOS n., humanoid. PAL Robotics, Spain, 2017. Torque controlled full size humanoid sold by PAL Robotics as a platform for whole body control and dynamic locomotion research in industry and academia. Research platform.

    TEO n., humanoid. Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, RoboticsLab, Spain, 2012. Full size Spanish research humanoid used to study household tasks such as ironing, folding laundry and opening doors. Research platform.

    Wheeled machines

    SUMMIT-XL n., wheeled machine. Robotnik, Spain, 2012. A Spanish research AMR base common in European labs and EU projects. In production.

    TIAGo n., wheeled machine. PAL Robotics, Spain, 2015. Wheeled robot with a lifting torso and one arm from PAL Robotics, widely used as a research platform for home and care tasks. In production.

    Other forms

    AISoy1 n., robot. AISoy Robotics, Spain. Consumer pet robot from Spain's AISoy Robotics, counted among the first with emotional learning, whose decisions weigh logical and emotional factors rather than fixed replies.

    El Ajedrecista n., automaton. Leonardo Torres Quevedo, Spain, 1912. Chess automaton created by the Spanish engineer Leonardo Torres Quevedo that played out a reduced endgame with three pieces entirely on its own. Retired.

    Tico n., robot. Adele Robots, Spain. Spanish social robot made by Adele Robots to engage with people, chiefly at marketing events and in schools as a teacher's assistant.
