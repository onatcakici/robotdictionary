    Robots of Russia

    7 entries built in Russia, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    FEDOR n., humanoid. FPI and Android Technics, Russia, 2019. Russian anthropomorphic robot flown uncrewed to the International Space Station in 2019 to test teleoperated work in weightlessness. Retired.

    Promobot V.4 n., humanoid. Promobot, Russia, 2019. A wheeled promotional humanoid staffing malls, banks and airports in dozens of countries. In production.

    Wheeled machines

    Marker n., wheeled machine. Russian Foundation for Advanced Research Projects in the Defense Industry, Russia. Robotic platform from Russia, known there as Маркер, developed by the Russian Foundation for Advanced Research Projects in the Defense Industry.

    Uran-9 n., wheeled machine. Rostec, Russia. Russian tracked unmanned combat vehicle from JSC 766 UPTK, offered abroad through Rosoboronexport, that scouts and gives fire support from a distance. In production.

    Rovers

    LAMA n., rover. Mobile Vehicle Engineering Institute and Laboratory for Analysis and Architecture of Systems, Russia, 1990. French autonomous Mars rover prototype from 1990, developed by the Mobile Vehicle Engineering Institute and the Laboratory for Analysis and Architecture of Systems and weighing 300 kg. Prototype.

    Marsokhod n., rover. VNII Transmash, Russia, 1993. Soviet designed six wheeled rover chassis used in Russian, French and American field trials at volcanoes and deserts through the 1990s. Research platform.

    Underwater and marine

    Klavesin-1R n., underwater robot. Russia. Russian autonomous underwater vehicle, a type of uncrewed submersible that navigates and operates underwater without direct human control.
