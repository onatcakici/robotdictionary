    Robots of India

    10 entries built in India, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    Indro n., humanoid. India. Indian humanoid robot that stands 6.5 feet (2.0 m) tall, making it the tallest of its kind in the country.

    Mitra n., humanoid. Invento Robotics, India, 2017. Humanoid robot from Bangalore startup Invento Robotics that made its debut at the Global Entrepreneurship Summit in November 2017, where it welcomed Ivanka Trump.

    Rashmi n., humanoid. India. Indian female humanoid robot with lip synced speech in several languages, developed by Ranchi programmer Ranjit Shrivastava.

    Vyommitra n., humanoid. Indian Space Research Organisation, Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre, India, 2020. Half humanoid robot built by ISRO to fly on uncrewed Gaganyaan test missions and monitor cabin systems in place of a crew. Prototype.

    Wheeled machines

    DRDO Daksh n., wheeled machine. Defence Research and Development Organisation, India, 2011. Electrically driven, remote controlled bomb disposal robot from India's Defence Research and Development Organisation, built to locate and safely neutralise hazardous objects.

    Rovers

    Pragyan n., rover. Indian Space Research Organisation, India, 2023. Indian solar rover that explored the high southern latitudes of the Moon for one lunar day after the Chandrayaan-3 landing. Mission completed.

    Medical and surgical

    Morphle n., medical robot. Morphle Labs, India. Robotic microscopes from Bengaluru that automate slide scanning for cancer diagnostics.

    Underwater and marine

    AUV-150 n., underwater robot. Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute, India. Indian autonomous underwater vehicle under development by scientists at the Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute in Durgapur, West Bengal, with support from IIT Kharagpur. In development.

    Maya AUV n., underwater robot. National Institute of Oceanography, India. Indian autonomous underwater vehicle built by the marine instrumentation team at the National Institute of Oceanography.

    Other forms

    Tipu's Tiger n., robot. India, 1790. Eighteenth century wooden automaton made for Tipu Sultan of Mysore, depicting a tiger attacking a British man, with a pipe organ inside its body.
