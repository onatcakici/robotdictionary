    Robots of Switzerland

    27 entries built in Switzerland, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    Luzi n., humanoid. Switzerland. Individual Pepper robot given the name Luzi and operated by ZHB Luzern in Switzerland. In service.

    Robert the Robot n., humanoid. RB Labs, Switzerland, 2025. Humanoid robot from the Swiss financial technology company RB Labs, launched in 2025 to give investment advice and financial guidance in conversation.

    Roboy n., humanoid. University of Zurich Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, Switzerland, 2013. Tendon driven humanoid built in about nine months by a Zurich led consortium to copy human muscle and tendon actuation in a child sized body. Research platform.

    Quadrupeds

    ANYmal n., quadruped. ANYbotics, Switzerland, 2016. The industrial inspection quadruped patrolling offshore platforms and plants, offered in explosion proof form. In production.

    ANYmal B n., quadruped. ANYbotics, Switzerland, 2016. First ANYmal generation, a sealed electric quadruped with torque controlled joints sold to research groups and early industrial pilots. Research platform.

    ANYmal C n., quadruped. ANYbotics, Switzerland, 2019. Industrial version of ANYmal, packaged for autonomous inspection rounds in power plants, chemical works and offshore facilities. In service.

    ANYmal D n., quadruped. ANYbotics, Switzerland, 2021. Current ANYbotics inspection quadruped, a fifty kilogram sealed machine that walks industrial plants on its own and returns to dock. In production.

    ANYmal X n., quadruped. ANYbotics, Switzerland, 2023. The explosion proof ANYmal certified for oil, gas and chemical plants. In production.

    Cheetah-cub n., quadruped. EPFL Biorobotics Laboratory, Switzerland, 2013. Cat sized compliant quadruped from EPFL that was the fastest small four legged robot of its time relative to body size. Research platform.

    RIVR n., quadruped. RIVR (Swiss-Mile), Switzerland, 2023. A wheel legged delivery quadruped that stands upright to hand over parcels; trained end to end in simulation. In pilot deployments.

    SpaceBok n., quadruped. ETH Zurich Robotic Systems Lab, Switzerland, 2018. Jumping quadruped built by Swiss students to study legged movement in low gravity for lunar and asteroid exploration. Retired.

    StarlETH n., quadruped. ETH Zurich, Switzerland, 2012. Series elastic electric quadruped from ETH Zurich that showed compliant actuation could give small legged robots dynamic gaits. Retired.

    Wheeled machines

    Ascento Guard n., wheeled machine. Ascento, Switzerland, 2023. A two wheeled jumping security robot balancing on legs with wheels for feet. In pilot deployments.

    e-puck n., wheeled machine. EPFL, Switzerland, 2005. An open hardware education puck used in classrooms and swarm labs worldwide. In production.

    Khepera n., wheeled machine. K-Team, Switzerland, 1996. A matchbox research robot that made swarm experiments tabletop affairs. In production.

    RoboX n., wheeled machine. Switzerland, 2002. Swiss mobile robot, 168 cm tall and 64 cm wide, that appeared at the Expo 02 exhibition in 2002.

    Thymio n., wheeled machine. Mobsya, Switzerland, 2011. A friendly white classroom robot teaching programming to children across Europe. In production.

    Arms and workcells

    FlexPicker n., robot arm. ABB, Switzerland. High speed parallel arm robot from ABB that picks small items from conveyors, based on the delta design invented by Reymond Clavel at EPFL. In production.

    GoFa n., robot arm. ABB, Switzerland, 2021. Collaborative arm from ABB with joint torque sensing, meant to be set up by staff without robot programming experience. In production.

    Grip n., workcell. Grip, Switzerland. Picking robot that removes contaminants from organic waste streams, learning from every successful and failed grasp.

    SWIFTI n., robot arm. ABB, Switzerland, 2021. Small, fast arm from ABB that slows or stops when a person comes near, bridging industrial and collaborative robots. In production.

    YuMi n., robot arm. ABB, Switzerland, 2015. Two armed collaborative robot from ABB, padded and lightweight, built for small parts assembly beside people. In production.

    Medical and surgical

    Dexter n., medical robot. Distalmotion, Switzerland. Compact surgical robot from Distalmotion that hospitals can switch between robotic and ordinary keyhole surgery during a single operation. In production.

    Lokomat n., medical robot. Hocoma, Switzerland. Robotic gait trainer that moves a patient's legs over a treadmill while part of their body weight is supported by a harness. In production.

    Other forms

    Alice n., robot. École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Switzerland, 1998. Tiny cube shaped mobile robot measuring 2 by 2 by 2 cm, created at the Autonomous Systems Lab of the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne. Research platform.

    s-bot n., robot. EPFL, Switzerland, 2004. Small differential wheeled mobile robot, 15 cm in size, developed at the EPFL in Lausanne between 2001 and 2004 for swarm robotics research in the Swarm bots project. Research platform.

    VertiGo n., robot. ETH Zurich and Disney Research Zurich, Switzerland. Remote controlled wall climbing robot on wheels, developed by ETH Zurich and Disney Research Zurich, that uses two fully tilting propellers to push itself onto and up walls. Research platform.
