    Robots of United Kingdom

    27 entries built in United Kingdom, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    Ai-Da n., humanoid. Engineered Arts, United Kingdom, 2019. Humanoid robot artist, completed in 2019 by Engineered Arts, that produces drawings, paintings and sculptures. In service.

    Ameca n., humanoid. Engineered Arts, United Kingdom, 2021. The grey faced expressive android whose stunned expressions became the face of the AI era. In production.

    Eric n., humanoid. United Kingdom, 1928. Early British humanoid robot made in 1928 by William Richards and Alan Reffell, which stood, bowed and gave a speech to open an exhibition at London's Royal Horticultural Hall.

    George n., humanoid. Tony Sale, United Kingdom, 1949. British humanoid robot that Tony Sale assembled in 1949 from scrap metal taken off a crashed RAF Wellington bomber. Retired.

    Kaspar n., humanoid. University of Hertfordshire, United Kingdom, 2005. Humanoid social robot developed at the University of Hertfordshire to act as a companion for autistic children and others who struggle with communication. Research platform.

    Piggy n., humanoid. Piggy Robotics, United Kingdom. Low cost household humanoid from London driven by artificial muscles made of braided tubes rather than electric motors.

    Porton Man n., humanoid. i-bodi Technology, United Kingdom. Animatronic humanoid robot made by i-bodi Technology for the Defence Science and Technology Laboratory, used mainly to evaluate protective clothing for British military personnel.

    RoboThespian n., humanoid. Engineered Arts, United Kingdom, 2005. Life size interactive performing humanoid sold to science centres and visitor attractions since 2005, one of the longest running commercial humanoid products. In production.

    Quadrupeds

    Robug III n., quadruped. University of Portsmouth, United Kingdom. Mobile robot shaped like a spider, developed at the University of Portsmouth in the United Kingdom.

    Wheeled machines

    360 Eye n., wheeled machine. Dyson, United Kingdom, 2016. Dyson's tank tracked vacuum with a panoramic camera crown. Discontinued.

    Dyson 360 Heurist n., wheeled machine. Dyson, United Kingdom. Successor to the Dyson 360 Eye, with a 360 degree camera and a ring of lights that let it navigate in dim rooms.

    Elsie n., wheeled machine. W. Grey Walter, United Kingdom, 1949. One of the first electronic tortoises, seeking light with two vacuum tubes for a brain. Retired.

    Ocado 600 Series Bot n., wheeled machine. Ocado Technology, United Kingdom, 2022. Grid running grocery picking robot from Ocado, much lighter than its predecessors and built partly from 3D printed parts. In production.

    Wheelbarrow n., wheeled machine. United Kingdom, 1972. Remote controlled bomb disposal robot created in 1972 for British Army teams and used in Iraq, Northern Ireland and mainland Britain.

    Arms and workcells

    Moley Robotic Kitchen n., workcell. Moley Robotics, United Kingdom, 2015. Pair of ceiling mounted arms with dexterous hands that cook recipes recorded from human chefs. In production.

    Mytos n., workcell. Mytos, United Kingdom. Robotic platform that turns stem cells into specific cell types such as neurons and heart cells over several weeks.

    Shadow Dexterous Hand n., robot hand. Shadow Robot Company, United Kingdom. Dexterous robot hand from the Shadow Robot Company in London, built to human hand size and shape with the same full set of degrees of freedom. In production.

    Rovers

    Bridget n., rover. Airbus Defence and Space, United Kingdom. Airbus Mars rover testbed used in the Stevenage Mars Yard and in Atacama Desert trials of European rover autonomy software. Research platform.

    Medical and surgical

    Versius n., medical robot. CMR Surgical, United Kingdom, 2019. Modular surgical robot from CMR Surgical whose arms sit on separate carts so they can be arranged around the operating table. In production.

    Underwater and marine

    Boaty McBoatface n., underwater robot. National Oceanography Centre, United Kingdom, 2017. British autonomous underwater vehicle belonging to the Autosub Long Range class, run by the National Oceanography Centre for scientific research. In service.

    Discovery n., underwater robot. Specialist Machine Development, United Kingdom. Remotely operated underwater vehicle built in Britain by Specialist Machine Development for use in China; little has been published about it.

    ROV PHOCA n., underwater robot. Sub-Atlantic, United Kingdom. Compact remotely operated underwater vehicle built by Sub-Atlantic in Aberdeen and owned by the GEOMAR ocean research centre in Kiel, able to dive to 3000 m. In service.

    USV Maxlimer n., marine robot. United Kingdom. Uncrewed surface vessel run by the British firm SEA-KIT International that can work semi autonomously or under remote control, and won the Shell Ocean Discovery X Prize. In service.

    Other forms

    Creoqode NOVA n., robot. Creoqode, United Kingdom. Educational robot model from the British company Creoqode, intended for teaching and learning about robotics.

    Mephisto n., robot. United Kingdom, 1876. Chess playing machine passed off as an automaton, built in London by Charles Godfrey Gumpel, an Alsatian maker of artificial limbs, and first exhibited in 1878.

    MiRo n., robot. Consequential Robotics, United Kingdom. Social robot made by the British company Consequential Robotics, intended to interact and communicate with people.

    The Grand Elephant n., robot. La Machine, United Kingdom. Giant animatronic elephant built by the French theater company La Machine, about 12 m tall and 8 m wide, with a top speed of 1 km/h.
