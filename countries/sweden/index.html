    Robots of Sweden

    9 entries built in Sweden, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    AEON n., humanoid. Hexagon, Sweden, 2025. An industrial humanoid from the metrology giant, aimed at scanning and inspection work. Announced, not yet in service.

    Furhat n., humanoid. Furhat Robotics, Sweden. Conversational social robot from the Swedish company Furhat Robotics, with a humanlike face projected from behind and software for speech recognition and synthesis. In production.

    Wheeled machines

    Automower 450X n., wheeled machine. Husqvarna, Sweden, 1995. The descendant of the world's first robotic lawn mower, three decades on the job. In production.

    Einride Pod n., wheeled machine. Einride, Sweden, 2017. Cabless battery electric freight vehicle with no driver seat, operated on closed sites and short public road stretches with a remote operator supervising. In service.

    Scooptram ST18 n., wheeled machine. Epiroc, Sweden. Articulated underground loader rated at eighteen tonnes that can be run remotely from surface or drive itself between loading and dumping points. In production.

    Trilobite n., wheeled machine. Electrolux, Sweden, 2001. The first mass market robot vacuum cleaner, a disc shaped machine that sensed rooms with ultrasound and returned to its dock to recharge. Discontinued.

    Arms and workcells

    DeLaval VMS n., workcell. DeLaval, Sweden. Voluntary milking system in which a robot arm attaches the teat cups as each cow enters the stall. In production.

    IRB 6 n., robot arm. ASEA, Sweden, 1974. Early industrial arm from ASEA, the forerunner of ABB, often described as the first all electric microprocessor controlled industrial robot. Retired.

    Underwater and marine

    Double Eagle n., marine robot. Saab Underwater Systems, Sweden. Remotely operated underwater vehicle built by the Swedish defense company Saab Underwater Systems for disposing of naval mines, in use with several navies. In service.
