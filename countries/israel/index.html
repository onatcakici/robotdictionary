    Robots of Israel

    13 entries built in Israel, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    MenteeBot n., humanoid. Mentee Robotics, Israel, 2024. A household and warehouse humanoid trained end to end on language to motion models. In pilot deployments.

    Wheeled machines

    AvantGuard n., wheeled machine. G-NIUS Unmanned Ground Systems, Israel. Modular unmanned ground combat vehicle developed by G-NIUS Unmanned Ground Systems in Israel for military use.

    Ecoppia E4 n., wheeled machine. Ecoppia, Israel. Waterless robotic brush that travels along a row of solar modules each night to remove desert dust, powered by its own solar panel. In production.

    Guardium n., wheeled machine. G-NIUS, Israel. Autonomous patrol vehicle used by the Israeli army along its borders. Retired.

    MTGR n., wheeled machine. Israel. Small Israeli military ground robot whose name stands for Micro Tactical Ground Robot.

    Pathfinder n., wheeled machine. Bluewhite, Israel. Retrofit autonomy kit that turns conventional orchard and vineyard tractors into driverless machines supervised from a control room by one operator running several tractors. In production.

    RoBattle n., wheeled machine. Israel Aerospace Industries, Israel. Modular unmanned ground vehicle for military use, developed as a robotic platform by Israel Aerospace Industries.

    Robomow n., wheeled machine. Friendly Robotics, Israel. Line of battery robot lawn mowers from Israel guided by a perimeter wire around the lawn.

    temi n., wheeled machine. Temi, Israel, 2019. A telepresence and assistant robot that follows its owner from room to room. In production.

    Medical and surgical

    Flex Robotic System n., medical robot. Technion, Israel Institute of Technology, Israel, 2014. Medical snake robot for single port surgery, developed at the Technion in Israel and dating from 2014.

    Mazor X n., medical robot. Mazor Robotics, Israel, 2016. Robotic guidance system for spine surgery from Mazor Robotics, which Medtronic acquired in 2018. In production.

    ReWalk n., exoskeleton. ReWalk Robotics, Israel, 2011. Commercial powered exoskeleton that lets people with paraplegia stand, walk and climb stairs, designed in Yokneam, Israel, by Amit Goffer. In production.

    Underwater and marine

    BlueWhale n., underwater robot. Elta, Israel, 2025. Israeli unmanned underwater vehicle, designated ELI-3325, built by an ELTA Systems and Atlas Elektronik joint venture to gather intelligence using radar and signals intelligence equipment.
