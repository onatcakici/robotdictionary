    Robots of Netherlands

    9 entries built in Netherlands, grouped by section.

    Humanoids

    Denise n., humanoid. Delft University of Technology, Delft Biorobotics Laboratory, Netherlands, 2005. Passive dynamic walking biped from Delft driven by pneumatic muscles, notable for walking on a small fraction of the energy typical robots need. Retired.

    Flame n., humanoid. Delft University of Technology, Netherlands, 2008. Humanoid walking robot built by Daan Hobbelen at Delft University of Technology to study how humans walk, standing 130 cm tall and weighing 15 kg. Research platform.

    Wheeled machines

    AgBot 2.055W4 n., wheeled machine. AgXeed, Netherlands, 2022. Wheeled diesel electric autonomous tractor with a three point hitch and power take off that works pre planned field routes without a driver. In production.

    Servo7 n., wheeled machine. Servo7, Netherlands. Robot that unloads loose cartons from shipping containers, used by logistics firms including DHL and PostNL.

    Arms and workcells

    European Robotic Arm n., robot arm. Airbus Defence and Space, Netherlands, 2021. Robotic arm for the Russian segment of the International Space Station, launched attached to the Nauka module in 2021. In service.

    Lely Astronaut n., workcell. Lely, Netherlands, 1992. Milking robot from the Dutch firm Lely, in which cows walk in to be milked whenever they choose with no farmer present. In production.

    Medical and surgical

    SOFIE n., medical robot. Eindhoven University of Technology, Netherlands. Surgical robot developed at Eindhoven University of Technology as part of Linda van den Bedem's doctoral thesis, the first such robot to give the surgeon force feedback. Research platform.

    Underwater and marine

    Roboat n., marine robot. Amsterdam Institute for Advanced Metropolitan Solutions, Netherlands, 2016. Autonomous research boats developed by MIT, Delft University of Technology and Wageningen University for moving people and goods on Amsterdam's canals, where prototypes were tested. Research platform.

    Other forms

    Phobot n., robot. University of Amsterdam, Netherlands, 2008. Robot made from Lego by students at the University of Amsterdam that mimics being afraid and conquering fear step by step, intended to help children face phobias.
