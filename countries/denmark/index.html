    Robots of Denmark

    17 entries built in Denmark, grouped by section.

    Wheeled machines

    MiR250 n., wheeled machine. Mobile Industrial Robots, Denmark, 2019. Low profile 250 kg autonomous mobile robot for factories and hospitals, built to slide under carts and shelves or carry custom top modules. In production.

    Robotti n., wheeled machine. AGROINTELLI, Denmark. Self propelled implement carrier that straddles crop beds and pulls seed drills, hoes and sprayers autonomously along pre planned tracks on European farms. In production.

    TinyLineMarker Pro n., wheeled machine. TinyMobileRobots, Denmark. Battery electric marking robot that sprays sports field lines and construction layout marks to satellite coordinates loaded from a drawing. In production.

    UVD Robot n., wheeled machine. Blue Ocean Robotics, Denmark, 2018. Mobile ultraviolet disinfection robot that drives itself around hospital rooms and exposes surfaces to short wavelength light after manual cleaning. In production.

    Arms and workcells

    Reshape n., workcell. Reshape Biotech, Denmark. Single purpose lab robots that fill plates and photograph cultures for microbiologists.

    UR10e n., robot arm. Universal Robots, Denmark, 2018. Long reach e-Series arm from Universal Robots used for palletising, packaging and machine tending. In production.

    UR16e n., robot arm. Universal Robots, Denmark, 2019. Heavy payload member of the Universal Robots e-Series, built for loading machines and carrying heavy tools. In production.

    UR20 n., robot arm. Universal Robots, Denmark, 2022. Redesigned collaborative arm from Universal Robots with new joints, aimed at palletising and welding. In production.

    UR30 n., robot arm. Universal Robots, Denmark, 2023. Compact high payload arm from Universal Robots that shares the joint design of the UR20. In production.

    UR3e n., robot arm. Universal Robots, Denmark, 2018. Smallest arm of the Universal Robots e-Series, sized for benchtop assembly and screwdriving. In production.

    UR5 n., robot arm. Universal Robots, Denmark, 2008. Six axis arm from Universal Robots that was among the first robots sold to work beside people without a safety cage. Discontinued.

    UR5e n., robot arm. Universal Robots, Denmark, 2018. Medium sized e-Series arm from Universal Robots with a built in force and torque sensor at the tool flange. In production.

    Other forms

    LEGO Boost n., robot kit. The LEGO Group, Denmark, 2017. Building and coding set from LEGO with a motorised hub and a colour and distance sensor, used to make five starter robots. Discontinued.

    LEGO Education SPIKE Prime n., robot kit. The LEGO Group, Denmark, 2020. Classroom robotics set from LEGO Education built around a programmable hub with six ports for motors and sensors. In production.

    Lego Mindstorms EV3 n., robot kit. The Lego Group, Denmark, 2013. Programmable robotics kit from the Lego Group, the third generation of the Mindstorms line, announced at CES in January 2013 and succeeding the NXT.

    Lego Mindstorms NXT n., robot kit. The Lego Group, Denmark, 2006. Robot building and programming kit from Lego, launched in August 2006 as the second generation of Mindstorms after the Robotics Invention System.

    LEGO Mindstorms RCX n., robot kit. The LEGO Group, Denmark, 1998. First LEGO Mindstorms programmable brick, which brought robot building with motors and sensors to children and hobbyists. Discontinued.
