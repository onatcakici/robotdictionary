    Wheeled machines

    Tally

    n., wheeled machine. Simbe, United States, 2015. In production.

    Slim retail robot that patrols store aisles photographing shelves to find out of stock items, misplaced products and wrong price labels.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSimbe
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2015
    StatusIn production
    Runtime12 h
    ActuationSlim wheeled base with a vertical camera tower and turn in place steering
    PowerRechargeable battery with a base station
    SensorsMore than a dozen high resolution cameras with depth sensing and optional radio frequency identification
    AutonomyMap based aisle navigation with path planning and obstacle avoidance among shoppers

    Tally is a narrow wheeled tower carrying more than a dozen high resolution cameras that read shelf labels and gauge stock depth as it passes. It drives well below walking pace on a stored store map, turning in place in tight aisles and working during trading hours or overnight. Results go to store staff as pick lists of gaps and pricing errors, and an option adds radio frequency identification reading. Simbe was founded in 2014 and Tally runs in around a thousand stores.

    Of note

    • Reads roughly 15,000 to 30,000 shelf barcodes an hour
    • Schnucks Markets became the first retail customer in 2017
    • In use in about 1,000 stores including BJ's Wholesale Club locations
