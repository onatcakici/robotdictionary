    Quadrupeds

    BigDog

    n., quadruped. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2005. Retired.

    The gasoline engined pack mule whose recovery from a kick announced the legged robot age.

    Measures and facts
    MakerBoston Dynamics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2005
    StatusRetired
    Payloadapprox. 150 kg
    Paceapprox. 6.4 km/h
    Enginetwo stroke gasoline
    Height76 cm
    Mass109 kg

    Funded by DARPA as a robotic pack mule, BigDog carried 150 kilograms across mud and snow behind a two stroke engine's scream. The 2008 video of it recovering from a kick introduced the world to dynamic legged balance.
