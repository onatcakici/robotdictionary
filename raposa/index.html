    Wheeled machines

    RAPOSA

    n., wheeled machine. Portugal.

    Portuguese remotely operated search and rescue robot built to work in dangerous outdoor areas such as the rubble of collapsed structures and detect victims with its sensors.

    Measures and facts
    CountryPortugal

    Of note

    • Measures 75 cm long, 37 cm wide and 17.5 cm high, weighing 27 kg
    • Sensor options include a thermal camera, explosive and toxic gas sensors and inclinometers
    • Currently fitted for searching only, not for rescue
