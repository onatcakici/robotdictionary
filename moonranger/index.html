    Rovers

    MoonRanger

    n., rover. Carnegie Mellon University and Astrobotic, United States. Prototype.

    Suitcase sized autonomous lunar rover built to scout hundreds of metres from its lander without waiting for driving commands.

    Measures and facts
    MakerCarnegie Mellon University and Astrobotic
    CountryUnited States
    StatusPrototype
    ActuationFour wheel drive with independent motors
    PowerFlip up solar panel with battery
    SensorsStereo cameras and neutron spectrometer
    AutonomyFully autonomous waypoint navigation with onboard mapping

    MoonRanger is designed to drive itself between distant waypoints, building terrain maps onboard and returning to its lander to relay data, because it has no direct radio link to Earth. It is solar powered through a flip up panel and carries a neutron spectrometer to look for hydrogen near the lunar south pole. The rover was built at Carnegie Mellon, is in systems testing in a clean room there, and is manifested on a Firefly Aerospace Blue Ghost mission in 2029.

    Of note

    • Built to navigate autonomously beyond radio contact with its lander
    • Scheduled to fly on a Blue Ghost lander in 2029
