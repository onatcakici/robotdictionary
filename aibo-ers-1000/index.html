    Quadrupeds

    aibo ERS-1000

    n., quadruped. Sony, Japan, 2018. Discontinued.

    Sony's revival of its robot dog, a twenty two axis home companion with camera based recognition and cloud connected behaviour.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSony
    CountryJapan
    First shown2018
    StatusDiscontinued
    Mass2.2 kg
    Degrees of freedom22
    ActuationCompact electric servo actuators
    PowerRechargeable lithium ion battery with charging station
    SensorsFront and rear cameras, time of flight sensor, four microphones, touch sensors
    AutonomyOnboard and cloud behaviour learning with mapping and face recognition

    The ERS-1000 walks on four legs driven by compact actuators, has organic light emitting diode eyes and uses front and rear cameras for mapping, face recognition and localisation. Behaviour learning runs partly in the cloud through a subscription service, so each unit develops differently depending on how its owner treats it. Sony launched it in Japan in January 2018 and later in the United States, and announced in June 2026 that sales would end once stock ran out while support for owners continued.

    Of note

    • Twenty two axes of movement, more than any earlier AIBO
    • Sold in Japan from January 2018 and withdrawn from sale in 2026 with owner support continuing
