    Humanoids

    Walker S

    n., humanoid. UBTech, China, 2024. In pilot deployments.

    An industrial humanoid piloted on automotive lines for quality inspection and assembly; deployed in fleets.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUBTech
    CountryChina
    First shown2024
    StatusIn pilot deployments

    UBTech's Walker S series moved the Shenzhen company from consumer humanoids to factory fleets, with pilots at NIO, BYD, Zeekr and Foxconn plants performing inspection and logistics tasks. UBTech reports the largest orderbook of any Chinese humanoid maker.
