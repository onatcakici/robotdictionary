    Underwater and marine

    Nereus

    n., underwater robot. Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, 2009.

    Hybrid uncrewed underwater vehicle from the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, made for research at full ocean depth, which reached the floor of the Challenger Deep in 2009.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWoods Hole Oceanographic Institution
    First shown2009

    Of note

    • Rated to operate as deep as 11,000 metres
    • Reached the bottom of the Challenger Deep on 31 May 2009
    • Name chosen through a nationwide student contest
