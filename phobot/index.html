    Phobot

    n., robot. University of Amsterdam, Netherlands, 2008.

    Robot made from Lego by students at the University of Amsterdam that mimics being afraid and conquering fear step by step, intended to help children face phobias.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of Amsterdam
    CountryNetherlands
    First shown2008

    Of note

    • Voted public favourite at a human robot interaction conference in Amsterdam
