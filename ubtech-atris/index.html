    Wheeled machines

    ATRIS

    n., wheeled machine. UBTECH Robotics, China. In service.

    Four wheeled outdoor patrol robot used for security rounds at Chinese industrial parks and substations, carrying cameras, thermal imaging and lidar.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUBTECH Robotics
    CountryChina
    StatusIn service
    ActuationFour wheeled electric drive chassis with a raised sensor mast
    PowerBattery electric with autonomous recharging
    SensorsLidar, panoramic cameras and thermal imaging
    AutonomyAutonomous patrol along mapped routes with remote operator handoff

    ATRIS is a heavy four wheeled outdoor patrol robot with a raised sensor head, built for perimeter and facility rounds in industrial settings. It drives mapped patrol routes autonomously, streaming panoramic and thermal video to a control room and flagging intruders, hotspots and anomalies. Operators can take manual control and speak through the robot. It is deployed at Chinese industrial parks, electrical substations and campuses, and a variant was used for temperature screening patrols during the COVID-19 pandemic.

    Of note

    • Used for outdoor security patrols at Chinese industrial parks and electrical substations
    • A pandemic variant carried thermal screening and public address equipment
    • Built by UBTECH Robotics of Shenzhen, which also produces humanoid and education robots
