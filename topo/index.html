    Wheeled machines

    Topo

    n., wheeled machine. Nolan Bushnell. Discontinued.

    Series of personal robots produced by Androbot in the 1980s for homes and schools, programmed wirelessly from an Apple II computer.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNolan Bushnell
    StatusDiscontinued

    Of note

    • Programmed in TopoBASIC, an adapted form of BASIC
    • Had no sensors and could not send information back to the computer
