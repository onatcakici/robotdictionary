    Int-Ball

    Also known as JEM Internal Ball Camera

    n., robot. Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency and University of Tokyo, Japan, 2016. In service.

    Ball shaped camera robot from the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency and the University of Tokyo that flies inside the ISS Kibō module, filming tasks to save crew time.

    Measures and facts
    MakerJapan Aerospace Exploration Agency and University of Tokyo
    CountryJapan
    First shown2016
    StatusIn service
    Mass1 kg

    Of note

    • Two different Int-Ball units had been delivered to the station by May 2024
    • Has a mass of about 1 kg
