    Rovers

    LAMA

    n., rover. Mobile Vehicle Engineering Institute and Laboratory for Analysis and Architecture of Systems, Russia, 1990. Prototype.

    French autonomous Mars rover prototype from 1990, developed by the Mobile Vehicle Engineering Institute and the Laboratory for Analysis and Architecture of Systems and weighing 300 kg.
