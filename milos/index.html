    Wheeled machines

    Miloš

    n., wheeled machine. Military Technical Institute Belgrade and Prva Petoletka, Serbia. In production.

    Serbian unmanned ground vehicle developed by the Military Technical Institute after its earlier Milica vehicle of 2009 and built in series for the Serbian Armed Forces.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMilitary Technical Institute Belgrade and Prva Petoletka
    CountrySerbia
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Also known as Little Miloš
    • In serial production, with the Serbian Armed Forces as first customer
