    Robots from Y Combinator companies

    73 robots built by companies that went through Y Combinator, newest batch first. Company facts come from the public YC company directory; Robot Dictionary is not affiliated with Y Combinator.

    Winter 2027

    Nuum n., robot. Nuum Robotics, Canada. Snake like robot that travels inside machinery to inspect and maintain it.

    Fall 2026

    Odin n., robot arm. Odin Robotics, United States. Robot arms placed in small and mid sized factories for machine tending and packing, working beside existing machines and people.

    Rodeo n., workcell. Rodeo Robotics, United States. Robotic technician that performs oil changes at fleet depots.

    Summer 2026

    Cosmic-1 n., wheeled machine. Cosmic Robotics, United States. Autonomous heavy lift robot from Cosmic Robotics that installs solar panels; the company also works on lunar construction under a NASA contract.

    Edgerun n., robot. Edgerun, United States. Powered exoskeleton developed for soldiers.

    Grip n., workcell. Grip, Switzerland. Picking robot that removes contaminants from organic waste streams, learning from every successful and failed grasp.

    Manifold n., robot. Manifold, United States. Drop in robotic labor from a 2026 Y Combinator company.

    Neuromorphic n., wheeled machine. Neuromorphic, United States. Mobile robot that moves between wet lab benches and operates existing scientific instruments like a technician.

    Nori n., humanoid. Nori, United States. American made humanoid announced at a price of $1,688.

    OS3 n., humanoid. OS3, United States. Low cost mobile humanoid designed and built in the United States by a two person team.

    Proprio n., robot. Proprio Robotics, United States. Robots for building out, maintaining and decommissioning data centers.

    Spring 2026

    Aseon Pod n., workcell. Aseon Labs, United States. Robotic micro depot that charges, cleans and services self driving cars between trips.

    Eden n., humanoid. Eden Robotics, United States. Semi humanoid robot for manufacturing and logistics, rented by usage rather than sold.

    InLoop n., workcell. InLoop Robotics, Germany. Robot arms that pick and pack online orders, with remote operators stepping in when a grasp fails.

    Synphony n., wheeled machine. Synphony, United States. Strawberry harvesting robot for California farms, paired with field analytics.

    Winter 2026

    Origami hand n., robot hand. Origami Robotics, United States. High degree of freedom direct drive robotic hand from Origami Robotics, matched to a glove like device that records human demonstrations.

    Remy n., workcell. Remy AI, United States. Robots and learned models that automate dexterous picking in online retail warehouses.

    RoboDock n., workcell. RoboDock, United States. Robots that plug in and check electric and autonomous vehicles at fleet depots.

    Servo7 n., wheeled machine. Servo7, Netherlands. Robot that unloads loose cartons from shipping containers, used by logistics firms including DHL and PostNL.

    Fall 2025

    Darwin n., robot. Darwin, United States. Robot technician being built to assemble, commission and maintain data center hardware, with first deployments targeted for 2027.

    Piggy n., humanoid. Piggy Robotics, United Kingdom. Low cost household humanoid from London driven by artificial muscles made of braided tubes rather than electric motors.

    Summer 2025

    Flywheel n., wheeled machine. Flywheel AI, United States. Kit that converts existing excavators for remote operation and gathers the data used to train autonomous digging.

    Spring 2025

    Boost n., wheeled machine. Boost Robotics, United States. Autonomous mobile manipulator that inspects and services equipment in data centers.

    Vassar n., robot arm. Vassar Robotics, United States. Gearless robot joints and arms driven by high torque transverse flux motors, built for AI research labs.

    Winter 2025

    Pave n., wheeled machine. Pave Robotics, United States. Robot that finds and seals cracks in asphalt for paving contractors.

    Proception n., robot hand. Proception Inc, United States. Dexterous robotic hand built for humanoids, aiming at fine work such as threading a needle.

    Red Barn weeder n., wheeled machine. Red Barn Robotics, United States. Autonomous field robot from Red Barn Robotics that kills weeds while leaving crops untouched.

    Revise n., workcell. Revise Robotics, United States. Robotic system that tests, wipes and refurbishes used laptops for electronic waste recyclers.

    Fall 2024

    Maurice n., wheeled machine. Innate, United States. Small home robot from Innate with a single arm, designed to be taught new chores through code, language and demonstration.

    Summer 2024

    AutoPallet n., wheeled machine. AutoPallet Robotics, United States. Swarm of small mobile robots that move cardboard boxes and install into existing warehouses.

    Azalea n., workcell. Azalea Robotics Corporation, United States. Robots that load and sort checked luggage in airport baggage halls.

    Isaac n., humanoid. Weave Robotics, United States, 2024. A folding home humanoid sold on preorder as a household assistant. Announced, not yet in service.

    Ultra n., workcell. Ultra, United States. General purpose robot for repetitive industrial tasks such as packing, installed in hours without custom integration.

    Winter 2024

    Stompy n., humanoid. K-Scale Labs, United States, 2024. An open source humanoid released with plans and firmware for anyone to build. Research platform.

    Tuesday n., robot. Tuesday Labs, United States. Home robot from a New York startup built to pick up and tidy rooms.

    Summer 2023

    Andromeda n., medical robot. Andromeda Surgical, United States. Surgical robot developed to carry out parts of procedures autonomously rather than only extending a surgeon's hands.

    Summer 2022

    Hedgehog n., workcell. Hedgehog, United States. Robotic growing system that automates mushroom farms.

    Winter 2022

    Eureka n., marine robot. Impossible Metals, United States. Autonomous underwater vehicle from Impossible Metals that hovers above the seabed and uses a robotic arm and vision to pick polymetallic nodules one at a time.

    Fleetzero n., marine robot. Fleetzero, United States. Electric cargo vessels designed to run with remote supervision instead of a full crew.

    Shinkei n., workcell. Shinkei Systems, United States. Robot for fishing boats that uses computer vision to apply the Japanese ikejime method, which keeps fish fresher for longer.

    Summer 2021

    Charge Robotics n., wheeled machine. Charge Robotics, United States. Robots that automate the most repetitive work of building utility scale solar farms.

    Revolve n., medical robot. Revolve Surgical, Canada. Compact surgical robot designed to fit operating rooms outside hospitals, where many abdominal procedures take place.

    Winter 2021

    BotBuilt n., workcell. BotBuilt, United States. Robotic cells that assemble wall panels and roof trusses for house framing.

    Hermes Robotics n., wheeled machine. Hermes Robotics, United States. Retrofit autonomy for street sweepers, sold as a sweeping service.

    Mezli n., workcell. Mezli, United States. Fully automated restaurant built into a shipping container that cooked meals for takeout and delivery. Discontinued.

    Reshape n., workcell. Reshape Biotech, Denmark. Single purpose lab robots that fill plates and photograph cultures for microbiologists.

    Roin n., wheeled machine. Roin Technologies, United States. Robots that finish commercial concrete floors, with one person supervising the work of several crews; the company was acquired.

    Winter 2020

    Morphle n., medical robot. Morphle Labs, India. Robotic microscopes from Bengaluru that automate slide scanning for cancer diagnostics.

    Perceptive n., medical robot. Perceptive Technologies, United States. Robotic system that pairs a new dental imaging method with automated tooth cutting, such as preparing a tooth for a crown.

    RoboTire n., workcell. RoboTire, United States. Automated bay that removes and fits vehicle tires, aimed at fleet servicing. Discontinued.

    Somatic n., wheeled machine. SOMATIC, United States. Mobile robot that cleans restrooms in office buildings on its own.

    Teleo n., wheeled machine. Teleo, United States. Retrofit that turns dozers, trucks and loaders into supervised autonomous machines so one operator can run several from a control room; the company was acquired.

    Summer 2019

    Lavo Bot n., wheeled machine. Lucid Bots, United States. Ground robot from Lucid Bots that pressure washes surfaces, launched to complement the company's Sherpa cleaning drone.

    Yummy Future n., workcell. Yummy Future, United States. Robotic stations that take over repetitive preparation work in restaurants and food retail.

    Zeta Surgical n., medical robot. ZETA SURGICAL, United States. Image guidance and robotics platform that uses computer vision to register a patient in minutes for neurosurgical and transcranial magnetic stimulation procedures.

    Winter 2019

    Mobot n., workcell. Mobot, United States. Mechanical robots that tap and swipe real phones to test mobile apps where emulators fall short.

    Taobotics n., wheeled machine. Taobotics, China. Autonomous robots for retail stores from a company based in Shenzhen.

    Trexo n., exoskeleton. Trexo Robotics, Canada. Powered walking exoskeleton that lets children with conditions such as cerebral palsy take steps, often for the first time.

    Summer 2018

    GR-100 n., wheeled machine. Four Growers, United States. Greenhouse robot from Four Growers that picks ripe tomatoes and records crop data as it travels the heating rails between rows.

    Mytos n., workcell. Mytos, United Kingdom. Robotic platform that turns stem cells into specific cell types such as neurons and heart cells over several weeks.

    Winter 2018

    Aerones n., robot. Aerones, Latvia. Rope suspended robotic system that climbs wind turbine blades to clean, inspect, test lightning conductors and repair leading edges.

    Bear Flag autonomous tractor n., wheeled machine. Bear Flag Robotics, United States. Retrofit kit that turned existing farm tractors and implements into driverless machines; the company was acquired by Deere.

    Orangewood Labs n., robot arm. Orangewood Labs, United States. San Francisco maker of low cost robot arms and the software that lets them learn new tasks.

    Summer 2016

    Multiply Labs n., workcell. Multiply Labs, United States. Cloud controlled modular robotic system that manufactures individualized drugs, a process normally done by hand.

    Robby n., wheeled machine. Robby Technologies, United States. Self driving sidewalk robot for last mile delivery of food, groceries and parcels, built by a company founded by two MIT PhDs. Discontinued.

    Winter 2016

    Gecko Robotics inspection robots n., robot. Gecko Robotics, United States. Fleet of magnetic wall climbing and crawling robots that scan boilers, tanks, pipelines and ship hulls for wall thickness and corrosion.

    Opentrons Flex n., workcell. Opentrons, United States, 2023. Larger successor to the OT-2 launched in May 2023, adding a gripper that moves labware between deck positions.

    OT-2 n., workcell. Opentrons, United States. Benchtop pipetting robot from Opentrons with an open source protocol library, built to bring lab automation to ordinary biology labs.

    Summer 2015

    Bistrobot n., workcell. Bistrobot, United States. Automated sandwich line from a San Francisco startup that assembled orders while customers watched. Discontinued.

    teaBOT n., workcell. teaBOT, Canada. Robotic kiosk that blends and brews loose leaf tea to order, placed in offices and showrooms as a workplace amenity.

    Winter 2015

    Transcriptic n., workcell. Transcriptic, United States. Remote controlled robotic laboratory where researchers ran life science experiments over the internet. Discontinued.

    Summer 2013

    IXI-Play n., robot. Ixiplay, United States. Android based robot companion for young children that read picture cards, mimicked the emotions on them, and ran games from its own app store. Discontinued.

    Summer 2012

    Double 3 n., wheeled machine. Double Robotics, United States, 2019. The self balancing tablet on a stick that lets remote workers roam the office. In production.
