    Kaspar

    Also known as Kaspar the social robot

    n., humanoid. University of Hertfordshire, United Kingdom, 2005. Research platform.

    Humanoid social robot developed at the University of Hertfordshire to act as a companion for autistic children and others who struggle with communication.
