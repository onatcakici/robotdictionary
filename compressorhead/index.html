    Compressorhead

    n., animatronic figure. Germany, 2013.

    Robot rock cover band whose members are animatronic machines, created by Berlin based artist Frank Barnes and several collaborators.

    Measures and facts
    CountryGermany
    First shown2013

    Of note

    • Musical director, songwriter and vocalist is John, formerly of NoMeansNo
