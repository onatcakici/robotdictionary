    Wheeled machines

    Freight500

    n., wheeled machine. Fetch Robotics, United States, 2017. In production.

    Flat deck autonomous mobile robot that carries up to 500 kg of goods on pallets or roll cages through warehouse and factory aisles.

    Measures and facts
    MakerFetch Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2017
    StatusIn production
    Payload500 kg
    PowerRechargeable battery with autonomous charging
    SensorsSafety laser scanners and 3D depth cameras
    AutonomyLaser SLAM with cloud based fleet management and no floor markers

    The Freight500 is the mid size member of the Fetch Freight family, a low platform that drives under or receives a load and delivers it across a site. Safety laser scanners and 3D cameras support laser SLAM, and jobs are dispatched from cloud fleet software that also handles charging and traffic. The smaller Freight100 base from the same family became a common research platform in university robotics labs because it ships with ROS support. Fetch Robotics of San Jose was acquired by Zebra Technologies in 2021.

    Of note

    • Zebra Technologies acquired Fetch Robotics in 2021
    • The smaller Freight100 base is widely used in university labs with ROS
    • A larger Freight1500 handles pallet sized loads
