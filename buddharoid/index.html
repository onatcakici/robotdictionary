    Buddharoid

    n., humanoid. Kyoto University, Japan.

    Japanese robot monk created by Kyoto University researchers with technology company partners to give Buddhist spiritual guidance, unveiled at a temple in Kyoto.

    Measures and facts
    MakerKyoto University
    CountryJapan

    Of note

    • First presented at Shoren in Temple, Kyoto
