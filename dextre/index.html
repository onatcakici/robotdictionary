    Arms and workcells

    Dextre

    Also known as Special Purpose Dexterous Manipulator, SPDM, Special Purpose Dextrous Manipulator

    n., workcell. MDA Space, Canada, 2008. In service.

    Robotic telemanipulator with two arms on the International Space Station, built by MDA Space to handle repair work that would otherwise call for spacewalking astronauts.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMDA Space
    CountryCanada
    First shown2008
    StatusIn service
    Height3.67 m
    Mass1710 kg
    Width2.37 m

    Of note

    • Launched on 11 March 2008 on mission STS-123
    • Part of the station's Mobile Servicing System
    • Stands 3.67 m tall with a mass of 1710 kg
