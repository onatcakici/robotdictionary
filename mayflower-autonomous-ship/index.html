    Mayflower Autonomous Ship

    Also known as MAS400

    n., marine robot. ProMare and IBM, United Kingdom, 2020.

    Solar powered research trimaran that sails without a crew, steered by an onboard AI captain.

    Measures and facts
    MakerProMare and IBM
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    First shown2020

    Of note

    • Crossed the Atlantic from Plymouth and reached Halifax, Nova Scotia in June 2022
    • Named for the Mayflower voyage of 1620

    Sources

    1. Autonomous Mayflower reaches American shores, in Canada, Associated Press via News4JAX, news4jax.com
