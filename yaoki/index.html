    Rovers

    Yaoki

    n., rover. Dymon, Japan, 2025. Lost.

    Palm sized Japanese commercial rover with two wide wheels that can right itself, flown on the IM-2 mission but never deployed.

    Measures and facts
    MakerDymon
    CountryJapan
    First shown2025
    StatusLost
    Mass0.498 kg
    ActuationTwo wide wheels on a self righting body
    PowerOnboard battery
    SensorsForward and rear cameras
    AutonomyTeleoperated with short command loops
    Height10 cm
    Length15 cm
    Width15 cm

    Yaoki weighs under half a kilogram and is built around two oversized wheels on a rigid body, so it keeps driving whichever way up it lands. Dymon developed it as a low cost commercial scout for lunar caves and shadowed terrain, controlled from Earth through a lander relay. It flew on the Intuitive Machines IM-2 mission in early 2025, but the Athena lander came to rest on its side in a shadowed crater and none of its rovers could be released.

    Of note

    • Named for a Japanese saying about rising after every fall
    • Undeployed after the IM-2 lander toppled in March 2025
