    I-Bot

    Also known as Microbric I-Bot

    n., robot kit. Microbric, Australia, 2005.

    Small robot from Microbric, given out in kit form by the Adelaide Advertiser newspaper in 2005 to introduce readers to electronics and robotics.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMicrobric
    CountryAustralia
    First shown2005

    Of note

    • Distributed through the Adelaide Advertiser in South Australia in November and December 2005
