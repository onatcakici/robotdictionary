    Curiosity

    n., rover. NASA JPL, United States, 2012. In production.

    The car sized nuclear laboratory still climbing Mount Sharp after a decade.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNASA JPL
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2012
    StatusIn production
    Massapprox. 899 kg
    Distanceover 30 km
    Powerradioisotope generator
    Height2.1 m
    Length3 m
    Width2.8 m

    Curiosity's sky crane landing in 2012 was the most audacious arrival in spaceflight, and the car sized laboratory has climbed Mount Sharp ever since, proving ancient Mars was habitable.
