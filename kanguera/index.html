    Kanguera

    n., robot hand. University of São Paulo, Brazil, 2007. Research platform.

    Research robot hand from the University of São Paulo that models the kinematics of the human hand to help develop better humanlike grippers and manipulators.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of São Paulo
    CountryBrazil
    First shown2007
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Runs the VxWorks operating system
    • Its name is an ancient indigenous word meaning bones outside the body
