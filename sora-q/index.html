    Rovers

    SORA-Q

    Also known as LEV-2, Lunar Excursion Vehicle 2

    n., rover. JAXA with Tomy, Sony and Doshisha University, Japan, 2023. Mission completed.

    Transformable ball shaped lunar robot the size of a baseball that photographed the tipped over SLIM lander in January 2024.

    Measures and facts
    MakerJAXA with Tomy, Sony and Doshisha University
    CountryJapan
    First shown2023
    StatusMission completed
    Mass0.25 kg
    ActuationTwo hemispherical shell wheels with a stabilising tail and two crawling gaits
    PowerNon rechargeable onboard battery
    SensorsTwo cameras front and rear
    AutonomyAutonomous imaging sequence after release with no live control

    SORA-Q travelled to the Moon as a sphere about eight centimetres across, then split its shell into two halves that act as wheels while a tail keeps it stable. Released by the SLIM lander during its descent on 19 January 2024, it crawled on the regolith using two gaits and photographed the lander, which had come to rest nose down. Images were relayed to Earth through its companion hopper LEV-1, and the pair worked on primary battery power alone.

    Of note

    • Among the smallest and lightest rovers ever landed on the Moon
    • Took the image showing SLIM resting nose down
    • Data relayed to Earth by the companion LEV-1 hopper
