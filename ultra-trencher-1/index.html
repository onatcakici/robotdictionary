    Underwater and marine

    Ultra Trencher 1

    n., marine robot.

    Submersible trenching robot that buries undersea pipelines by cutting trenches in the seabed, described as the largest submersible robot in the world.

    Measures and facts

    Of note

    • Mass of 60 tons; 7.8 m long, 7.8 m wide and 5.6 m tall
    • Trenches pipelines up to 1 m in diameter at depths of up to 1,500 m
    • Cuts trenches with a pair of jet swords that force pressurised water into sediment
