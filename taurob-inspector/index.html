    Taurob Inspector

    n., wheeled machine. Taurob GmbH, Austria.

    Mobile robot certified under ATEX rules, made by the Austrian firm Taurob for offshore oil and gas sites and derived from its Taurob Tracker.

    Measures and facts
    MakerTaurob GmbH
    CountryAustria

    Of note

    • Holds ATEX certification
    • Grew out of the Taurob Tracker during the OGRIP project
