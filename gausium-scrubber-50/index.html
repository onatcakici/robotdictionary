    Scrubber 50

    n., wheeled machine. Gausium, China, 2020. In production.

    Commercial scrubbing robot the size of a walk behind machine that cleans roughly 500 to 1,300 square metres an hour without a driver.

    Measures and facts
    MakerGausium
    CountryChina
    First shown2020
    StatusIn production
    Height1.07 m
    Length0.81 m
    Mass157 kg
    Top speed1.2 m/s (4.3 km/h)
    Runtime3 h
    Power24 V 60 Ah lithium iron phosphate battery with a two hour charge
    Sensors2D lidar, 3D depth camera, colour camera and collision sensors
    AutonomyMaps the site and repeats cleaning plans with obstacle avoidance

    The Scrubber 50 is a compact autonomous scrubber with a 30 litre clean water tank and a 24 litre recovery tank, offered with disc or roller brush decks. A 2D lidar, 3D depth camera, colour camera and collision sensors let it map a site and repeat cleaning plans while avoiding people and obstacles. It runs about three hours scrubbing or six to eight hours dust mopping between two hour charges. Gausium, founded in Shanghai in 2013, sells it for malls, offices, transport hubs and hospitals.

    Of note

    • Clean water tank holds 30 litres and the recovery tank 24 litres
    • Practical cleaning rate of about 500 to 1,300 square metres an hour
    • Gausium was founded in Shanghai in 2013
