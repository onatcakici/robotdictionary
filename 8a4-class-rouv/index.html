    Underwater and marine

    8A4 class ROUV

    n., underwater robot. Shenyang Institute of Automation, China.

    Remotely operated underwater vehicle of the work class type, developed by China's Shenyang Institute of Automation for jobs ranging from oil platform servicing to salvage and rescue.

    Measures and facts
    MakerShenyang Institute of Automation
    CountryChina

    Of note

    • Improved from the American AMETEK 2006 design
    • Preceded by the RECON-IV and followed by the improved 7B8
