    Quadrupeds

    Max

    Also known as Tencent Max

    n., quadruped. Tencent Robotics X, China, 2021. Research platform.

    Wheel legged quadruped from Tencent's robotics laboratory that switches between walking on four legs and rolling on wheels.

    Measures and facts
    MakerTencent Robotics X
    CountryChina
    First shown2021
    StatusResearch platform
    Top speed6.9 m/s (24.8 km/h)
    ActuationElectric joint actuators with a driven wheel on each leg
    PowerOnboard battery
    AutonomyOnboard hybrid locomotion control with fall recovery

    Max carries a wheel at the end of each leg, so it can walk and jump over obstacles like a legged robot and then glide on flat ground at up to twenty five kilometres per hour. Tencent Robotics X built it as a research platform for hybrid locomotion control, showing backflips, standing up on two wheels and recovery after falls with control decisions computed in well under a millisecond. It was announced in March 2021 as the laboratory's first self developed quadruped, aimed at patrol, security and rescue research.

    Of note

    • Combines legged and wheeled locomotion in one machine, switching modes on the move
    • Announced in March 2021 as Tencent Robotics X's first self developed quadruped
