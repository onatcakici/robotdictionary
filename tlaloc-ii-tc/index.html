    Tlaloc II-TC

    n., robot.

    Robot built to explore the tunnel found in 2003 beneath the Temple of the Feathered Serpent at Teotihuacan, the ancient site in central Mexico.

    Measures and facts

    Of note

    • Also known as the Tlaloc II Robot
    • Used at Teotihuacan, a site founded in the second century BC
