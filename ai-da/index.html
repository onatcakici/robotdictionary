    Humanoids

    Ai-Da

    Also known as Aida, Ai da

    n., humanoid. Engineered Arts, United Kingdom, 2019. In service.

    Humanoid robot artist, completed in 2019 by Engineered Arts, that produces drawings, paintings and sculptures.

    Measures and facts
    MakerEngineered Arts
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    First shown2019
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • Named after Ada Lovelace
    • Draws people in pencil using a bionic hand and cameras in its eyes
