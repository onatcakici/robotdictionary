    Wheeled machines

    PROWLER

    Also known as R

    n., wheeled machine. Prototype.

    Experimental armed robot from the mid 1980s that acted as a mobile sentry gun, built in the United States.

    Measures and facts
    StatusPrototype

    Of note

    • Name stood for Programmable Robot Observer With Logical Enemy Response
