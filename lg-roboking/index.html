    Wheeled machines

    LG Roboking

    Also known as Hom-Bot

    n., wheeled machine. LG, 2001. In production.

    Line of robotic vacuum cleaners made by LG, sold as Roboking in Korea and under the Hom-Bot name in other markets.

    Measures and facts
    MakerLG
    First shown2001
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • The first version was launched in 2001
