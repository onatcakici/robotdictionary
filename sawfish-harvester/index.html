    Underwater and marine

    Sawfish harvester

    Also known as Sawfish

    n., underwater robot. Triton Logging Inc..

    Submersible logging robot made by Triton Logging that uses large pincers and a chainsaw to fell trees flooded by dams and float them to the surface.

    Measures and facts
    MakerTriton Logging Inc.

    Of note

    • Targets forests flooded when dams are built nearby
    • Submerged wood stays usable for long periods
