    Rovers

    Rashid

    Also known as Emirates Lunar Mission rover

    n., rover. Mohammed bin Rashid Space Centre, United Arab Emirates, 2022. Lost.

    Emirati micro rover built to study lunar dust and plasma, lost when the ispace Hakuto-R lander crashed in April 2023.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMohammed bin Rashid Space Centre
    CountryUnited Arab Emirates
    First shown2022
    StatusLost
    Mass10 kg
    ActuationFour wheel drive with material test patches on the wheels
    PowerSolar panels with battery
    SensorsTwo high resolution cameras, microscopic camera, thermal imager, Langmuir probe
    AutonomyTeleoperated from Dubai through the lander relay

    Rashid was the first lunar rover designed and built in the Arab world, assembled at the Mohammed bin Rashid Space Centre in Dubai. It carried two high resolution cameras, a microscopic camera, a thermal imager and a Langmuir probe to study regolith properties, dust adhesion and the plasma environment, with test patches of different materials on its wheels. Communication with the Hakuto-R Mission 1 lander was lost seconds before touchdown on 25 April 2023 and the spacecraft crashed near Atlas crater.

    Of note

    • First lunar rover designed and built in the Arab world
    • Lost in the Hakuto-R Mission 1 landing failure
