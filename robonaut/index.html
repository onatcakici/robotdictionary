    Humanoids

    Robonaut

    n., humanoid. Lyndon B. Johnson Space Center, United States. In development.

    NASA humanoid robot from the Dexterous Robotics Laboratory at Johnson Space Center in Houston, built for space jobs that need nimble handling rather than heavy lifting.

    Measures and facts
    MakerLyndon B. Johnson Space Center
    CountryUnited States
    StatusIn development

    Of note

    • Developed at NASA's Johnson Space Center in Houston, Texas
    • Built for tasks needing more dexterity than space robots that move large objects
