    Quadrupeds

    Solo 12

    Also known as SOLO12

    n., quadruped. Open Dynamic Robot Initiative, Germany, 2020. Research platform.

    Open hardware torque controlled quadruped weighing a few kilograms, designed so any laboratory could build one from printed parts.

    Measures and facts
    MakerOpen Dynamic Robot Initiative
    CountryGermany
    First shown2020
    StatusResearch platform
    Degrees of freedom12
    ActuationTwelve back drivable brushless actuators with low gear ratios
    PowerOnboard lithium polymer battery
    SensorsJoint encoders, inertial measurement unit
    AutonomyOff board torque control over a low latency link

    Solo 12 came from the Open Dynamic Robot Initiative, a collaboration of the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, New York University and LAAS-CNRS, which published complete plans for a quadruped made from three dimensional printed parts and off the shelf components. Twelve back drivable brushless actuators give torque control at every joint, so it supports whole body control and learning research usually reserved for expensive hardware. PAL Robotics later assembled and sold ready made units, and the design has been rebuilt in laboratories worldwide.

    Of note

    • Complete hardware design released as open source so laboratories can build their own
    • Developed jointly by the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, New York University and LAAS-CNRS
