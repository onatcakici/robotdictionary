    Arms and workcells

    Vulcan

    Also known as Amazon Vulcan

    n., robot arm. Amazon Robotics, United States, 2025. In service.

    Warehouse picking robot from Amazon with force sensing grippers, described by the company as its first robot with a sense of touch.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAmazon Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2025
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • Stows and picks items in the fabric pods of Amazon fulfilment centres
    • Shown in May 2025, working in Spokane and Hamburg

    Sources

    1. Amazon touts Vulcan, its first robot with a sense of touch, The Register, theregister.com
    2. Amazon's newest fulfillment robot has a sense of touch, Engadget, engadget.com
