    Wheeled machines

    Oz

    n., wheeled machine. Naio Technologies, France, 2015. In production.

    Small electric tool carrier that weeds, sows and marks rows in market garden beds, driving itself between crop rows using satellite guidance.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNaio Technologies
    CountryFrance
    First shown2015
    StatusIn production
    Payload60 kg
    Runtime8 h
    ActuationFour wheeled electric drive tool carrier with a maximum working width of one metre
    PowerLithium battery giving up to eight hours of work
    AutonomyAutonomous row work guided by corrected satellite positioning, with obstacle detection and emergency stop

    Oz is the smallest of Naio's field robots, a compact electric platform that straddles or runs between narrow vegetable beds. It follows rows autonomously using corrected satellite positioning and carries a wide catalogue of interchangeable tools for weeding, sowing and row marking. It can also tow a small trailer or crates around a holding. Market gardeners and specialty crop growers in France and neighbouring countries use it on small intensive plots.

    Of note

    • More than 35 compatible implements are offered for weeding, sowing and row marking
    • Naio also builds the larger Dino for vegetable beds and Ted for vineyards
    • Aimed at market gardening and aromatic plant growers working small plots
