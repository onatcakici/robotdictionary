Lunar Roving Vehicle

Also known as LRV, Moon buggy

Battery powered four wheeled car driven by Apollo astronauts on the Moon, extending each crew's exploration range far beyond walking distance.

MakerBoeing with General Motors Delco for NASA
CountryUnited States
Launched1971
StatusCompleted
Length3.1 m
Mass210 kg
Payload490 kg
Top speed3.6 m/s (13 km/h)
ActuationFour wire mesh wheels with individual electric motors and front and rear steering
PowerTwo 36 volt silver zinc batteries
SensorsSun compass, odometer and gyroscope driven navigation display

Three Lunar Roving Vehicles were folded into the descent stages of Apollo 15, 16 and 17 and unfolded by hand on the surface. Each had four wire mesh wheels with individual electric motors, two silver zinc batteries, a T handle controller and a navigation system that dead reckoned the route back to the lander. The Apollo 17 vehicle covered roughly 36 kilometres, the longest lunar traverse, and all three remain parked where the crews left them.

Of note

  • Let Apollo crews work several kilometres from the lander
  • Apollo 17 crew drove about 36 km, the longest lunar traverse
  • All three vehicles remain on the Moon
