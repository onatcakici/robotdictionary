    Arms and workcells

    LBR iiwa

    n., robot arm. KUKA, Germany, 2013. In production.

    Seven axis lightweight arm from KUKA with torque sensors in every joint, developed from DLR research arms.

    Measures and facts
    MakerKUKA
    CountryGermany
    First shown2013
    StatusIn production
    Axes7
    Payload7 kg or 14 kg

    Of note

    • Name stands for intelligent industrial work assistant
