    Peacock Clock

    n., automaton. James Cox, 1772.

    Large eighteenth century automaton clock with three mechanical birds of full life size, created by the businessman James Cox.

    Measures and facts
    MakerJames Cox
    First shown1772

    Of note

    • Entered Catherine the Great's collection in 1781 with help from Grigory Potemkin
    • Exhibited at the Hermitage Museum in Saint Petersburg
    • Shown every day on a Russian television channel
