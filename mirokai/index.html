    Humanoids

    Mirokai

    n., humanoid. Enchanted Tools, France, 2023. Prototype.

    Character styled service robot that balances on a single ball, trialled in Paris hospitals for carrying samples, linens and small deliveries.

    Measures and facts
    MakerEnchanted Tools
    CountryFrance
    First shown2023
    StatusPrototype
    ActuationBall balancing single sphere drive with two electric arms
    SensorsDepth cameras, lidar, microphone array, animated face display
    AutonomyAutonomous indoor navigation with remote operator assistance

    Mirokai is a humanoid upper body with two arms and an animated face that rides on a ball balancing drive rather than legs or a wheeled chassis, letting it pivot in place in narrow corridors. Enchanted Tools designed it deliberately as a fictional character, with a puppet inspired head and voice, on the argument that people accept a stylised robot more readily than an imitation human. It carries trays and containers, driven by a mix of autonomous navigation and remote operator support. The company has run trials in Paris hospitals and shown the robot at CES, but it is not yet a volume product.

    Of note

    • Trialled in Paris hospitals for internal transport of samples and supplies
    • Ball balancing drive lets it turn on the spot in narrow corridors
    • Designed as a stylised fictional character rather than a human likeness
