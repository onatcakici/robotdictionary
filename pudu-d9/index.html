    PUDU D9

    n., humanoid. Pudu Robotics, China, 2025. In production.

    Full size biped from a large commercial service robot maker, sold for object handling and customer engagement in hospitality and retail settings.

    Measures and facts

    CountryChina
    First shown2025
    StatusIn production
    Height1.7 m
    Mass65 kg

    Top speed2 m/s (7.2 km/h)
    Degrees of freedom42

    AutonomyOnboard compute rated at 275 trillion operations per second

    D9 is 170 centimetres tall, weighs 65 kilograms and has 42 degrees of freedom with six per leg and seven per arm, walking at up to 2 metres per second and carrying 20 kilograms. Its DH11 hands have eleven degrees of freedom and more than a thousand tactile sensing pixels across twelve regions, with 30 newtons of grip force. Onboard compute is rated at 275 trillion operations per second, powered by a 0.72 kilowatt hour pack, and maximum joint torque is 352 newton metres. Pudu already ships large numbers of wheeled delivery and cleaning robots to restaurants and hotels, and positions D9 as the bipedal addition to that commercial fleet.

    Of note

    • Announced in 2025 as Pudu's first full size bipedal humanoid
    • DH11 hands carry more than 1,000 tactile sensing pixels
    • Sold into a commercial channel already serving restaurants and hotels with wheeled robots
