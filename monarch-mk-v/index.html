    Monarch MK-V

    n., wheeled machine. Monarch Tractor, United States, 2020. In production.

    Battery electric compact utility tractor with driver optional autonomy, sold for vineyards, orchards and dairies, with cameras for row following and safety.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMonarch Tractor
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2020
    StatusIn production
    Runtime14 h
    ActuationFour wheel drive battery electric tractor with a category one and two three point hitch
    PowerBattery electric with 5.6 kilowatts of exportable power
    Sensors360 degree camera coverage with collision prevention and power take off protection
    AutonomyDriver optional operation with row following and gesture control; one operator can supervise up to eight tractors

    The MK-V is a battery electric utility tractor sized for vineyard and orchard rows, rated at about 40 horsepower continuous with a 70 horsepower peak. It can be driven conventionally, driven by gesture and remote control, or left to run recorded rows on its own, with a camera ring watching for people and obstacles. Exportable electrical power lets it run electric implements. Farms use it for mowing, spraying, towing and hauling in California and elsewhere.

    Of note

    • Rated 40 horsepower continuous and 70 horsepower peak, with a 14 hour working day on a charge
    • Assembled in Livermore, California, with a manufacturing partnership with Foxconn in Ohio
    • Widely used in California wine grape vineyards and on dairies
