Robot Dictionary. Humanoids

Grace

n., humanoid.

Awakening Health, Hong Kong, 2021. A humanlike care android trialled in eldercare and health settings; built for conversation as much as task work. In pilot deployments.

Grace, sister project to Sophia, was built during the pandemic for eldercare wards, pairing a nurse's uniform with thermal cameras and multilingual bedside talk.
