    Humanoids

    Ameca

    n., humanoid. Engineered Arts, United Kingdom, 2021. In production.

    The grey faced expressive android whose stunned expressions became the face of the AI era.

    Measures and facts
    MakerEngineered Arts
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    First shown2021
    StatusIn production

    Ameca's grey skinned double takes made it the most photographed face of the AI boom, headlining trade shows and museum halls. Engineered Arts builds each unit in Cornwall around its Mesmer facial actuation system.
