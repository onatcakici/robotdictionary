    Wheeled machines

    DEEBOT X1 OMNI

    n., wheeled machine. Ecovacs Robotics, China. In service.

    Home vacuum and mop robot paired with a station that empties its dustbin, washes the mop pads and refills the water tank automatically.

    Measures and facts
    MakerEcovacs Robotics
    CountryChina
    StatusIn service
    ActuationTwo drive wheels with a brush roller and twin spinning mop pads
    PowerRechargeable battery with an all in one docking station
    SensorsLaser distance sensor with a depth camera for obstacle recognition
    AutonomyLaser mapping with camera based obstacle avoidance and voice control

    The X1 OMNI navigates with a laser distance sensor and adds a forward depth camera that recognises small obstacles such as cables, shoes and pet bowls. Twin spinning mop pads scrub while the robot vacuums at up to 5,000 Pa, and it returns to an all in one station that empties dust, washes the pads and dries them with warm air. It supports voice commands and a phone app with stored floor maps. Later Ecovacs models have superseded it, though many remain in service.

    Of note

    • Rated at 5,000 Pa of suction
    • Station empties the bin, washes the mop pads and dries them with hot air
