    Wheeled machines

    Whiz

    n., wheeled machine. SoftBank Robotics, Japan, 2019. In production.

    A vacuum sweeper trained by walking it once, then set loose on office carpets.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSoftBank Robotics
    CountryJapan
    First shown2019
    StatusIn production

    SoftBank's Whiz is taught its route once by hand, then vacuums office carpets nightly; tens of thousands were leased across Asia's commercial cleaning market.

    1. SoftBank Robotics website, softbankrobotics.com
