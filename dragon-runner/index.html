    Dragon Runner

    Also known as FOSTER MILLER DRAGON RUNNER

    n., wheeled machine. Qinetiq North America and Foster-Miller, United States, 2004.

    Small throwable ground robot for urban combat, developed at Carnegie Mellon University with US Marine Corps funding and fitted with QinetiQ electronics and casing.

    Measures and facts
    MakerQinetiq North America and Foster-Miller
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2004
    Height13 cm
    Mass7.3 kg
    Length39 cm
    Top speed6 km/h

    Of note

    • Measures 13 cm high and 39 cm long
    • Top speed of 6 km/h
    • Early design and field testing done at the US Naval Research Laboratory
