    Quadrupeds

    RunBot

    n., quadruped. 2005. Research platform.

    Miniature two legged walking robot that uses reflexes triggered by foot contact sensors instead of a central pattern generator to produce its gait.

    Measures and facts
    First shown2005
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Built in 2005 by Tao Geng as part of his PhD
    • Its walking speed can be improved through reinforcement learning
    • Its movement draws on the work of neurophysiologist Nikolai Bernstein
