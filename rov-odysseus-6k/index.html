    Underwater and marine

    ROV Odysseus 6K

    n., underwater robot. Pelagic Research Services, United States. In service.

    Deep diving remotely operated vehicle from Pelagic Research Services of South Wellfleet, Massachusetts, rated to work as deep as 6,000 m of seawater.

    Measures and facts
    MakerPelagic Research Services
    CountryUnited States
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • Maximum rated depth of 6,000 m (20,000 ft)
    • Name combines the hero Odysseus with its 6,000 m depth rating
