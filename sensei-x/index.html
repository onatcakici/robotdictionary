    Medical and surgical

    Sensei X

    n., medical robot.

    Medical robot that helps physicians guide a catheter, a small flexible tube, during complex minimally invasive operations.

    Measures and facts

    Of note

    • Guides catheters used to treat target tissue and deliver medicines
