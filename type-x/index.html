    Wheeled machines

    Type-X

    n., wheeled machine. Milrem Robotics, Estonia, 2020.

    Uncrewed ground combat vehicle from the Estonian firm Milrem Robotics, first presented in 2020 and the company's second robotic vehicle after THeMIS.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMilrem Robotics
    CountryEstonia
    First shown2020

    Of note

    • Unveiled in 2020
    • Milrem's second unmanned ground vehicle, following its flagship THeMIS
