    Wheeled machines

    Proteus

    n., wheeled machine. Amazon Robotics, United States, 2022. In service.

    Amazon's first fully autonomous warehouse robot, a flat low base that lifts wheeled GoCart containers and moves them through areas shared with staff.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAmazon Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2022
    StatusIn service
    ActuationLow profile drive base that tunnels under wheeled carts and lifts them
    PowerRechargeable battery with autonomous charging
    SensorsLidar, cameras and safety scanners paired with a projected light field
    AutonomyOnboard perception and fleet coordination with no floor infrastructure or fencing
    Safetyfree roaming among workers

    Proteus is a low profile mobile robot that drives under a wheeled GoCart of packages, lifts it and hauls it between sortation and outbound dock areas. Earlier Amazon floor robots worked inside fenced fields, while Proteus uses onboard perception and a projected light field to move safely in open aisles alongside employees. It navigates without floor tape or barcode grids and is coordinated as part of a larger fleet inside Amazon fulfillment and sortation buildings.

    Proteus is Amazon's first floor robot safe to roam freely among workers, glowing a green beam as it slides beneath carts; it marks the merger of the Kiva lineage with modern perception.

    Of note

    • Announced in June 2022 as Amazon's first fully autonomous mobile robot
    • Built to work in the same floor space as employees rather than in a caged field
    • Moves the wheeled GoCart containers used for packages in outbound docks
