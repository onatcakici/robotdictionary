    Medical and surgical

    ZEUS Robotic Surgical System

    n., medical robot. Intuitive Surgical, United States. Discontinued.

    Surgical robot made by Computer Motion that had three remotely controlled arms, won FDA clearance in 2001 and was dropped in 2003 after the merger with Intuitive Surgical.

    Measures and facts
    MakerIntuitive Surgical
    CountryUnited States
    StatusDiscontinued

    Of note

    • Cleared by the FDA in 2001
    • One arm, AESOP, was a voice controlled endoscope
    • Discontinued in 2003; Intuitive Surgical developed the da Vinci system instead
