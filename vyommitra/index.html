Vyommitra

Half humanoid robot built by ISRO to fly on uncrewed Gaganyaan test missions and monitor cabin systems in place of a crew.

MakerIndian Space Research Organisation, Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre
CountryIndia
First shown2020
StatusPrototype
ActuationElectric actuators in the head, torso and arms
SensorsCameras and microphones for face and speech recognition, cabin parameter sensing
AutonomyMonitors panels and answers crew questions in Hindi and English

Vyommitra has a head, torso and arms but no legs, built largely from aluminium alloy to survive launch loads and the spacecraft environment. It is designed to read and operate control panels, monitor cabin parameters such as pressure, manage life support functions and report by voice in Hindi and English while recognising the crew it works with. ISRO unveiled it in January 2020 at a human spaceflight symposium in Bengaluru, with flight planned on uncrewed Gaganyaan missions before crewed flights begin.

Of note

  • Built for uncrewed Gaganyaan test flights ahead of Indian crewed missions
  • Unveiled in January 2020 at a human spaceflight symposium in Bengaluru
