    Wheeled machines

    Servi

    n., wheeled machine. Bear Robotics, United States, 2020. In production.

    Tray carrying restaurant robot that brings food from the kitchen to tables and returns dirty dishes along a mapped route.

    Measures and facts
    MakerBear Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2020
    StatusIn production
    Height1.04 m
    Payload30 kg
    Top speed1.2 m/s (4.3 km/h)
    Runtime10 h
    PowerRechargeable battery with a charging dock
    SensorsLidar and depth cameras with near zero blind spot coverage
    AutonomySelf driving navigation on a learned floor map with obstacle avoidance
    Traysthree

    Servi is a wheeled service robot with two open trays and a bus tub, sized to pass through aisles as narrow as about 55 cm. Lidar and depth cameras give it a near continuous view of its surroundings so it can drive itself between kitchen, tables and bussing station without ceiling markers. Staff load it, tap a table number and let it run, which cuts the walking part of table service. It is deployed in casual dining chains, hotels, cafeterias and care facilities.

    Of note

    • Distributed in Japan by SoftBank Robotics from 2020
    • LG Electronics became the majority owner of Bear Robotics in 2024
    • Carries about 10 kg per tray, roughly 30 kg in total
