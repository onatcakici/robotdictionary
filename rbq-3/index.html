    Quadrupeds

    RBQ-3

    n., quadruped. Rainbow Robotics, South Korea. In production.

    Electric quadruped from the Korean maker of the HUBO humanoids, sold as a platform for research and field inspection.

    Measures and facts
    MakerRainbow Robotics
    CountrySouth Korea
    StatusIn production
    Degrees of freedom12
    ActuationElectric joint actuators, three per leg
    PowerOnboard swappable battery
    SensorsInertial measurement unit, optional cameras and lidar
    AutonomyOnboard balance and gait control with a development kit

    Rainbow Robotics, founded in 2011 out of the KAIST HUBO laboratory, developed the RBQ series as its legged mobile platform line. RBQ-3 has twelve electric joints, onboard balance control and a software development kit, and is offered with camera and lidar options for navigation research and inspection work. The company has since scaled the design up in the larger RBQ-10 and has worked with Hyundai Rotem on a multi legged robot for defence use.

    Of note

    • Built by the KAIST spin off behind the HUBO humanoid robots
    • Basis of the larger RBQ-10 and of a defence quadruped studied with Hyundai Rotem
