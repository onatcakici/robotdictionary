    Humanoids

    Apollo

    n., humanoid. Apptronik, United States, 2023. In pilot deployments.

    A warehouse humanoid designed around swappable batteries and friendly form language; piloted with Mercedes-Benz and GXO.

    Measures and facts
    MakerApptronik
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2023
    StatusIn pilot deployments
    Heightapprox. 173 cm
    Payloadapprox. 25 kg
    DeploymentsMercedes-Benz; GXO pilots
    Mass160 lb

    Apptronik built Apollo on lessons from NASA's Valkyrie programme at the University of Texas. Its swappable batteries and friendly proportions target warehouse and factory work; Mercedes-Benz began piloting it in 2024 and returned as an investor, and Google DeepMind partnered on its intelligence.
