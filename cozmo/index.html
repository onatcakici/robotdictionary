    Wheeled machines

    Cozmo

    n., wheeled machine. Anki, United States, 2016. Discontinued.

    A toy forklift with the soul of a Pixar character; a million sold before its maker folded.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAnki
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2016
    StatusDiscontinued

    Cozmo out charmed toys costing ten times more, animated by former Pixar talent; a million sold before Anki's 2019 shutdown became the industry's cautionary tale about consumer robotics.
