    Rashmi n., humanoid. India. Indian female humanoid robot with lip synced speech in several languages, developed by Ranchi programmer Ranjit Shrivastava.

    RB-Y1 n., humanoid. Rainbow Robotics, South Korea, 2024. Wheeled two armed humanoid manipulator sold as a development platform, notable for a six axis body that raises and leans the torso while driving. In production.

    Reachy 2 n., humanoid. Pollen Robotics, France, 2024. Open source humanoid torso on an omnidirectional base, sold to research and teleoperation customers and now built under Hugging Face ownership. In production.

    REEM-C n., humanoid. PAL Robotics, Spain, 2013. Adult size biped from PAL Robotics with fully articulated hands, sold as a research platform for walking, manipulation and human robot interaction work. Research platform.

    Reflex n., humanoid. Reflex Robotics, United States, 2024. A wheeled telepresence trained humanoid that gets to work minutes out of the crate. In pilot deployments.

    Repliee Q1 n., android. Lifelike android, a humanoid robot made to resemble a human being closely in appearance.

    Repliee Q2 n., android. Osaka University and Kokoro, Japan, 2005. Female android from Hiroshi Ishiguro's laboratory, built with air actuators under silicone skin to study how people react to lifelike machines. Retired.

    RIBA n., humanoid. RIKEN and Tokai Rubber Industries, Japan, 2009. Bear faced care robot that could lift a person from a bed into a wheelchair, the forerunner of ROBEAR. Retired.

    Ribo n., humanoid. Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh, 2015. Social humanoid robot built by RoboSUST, a robotics group at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology in Bangladesh, described as the first to speak Bengali.

    ROBEAR n., humanoid. RIKEN, Japan, 2015. A bear faced nursing robot strong enough to lift patients gently from bed to wheelchair. Research platform.

    RoBee n., humanoid. Oversonic Robotics, Italy. Italian cognitive humanoid on a wheeled base, leased to manufacturers and hospitals for inspection, material handling and logistics work. In service.

    Robert the Robot n., humanoid. RB Labs, Switzerland, 2025. Humanoid robot from the Swiss financial technology company RB Labs, launched in 2025 to give investment advice and financial guidance in conversation.

    RoBoHoN n., humanoid. Sharp, Japan, 2016. Palm size walking humanoid that is also a working mobile phone, sold to Japanese consumers and businesses across several model revisions. In service.

    Robonaut n., humanoid. Lyndon B. Johnson Space Center, United States. NASA humanoid robot from the Dexterous Robotics Laboratory at Johnson Space Center in Houston, built for space jobs that need nimble handling rather than heavy lifting. In development.

    Robonaut 1 n., humanoid. NASA Johnson Space Center with DARPA, United States, 2000. NASA's first dexterous humanoid torso, built with DARPA to test whether a teleoperated robot could handle astronaut tools and handrails. Retired.

    Robonaut 2 n., humanoid. NASA Johnson Space Center with General Motors, United States, 2010. First humanoid robot flown to the International Space Station, a dexterous torso that operated station equipment and human tools in orbit. Retired.

    Robosapien n., humanoid. WowWee, 2004. Biomorphic toy robot created by Mark Tilden and made by WowWee, released in 2004 with preset moves and infrared remote control.

    Robosapien V2 n., humanoid. Second generation humanoid toy robot in Mark Tilden's Robosapien line, about 56 cm tall and able to speak a set of recorded phrases.

    Robot Kopernicus n., humanoid. 2023. Humanoid robot modelled on the features of Nicolaus Copernicus that talks with museum visitors and answers their questions, introduced in 2023.

    Robot Man of Szeged n., humanoid. University of Szeged, Hungary, 1962. Early Hungarian robot built in 1962 by Dániel Muszka of the University of Szeged to welcome guests and direct them at the entrance of the Szeged Pioneer House. Retired.

    RoboThespian n., humanoid. Engineered Arts, United Kingdom, 2005. Life size interactive performing humanoid sold to science centres and visitor attractions since 2005, one of the longest running commercial humanoid products. In production.

    ROBOTIS OP3 n., humanoid. ROBOTIS, South Korea, 2017. Small open platform biped sold worldwide for education and research, built from the maker's Dynamixel smart servos and used in humanoid soccer leagues. In production.

    Roboy n., humanoid. University of Zurich Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, Switzerland, 2013. Tendon driven humanoid built in about nine months by a Zurich led consortium to copy human muscle and tendon actuation in a child sized body. Research platform.

    Rollin' Justin n., humanoid. German Aerospace Center DLR, Germany, 2008. Wheeled humanoid from DLR with torque controlled arms and hands, commanded from the International Space Station in orbital telerobotics experiments. Research platform.

    Ropid n., humanoid. Robo Garage, Japan. Small humanoid robot created by Tomotaka Takahashi of Robo Garage at Kyoto University.

    RS Media n., humanoid. WowWee. Biomorphic robot from WowWee built on the Robosapien V2 body, running Linux based software and able to record and replay sound, photos and video.

    S

    S-One n., humanoid. SCHAFT, Japan, 2013. The DRC Trials winner from a Google acquired startup later shut down; its walking was years ahead. Retired.

    S1 n., humanoid. Astribot, China, 2024. A dual armed humanoid noted for startling manipulation speed in kitchen demonstrations. In pilot deployments.

    Sabor n., humanoid. Switzerland. Humanoid robot built in Switzerland, with a body shaped broadly like that of a person.

    SAFFiR n., humanoid. Virginia Tech with the US Naval Research Laboratory, United States, 2014. Bipedal firefighting robot developed for the US Navy that walked a decommissioned ship's compartments and aimed a hose at a live fire. Prototype.

    Salvius n., humanoid. United States, 2008. Open source humanoid made in the United States, favouring salvaged and recycled parts to cut costs, with the goal of working in an ordinary home.

    Sanbot n., humanoid. Qihan Technology, China. Family of cloud connected service robots developed by Qihan Technology, a robotics and artificial intelligence firm based in Shenzhen, China. In production.

    SDR-4X n., humanoid. Sony Group, Japan. Humanoid robot developed in Japan by Sony; its SDR designation stands for Sony Dream Robot.

    SE01 n., humanoid. EngineAI, China, 2024. EngineAI's full size humanoid with a strikingly natural human gait. Research platform.

    SEROPI n., humanoid. KITECH. Humanoid robot that moves on a wheeled base rather than legs, developed by KITECH.

    Shalu n., humanoid. Dinesh Kunwar Patel, India. Humanoid robot for social interaction and teaching that Mumbai computer science teacher Dinesh Kunwar Patel built out of discarded materials.

    SICO n., humanoid. International Robotics Inc.. Entertainment robot made by International Robotics and offered commercially, featured in the film Rocky IV.

    Sophia n., humanoid. Hanson Robotics, Hong Kong, 2016. Expressive interaction humanoid known for public appearances and media work, produced in small numbers and supplied to research, education and events. In production.

    Spring Flamingo n., humanoid. MIT Leg Laboratory, United States, 1996. Planar walking biped at MIT that pioneered series elastic actuators and force controlled walking on flat ground and shallow slopes. Retired.

    Sprout n., humanoid. Fauna Robotics, United States, 2025. A compact home humanoid from CTRL-labs and DeepMind alumni; acquired by Amazon in 2026. Research platform.

    Star1 n., humanoid. Robot Era, China, 2024. A sprinting humanoid clocked at record speed across the Gobi desert in trainers. Research platform.

    Stompy n., humanoid. K-Scale Labs, United States, 2024. An open source humanoid released with plans and firmware for anyone to build. Research platform.

    Stuntronics n., humanoid. Walt Disney Imagineering, United States, 2018. Untethered acrobatic figure that performs flips and poses in mid air after being launched from a swing. Research platform.

    Surena IV n., humanoid. University of Tehran, Center of Advanced Systems and Technologies, Iran, 2019. Iranian full size humanoid with 43 degrees of freedom built to a low cost design that replaces force sensors with predictive foot contact sensing. Research platform.

    T

    T-HR3 n., humanoid. Toyota, Japan, 2017. A teleoperated avatar humanoid that mirrors its pilot through force feedback exoskeleton controls. Research platform.

    TALOS n., humanoid. PAL Robotics, Spain, 2017. Torque controlled full size humanoid sold by PAL Robotics as a platform for whole body control and dynamic locomotion research in industry and academia. Research platform.

    Telenoid n., humanoid. Ishiguro Laboratories, Japan, 2010. A minimal, ghost like telepresence android reduced to the essence of a person. Research platform.

    TEO n., humanoid. Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, RoboticsLab, Spain, 2012. Full size Spanish research humanoid used to study household tasks such as ironing, folding laundry and opening doors. Research platform.

    Tiangong n., humanoid. Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center, China, 2024. Full size electrically driven humanoid from a Beijing state backed innovation centre, demonstrated running at about 6 km/h in April 2024. Research platform.

    TOPIO n., humanoid. TOSY, Vietnam, 2007. Two legged humanoid robot from the Vietnamese firm TOSY, built to compete against people at table tennis.

    TOPIO Dio n., humanoid. Tosy, Vietnam. Wheeled humanoid service robot made by the Vietnamese company Tosy to serve customers in restaurants and coffee shops or to mix cocktails.

    TORO n., humanoid. German Aerospace Center DLR, Germany, 2013. Torque controlled humanoid from DLR assembled from lightweight robot arm joint modules to test compliant whole body balancing and walking. Research platform.

    Torobo n., humanoid. Tokyo Robotics, Japan, 2015. A research grade torque controlled humanoid sold to manipulation labs. In production.

    Toyota Partner Robot n., humanoid. Toyota, Japan, 2005. Series of humanoid robots from Toyota that debuted by playing drums and trumpets at Expo 2005 in Aichi, Japan.

    TRON 1 n., humanoid. LimX Dynamics, China, 2024. A multi modal point foot biped platform for locomotion research. In production.

    Twendy-One n., humanoid. Waseda University, Japan, 2007. A wheeled care humanoid with soft hands able to hold an egg or support a person standing. Research platform.

    U

    Unitree G1 n., humanoid. Unitree Robotics, China, 2024. Compact humanoid priced from around 13,500 dollars, sold in volume to developers, universities and companies as an affordable locomotion and manipulation platform. In production.

    Unitree H1 n., humanoid. Unitree Robotics, China, 2023. Full size electric biped sold at a fraction of Western prices, notable for a fast recorded walking speed and for widespread laboratory adoption. In production.

    Unitree H2 n., humanoid. Unitree Robotics, China, 2025. Unitree's flagship full size humanoid with more human proportions and 31 joints, aimed at performance, service and research customers. In production.

    Unitree R1 n., humanoid. Unitree Robotics, China, 2025. Humanoid robot introduced by the Chinese company Unitree Robotics in 2025, standing 123 cm tall.

    V

    Valkyrie n., humanoid. NASA Johnson Space Center, United States, 2013. NASA's first walking humanoid, built in about fifteen months for the 2013 DARPA Robotics Challenge and later loaned to university laboratories. Research platform.

    Vyommitra n., humanoid. Indian Space Research Organisation, Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre, India, 2020. Half humanoid robot built by ISRO to fly on uncrewed Gaganyaan test missions and monitor cabin systems in place of a crew. Prototype.

    W

    WABIAN-2R n., humanoid. Waseda University, Humanoid Robotics Institute, Japan, 2006. Full size biped used at Waseda to study human like walking, carrying a movable waist and trunk plus a passive toe joint. Research platform.

    WABOT-1 n., humanoid. Waseda University, Kato Laboratory, Japan, 1973. Full scale anthropomorphic robot built at Waseda University that walked on two legs, gripped objects and held simple spoken exchanges. Retired.

    WABOT-2 n., humanoid. Waseda University, Kato Laboratory, Japan, 1984. Keyboard playing humanoid from Waseda University that read printed sheet music with a camera eye and performed on an electronic organ. Retired.

    Wakamaru n., humanoid. Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Japan, 2005. Yellow domestic robot from Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, built to interact with people in a natural way and offered only to Japanese households from 2005.

    WALK-MAN n., humanoid. Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia, Italy, 2015. Full size compliant humanoid built for European disaster response research and later tested in earthquake damaged buildings in Amatrice, Italy. Research platform.

    Walker n., humanoid. UBTech, China, 2018. UBTech's first full size home humanoid, shown serving drinks at CES. Retired.

    Walker S n., humanoid. UBTech, China, 2024. An industrial humanoid piloted on automotive lines for quality inspection and assembly; deployed in fleets. In pilot deployments.

    Walker S2 n., humanoid. UBTECH Robotics, China, 2025. Industrial humanoid that swaps its own battery in about three minutes, sold to Chinese carmakers and electronics plants for continuous shift work. In production.

    Wanda n., humanoid. UniX AI, China, 2024. A wheeled home humanoid pitched at laundry and dishwashing. Announced, not yet in service.

    X

    Xianxingzhe n., humanoid. National University of Defense Technology, China, 2000. First bipedal humanoid built in China, a 140 cm machine that walked at about two steps per second and was announced in November 2000. Retired.

    XMAN-R1 n., humanoid. Keenon Robotics, China, 2025. A wheeled embodied service humanoid from the restaurant robot leader. Announced, not yet in service.
