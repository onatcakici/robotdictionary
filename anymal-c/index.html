    Quadrupeds

    ANYmal C

    n., quadruped. ANYbotics, Switzerland, 2019. In service.

    Industrial version of ANYmal, packaged for autonomous inspection rounds in power plants, chemical works and offshore facilities.

    Measures and facts
    MakerANYbotics
    CountrySwitzerland
    First shown2019
    StatusIn service
    Mass50 kg
    Payload10 kg
    Top speed1 m/s (3.6 km/h)
    Degrees of freedom12
    ActuationTwelve ANYdrive series elastic electric actuators
    PowerSwappable lithium ion battery with autonomous docking
    SensorsRotating lidar, depth cameras, zoom and thermal cameras, ultrasonic microphone
    AutonomyAutonomous mission execution with docking and remote supervision

    ANYmal C wrapped the torque controlled ANYdrive legs in a sealed industrial shell with a docking station, an inspection payload and mission planning software. It walks routes on its own, reads gauges, listens for anomalies with an ultrasonic microphone and records thermal images, then returns to charge. Utilities and process plants used it for unmanned inspection rounds, and four ANYmal C robots formed the legged element of team CERBERUS, winner of the DARPA Subterranean Challenge final in 2021.

    Of note

    • Legged element of team CERBERUS, winner of the DARPA Subterranean Challenge final in 2021
    • Deployed for routine unmanned inspection rounds in industrial plants and offshore installations
