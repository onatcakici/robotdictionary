    Astrobee

    Also known as Astrobee system

    n., robot. Ames Research Center, United States, 2014. In service.

    System of three cube shaped free flying robots from NASA's Ames Research Center that helps astronauts with routine chores aboard the International Space Station.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAmes Research Center
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2014
    StatusIn service
    Height31.75 cm
    Mass10.1 kg
    Length31.75 cm
    Width31.75 cm

    Of note

    • The three robots are named Honey, Queen and Bumble
    • Each robot is a cube 31.75 cm on a side
    • A docking station lets the robots recharge
