    RoboBee

    n., robot. Harvard University. Research platform.

    Insect sized robot from Harvard University that flies, partly untethered, on wings flapped by artificial muscles.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHarvard University
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Took twelve years of research to develop
    • Built with a fabrication method inspired by pop up books, at submillimeter scale
    • Its wings flap 120 times a second, driven by artificial muscles
