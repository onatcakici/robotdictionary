    European Robotic Arm

    n., robot arm. Airbus Defence and Space, Netherlands, 2021. In service.

    Robotic arm for the Russian segment of the International Space Station, launched attached to the Nauka module in 2021.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAirbus Defence and Space
    CountryNetherlands
    First shown2021
    StatusIn service
