    Wheeled machines

    OTTO 1500

    n., wheeled machine. OTTO Motors, Canada, 2015. In production.

    Heavy duty self driving vehicle for factories that moves pallets and large assemblies weighing up to 1,900 kg without floor guidance.

    Measures and facts
    MakerOTTO Motors
    CountryCanada
    First shown2015
    StatusIn production
    Height0.351 m
    Length1.837 m
    Mass627 kg
    Payload1900 kg
    Top speed2 m/s (7.2 km/h)

    Power80 Ah battery with autonomous charging
    SensorsSafety lidar, four depth cameras and a six axis inertial unit
    AutonomyLidar SLAM with fleet manager traffic control and no floor infrastructure

    The OTTO 1500 is a flat deck industrial mobile robot the size of a small pallet truck, able to turn in place under load. Safety lidar, four depth cameras and an inertial unit feed SLAM navigation and traffic control by a fleet manager, so it shares aisles with forklifts and staff. Typical work is moving raw material, work in progress and finished pallets between production cells and warehouses. OTTO Motors grew out of Clearpath Robotics in Kitchener, Ontario and became part of Rockwell Automation in 2023.

    Of note

    • Grew out of Clearpath Robotics and was acquired by Rockwell Automation in 2023
    • Heavy class OTTO robots passed one million production hours
    • Turns in place with a maximum turning speed of 1.5 rad per second
