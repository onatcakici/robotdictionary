    Underwater and marine

    ROV KIEL 6000

    n., underwater robot. Schilling Robotics, United States. In service.

    Electrically powered remotely operated vehicle made by Schilling Robotics of California and owned by Germany's GEOMAR, used for scientific work down to 6,000 m.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSchilling Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • Linked to its ship by a deep sea glass fibre cable
    • Can work from ships of opportunity with enough deck space, power and crane capacity
