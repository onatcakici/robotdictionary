    Humanoids

    TORO

    Also known as TOrque controlled humanoid RObot

    n., humanoid. German Aerospace Center DLR, Germany, 2013. Research platform.

    Torque controlled humanoid from DLR assembled from lightweight robot arm joint modules to test compliant whole body balancing and walking.

    Measures and facts
    MakerGerman Aerospace Center DLR
    CountryGermany
    First shown2013
    StatusResearch platform
    Height1.74 m
    Mass76.4 kg
    Degrees of freedom27
    ActuationTorque controlled electric drives with harmonic gears and joint torque sensors
    SensorsJoint torque and position sensing, six axis foot force torque sensors, torso inertial unit, stereo camera head
    AutonomyTorque level whole body control

    TORO is 1.74 m tall, weighs 76.4 kg and has 25 torque controlled joints plus two position controlled ones, built from DLR lightweight robot drive units with integrated torque sensing. Force and torque sensors in the feet, a torso inertial unit and a stereo camera head feed whole body controllers based on passivity and capture point ideas rather than position tracking. DLR developed it from an earlier biped between 2010 and 2014 and has used it for multi contact balancing, walking on uneven ground and physical human robot interaction studies.

    Of note

    • Assembled from DLR lightweight robot arm joint modules
    • Used to demonstrate multi contact whole body balancing
