    USX-1 Defiant

    n., marine robot. Serco Inc, United States, 2025. Prototype.

    Uncrewed surface warship built for DARPA as a technology demonstrator, completed in February 2025, with sea trials beginning a month later.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSerco Inc
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2025
    StatusPrototype
    Height12.8 m
    Mass240 tonne
    Length55 m

    Of note

    • First seen publicly in March 2025 near Naval Air Station Whidbey Island
    • 55 m long and 12.8 m high, with a displacement of 240 tonnes
    • Construction overseen by the shipbuilder Serco
