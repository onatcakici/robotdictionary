    Wheeled machines

    Automower 450X

    n., wheeled machine. Husqvarna, Sweden, 1995. In production.

    The descendant of the world's first robotic lawn mower, three decades on the job.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHusqvarna
    CountrySweden
    First shown1995
    StatusIn production

    Husqvarna shipped the first robotic lawn mower in 1995, solar powered and wire guided; three decades of descendants make mowing the most automated chore after vacuuming.
