    Underwater and marine

    Slocum glider

    n., underwater robot. Teledyne Webb Research, United States. In production.

    Buoyancy driven underwater glider that travels for weeks by changing its displacement, named after the sailor Joshua Slocum.

    Measures and facts
    MakerTeledyne Webb Research
    CountryUnited States
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • A Slocum glider named Scarlet Knight crossed the Atlantic in 2009
