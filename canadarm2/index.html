    Arms and workcells

    Canadarm2

    n., robot arm. MDA, Canada, 2001. In service.

    Robotic arm of the International Space Station, built in Canada, that moves end over end between fixtures and captures visiting cargo ships.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMDA
    CountryCanada
    First shown2001
    StatusIn service
    Length17.6 m
    Joints7

    Of note

    • Launched on Space Shuttle mission STS-100 in April 2001
