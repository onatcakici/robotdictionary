TALOS

Torque-controlled full-size humanoid sold by PAL Robotics as a platform for whole-body control and dynamic locomotion research in industry and academia.

MakerPAL Robotics
CountrySpain
First shown2017
StatusResearch platform
Height1.75 m
Mass95 kg
Payload6 kg
Degrees of freedom32
Actuation32 high-torque brushless DC motors with torque sensing in every joint
SensorsJoint torque sensors, IMU, RGB-D head camera, expandable sensor modules
AutonomyROS-based whole-body control stack supplied with the robot

TALOS is 175 centimetres tall, weighs 95 kilograms and has 32 degrees of freedom driven by high-torque brushless DC motors, with torque sensing in every joint. That joint-level torque feedback is the point of the machine: it allows compliant whole-body control, so the robot can absorb pushes, take steps it was not commanded and lift six kilograms per arm. It ships with ROS support and an RGB-D head camera, and accepts additional sensors and hands through expansion modules. PAL sells it commercially to corporate and university laboratories.

Of note
