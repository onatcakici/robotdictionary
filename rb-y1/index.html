    Humanoids

    RB-Y1

    n., humanoid. Rainbow Robotics, South Korea, 2024. In production.

    Wheeled two armed humanoid manipulator sold as a development platform, notable for a six axis body that raises and leans the torso while driving.

    Measures and facts
    MakerRainbow Robotics
    CountrySouth Korea
    First shown2024
    StatusIn production
    Height1.4 m
    Mass131 kg
    Payload3 kg
    Top speed2.5 m/s (9 km/h)
    ActuationTwo seven axis electric arms on a six axis torso and two wheel balancing base
    SensorsHead and wrist cameras with joint encoders and IMU
    AutonomySupplied development kit for building manipulation and learning applications

    RB-Y1 is 1.4 metres tall in its neutral pose, weighs 131 kilograms and combines two seven axis arms with a six axis torso on a two wheel balancing base. The torso lets it change working height by more than 50 centimetres and lean into turns, so it can pick from the floor and from a high shelf without a lift column. Each arm carries three kilograms and the base moves at up to 2.5 metres per second. Rainbow Robotics, in which Samsung Electronics took a controlling stake, sells it with a development kit to companies building manipulation and embodied learning applications.

    Of note

    • Presented in April 2024 as Korea's first bimanual mobile manipulator of its type
    • Torso axes change working height by more than 50 centimetres
    • Samsung Electronics became the controlling shareholder of Rainbow Robotics
