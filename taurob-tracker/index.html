    Taurob Tracker

    n., wheeled machine. Taurob GmbH, Austria. In production.

    Austrian remote controlled ground robot made by Taurob, first developed for fire brigade reconnaissance and now also used by armed forces, universities and oil and gas companies.

    Measures and facts
    MakerTaurob GmbH
