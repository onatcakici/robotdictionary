Robot Dictionary. Humanoids

EVE

n., humanoid.

1X Technologies, Norway, 2019. A wheeled humanoid used for security patrol and logistics; 1X's first commercial platform. In production.

EVE patrolled warehouses and offices as 1X's first commercial product, and its teleoperation data became the training corpus for the company's home humanoid NEO.

Base
wheeled
Pace
approx. 14 km/h
