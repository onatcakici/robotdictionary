    Rovers

    PrOP-M

    Also known as Mars 2 rover, Mars 3 rover

    n., rover. VNII Transmash and NPO Lavochkin, Soviet Union, 1971. Lost.

    Small tethered walking rover carried by the Mars 2 and Mars 3 landers, intended to skid across the surface on twin skis.

    Measures and facts
    MakerVNII Transmash and NPO Lavochkin
    CountrySoviet Union
    First shown1971
    StatusLost
    Mass4.5 kg
    ActuationTwo powered skis with obstacle contact reversing
    PowerSupplied by cable from the lander
    SensorsPenetrometer and gamma ray densitometer
    AutonomyHardwired reflex behaviour on contact with obstacles

    Each PrOP-M was a squat box that moved on two powered skis rather than wheels, connected to its lander by a 15 metre umbilical and steered by simple contact logic that reversed and turned at obstacles. A manipulator arm was to lift the rover onto the ground, after which it would leave tracks and take penetrometer and radiation density readings within view of the lander cameras. Mars 2 crashed in November 1971 and Mars 3 stopped transmitting seconds after landing, so neither rover was ever deployed.

    Of note

    • First rovers sent to Mars, neither of which operated
    • Tether limited operation to 15 metres from the lander
