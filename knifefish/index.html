    Underwater and marine

    Knifefish

    n., underwater robot. General Dynamics Mission Systems and Bluefin Robotics, United States, 2012. In service.

    Propeller driven autonomous underwater vehicle for mine countermeasures, developed by General Dynamics Mission Systems and Bluefin Robotics for the United States Navy.

    Measures and facts
    MakerGeneral Dynamics Mission Systems and Bluefin Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2012
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • First unveiled at a Navy exposition in April 2012
    • Meant to take over mine hunting from the Navy's trained dolphins and sea lions
    • Used by the United States in the 2026 Strait of Hormuz campaign
