    Muppet Mobile Lab

    n., animatronic figure. Walt Disney Imagineering, United States. Retired.

    Free roving animatronic attraction created by Walt Disney Imagineering and trialled at Disney theme parks in Anaheim, Orlando and Hong Kong.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWalt Disney Imagineering
    CountryUnited States
    StatusRetired

    Of note

    • Tested at Disney California Adventure, Walt Disney World and Hong Kong Disneyland
