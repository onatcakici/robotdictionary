    Humanoids

    Rashmi

    n., humanoid. India.

    Indian female humanoid robot with lip synced speech in several languages, developed by Ranchi programmer Ranjit Shrivastava.

    Measures and facts
    CountryIndia

    Of note

    • Developed by Ranjit Shrivastava, a programmer from Ranchi, India
