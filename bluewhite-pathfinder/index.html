    Wheeled machines

    Pathfinder

    n., wheeled machine. Bluewhite, Israel. In production.

    Retrofit autonomy kit that turns conventional orchard and vineyard tractors into driverless machines supervised from a control room by one operator running several tractors.

    Measures and facts
    MakerBluewhite
    CountryIsrael
    StatusIn production
    ActuationRetrofit actuation of steering, throttle, brakes and implement controls on existing tractors
    PowerThe host tractor's diesel engine
    SensorsLidar, radar, cameras and corrected satellite positioning
    AutonomyDriverless row work supervised from a control room and mobile app, one operator overseeing several tractors

    Pathfinder is a retrofit kit rather than a new vehicle, adding steering, throttle, brake and implement actuation plus a sensor mast to tractors a grower already owns. Lidar, radar, cameras and corrected satellite positioning let the tractor drive orchard and vineyard rows driverless while pulling sprayers, mowers and harvest aids. A supervisor watches a fleet from a dashboard and can stop or reroute any machine. It runs in California almond, citrus and vineyard operations as well as in Israel.

    Of note

    • Works as a brand agnostic kit fitted to tractors from several manufacturers
    • Announced a collaboration with the farm equipment maker CNH on autonomous tractors
    • Bluewhite was acquired by the defence and technology group Elbit Systems
