    Robotis Bioloid

    n., robot kit. Robotis, South Korea.

    Robot kit for hobbyists and education from the South Korean company Robotis, using modular servo units that combine into wheeled, legged or humanoid machines.

    Measures and facts
    MakerRobotis
    CountrySouth Korea

    Of note

    • Built around daisy chained AX-12A Dynamixel servomotors
    • Programmed with the C based RoboPlus software
    • Often compared with Lego Mindstorms and VEX Robotics kits
