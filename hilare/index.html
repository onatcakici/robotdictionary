    Wheeled machines

    Hilare

    n., wheeled machine. Laboratory for Analysis and Architecture of Systems, France, 1977.

    French mobile robot built in 1977 by the Laboratory for Analysis and Architecture of Systems, its name an acronym for heuristics integrated into software and automation in an evolving robot.
