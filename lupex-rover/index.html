    Rovers

    LUPEX rover

    n., rover. Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Japan. In development.

    Tracked lunar rover from Mitsubishi Heavy Industries of Japan for the Lunar Polar Exploration Mission, weighing 350 kg and measuring 2 m long.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMitsubishi Heavy Industries
    CountryJapan
    StatusIn development
    Height3.3 m
    Mass350 kg
    Length2 m
    Width1.8 m
