    Underwater and marine

    Hammerhead USV-T

    n., marine robot. Meggitt Training Systems. In production.

    Fast remote controlled boat that serves as a moving target in naval training exercises, built by Meggitt Training Systems with A. F. Theriault & Son.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMeggitt Training Systems
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • About eighty other companies supply internal parts and accessories
