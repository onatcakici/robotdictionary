    Intelligent Water class AUV

    n., underwater robot. Harbin Engineering University, China.

    Class of Chinese autonomous underwater vehicle developed by Harbin Engineering University's Shipbuilding Engineering Institute for the People's Liberation Army Navy.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHarbin Engineering University
    CountryChina
