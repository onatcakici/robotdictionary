    Underwater and marine

    Roboshark

    n., marine robot. 2003.

    Robotic shark built by Andrew Sneath in 2003 and fitted with a camera to film whale sharks, bull sharks and great white sharks for the BBC.

    Measures and facts
    First shown2003

    Of note

    • Featured in the BBC Natural History Unit special Smart Sharks: Swimming with Roboshark
