    Humanoids

    Elektro

    n., humanoid. Westinghouse Electric Corporation, United States, 1937.

    Humanoid robot constructed in 1937 and 1938 by a team of Westinghouse Electric Corporation engineers at the company's plant in Mansfield, Ohio.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWestinghouse Electric Corporation
    CountryUnited States
    First shown1937
    Height7 ft
    Mass265 lb

    Of note

    • Stood 7 ft tall and weighed 265 lb
    • Engineering team included J.M. Barnett, Jack Weeks Sr. and Harold Gorsuch
