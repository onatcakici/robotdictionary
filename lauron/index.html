    Quadrupeds

    LAURON

    n., legged robot. FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik, Germany, 1994. Research platform.

    Six legged walking robot modelled on the stick insect Carausius morosus, developed over several generations by the German research centre FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik.

    Measures and facts
    MakerFZI Forschungszentrum Informatik
    CountryGermany
    First shown1994
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • First version shown to the public at CeBIT in Hanover in 1994
    • First generation relied on an artificial neural network for control
    • Fifth generation, LAURON V, was completed in 2013
