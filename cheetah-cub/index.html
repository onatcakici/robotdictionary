    Quadrupeds

    Cheetah-cub

    n., quadruped. EPFL Biorobotics Laboratory, Switzerland, 2013. Research platform.

    Cat sized compliant quadruped from EPFL that was the fastest small four legged robot of its time relative to body size.

    Measures and facts
    MakerEPFL Biorobotics Laboratory
    CountrySwitzerland
    First shown2013
    StatusResearch platform
    Height0.21 m
    Mass1.1 kg
    Top speed1.42 m/s (5.1 km/h)
    ActuationServo motors driving cable and spring loaded three segment legs
    PowerExternal electrical supply
    AutonomyOpen loop central pattern generator

    Cheetah cub has three segment legs built from springs and cables, a layout taken from measured cat limb anatomy, driven by hobby scale servo motors. A central pattern generator produces its gait with no feedback from the terrain, so the springy legs alone supply the stability needed to run. It reached 1.42 metres per second, close to seven body lengths per second, which made it the fastest quadruped robot under thirty kilograms when published.

    Of note

    • Ran at about seven body lengths per second, a record for small quadruped robots at publication
    • Leg design derived directly from cat limb anatomy
