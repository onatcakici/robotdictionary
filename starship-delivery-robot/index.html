    Wheeled machines

    Starship delivery robot

    n., wheeled machine. Starship Technologies, Estonia and United States, 2015. In service.

    Six wheeled sidewalk robot that carries groceries and meals a few kilometres from a hub to a customer, navigating autonomously with remote human oversight.

    Measures and facts
    MakerStarship Technologies
    CountryEstonia and United States
    First shown2015
    StatusIn service
    Mass25 kg
    Payload9.1 kg
    Top speed1.67 m/s (6 km/h)
    ActuationSix wheels driven by electric hub motors
    PowerRechargeable lithium ion battery pack
    SensorsCameras, ultrasonic sensors, radar, satellite positioning and inertial measurement
    AutonomyAutonomous navigation with remote operators supervising and able to take control
    Deliveriesmillions completed
    Paceapprox. 6 km/h

    The Starship robot is a knee high six wheeled electric container that crosses sidewalks and road crossings at walking pace. It drives itself using cameras, ultrasonic sensors, radar and satellite positioning, while remote operators monitor fleets and take over at difficult crossings. Cargo sits in a locked bin that the customer opens from a phone app. Fleets work university campuses, suburbs and town centres in the United States, the United Kingdom, Finland, Denmark and Estonia.

    Starship's coolers on wheels have crossed streets in dozens of cities and campuses since 2016, passing millions of commercial deliveries years before any rival. The company was founded by two Skype co founders in Estonia.

    Of note

    • Passed 9 million autonomous deliveries in October 2025, with a fleet of roughly 2,700 robots
    • Founded in 2014 in Tallinn, Estonia, by Skype co founders Ahti Heinla and Janus Friis
    • Delivers for Grubhub on university campuses, and for Co op and Tesco stores in the United Kingdom
