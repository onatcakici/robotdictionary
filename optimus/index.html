Optimus

Also known as Tesla Bot

Full-size electric humanoid that Tesla is developing for factory and general labour tasks, currently used only inside Tesla facilities.

MakerTesla
CountryUnited States
First shown2022
StatusPrototype
Height1.73 m
ActuationCustom rotary and linear electric actuators with an 11-degree-of-freedom hand
Power2.3 kWh battery pack housed in the torso
SensorsCamera-based vision with tactile fingertip sensing
AutonomyEnd-to-end neural network policies sharing perception software with Tesla's driver assistance stack

Optimus is a bipedal humanoid roughly 1.73 metres tall, driven by custom rotary and linear electric actuators with an 11-degree-of-freedom hand. Control comes from end-to-end neural network policies trained on video and teleoperation data, reusing perception software from Tesla's driver assistance stack. Tesla first walked a working prototype on stage in September 2022 and showed a lighter second generation in December 2023. Units have been shown handling parts inside Tesla plants and serving food at a company diner, but none have been sold to outside customers.

Of note

  • Walked unassisted on stage at Tesla AI Day in September 2022
  • Second generation announced in December 2023 with reduced mass and faster walking
  • Shown handling parts inside Tesla manufacturing sites and serving food at a company diner
