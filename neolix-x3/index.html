    Neolix X3

    n., wheeled machine. Neolix, China, 2019. In production.

    Low speed cabless electric delivery van used for parcels, groceries and vending on Chinese public roads under Level 4 autonomy with remote supervision.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNeolix
    CountryChina
    First shown2019
    StatusIn production
    ActuationCabless four wheeled low speed electric vehicle with a modular cargo body
    PowerBattery electric drive with swappable battery packs
    SensorsLidar, radar and cameras
    AutonomySAE Level 4 map free navigation on public roads with remote supervision

    The X3 is one of Neolix's cabless RoboVan models, a boxy low speed electric vehicle with a shelved or locker cargo body and no driver position. It navigates public roads with lidar, radar and cameras using map free planning, monitored from a remote operations centre. Operators use it for parcel runs, supermarket restocking, mobile vending and campus logistics. Neolix builds the vehicles on its own production line and rents or sells them to logistics firms.

    Of note

    • Neolix reports more than 10,000 RoboVans deployed in 300 cities across 15 countries
    • Fleet has covered more than 50 million kilometres, with over 1,200 units in Qingdao alone
    • Founded in 2018 and among the first Chinese firms to mass produce driverless delivery vehicles
