    ACM III

    n., robot. Tokyo Institute of Technology, Japan, 1972. Retired.

    Snake robot built by Shigeo Hirose, among the first machines to crawl by undulating its body like a real snake.

    Measures and facts
    MakerTokyo Institute of Technology
    CountryJapan
    First shown1972
    StatusRetired

    Of note

    • ACM stands for Active Cord Mechanism
