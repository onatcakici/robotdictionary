    Wheeled machines

    MarkV-A1

    n., wheeled machine. Northrop Grumman.

    Remotely operated bomb disposal robot designed by Northrop Grumman to handle suspected explosives without putting people at risk.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNorthrop Grumman

    Of note

    • Part of the Remotec ANDROS line of bomb disposal robots
    • Used by bomb squads and first responders around the world
