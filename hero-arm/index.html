    Hero Arm

    n., medical robot. Open Bionics, United Kingdom, 2018. In production.

    Lightweight myoelectric prosthetic arm with a multi grip hand, 3D printed to fit each wearer and offered with swappable covers.

    Measures and facts
    MakerOpen Bionics
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    First shown2018
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Recognised by Guinness World Records as the first medically certified 3D printed bionic arm
    • Made in Bristol

    Sources

    1. First medically certified 3D printed bionic arm, Guinness World Records, guinnessworldrecords.com
    2. Robotics startup launches first medically approved 3D printed bionic arms, Medical Xpress, medicalxpress.com
