    SPHERES

    Also known as Synchronized Position Hold Engage and Reorient Experimental Satellite

    n., robot. Payload Systems, Inc., United States, 1999.

    Set of free flying robots developed by MIT's Space Systems Laboratory for NASA and the US military to test formation flight, docking and autonomy algorithms aboard the International Space Station.

    Of note
