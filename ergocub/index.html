Robot Dictionary. Humanoids

ergoCub

n., humanoid.

Italian Institute of Technology, Italy, 2023. iCub's industrial descendant, built with INAIL to study safe human-robot labour. A research platform.

ergoCub was introduced by Italian Institute of Technology of Italy and first filed in this dictionary under the year 2023. It belongs to the humanoid class, machines built to work in spaces made for people. It serves as a research platform rather than a commercial product.

See also iCub, WALK-MAN.
