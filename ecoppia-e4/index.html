Ecoppia E4

Waterless robotic brush that travels along a row of solar modules each night to remove desert dust, powered by its own solar panel.

MakerEcoppia
CountryIsrael
StatusIn production
ActuationWheeled brush unit that travels along the module row on the panel frame
PowerOnboard solar panel and battery, with no water and no external supply
SensorsPosition and edge sensors with cloud connectivity for scheduling and reporting
AutonomyCleans automatically on a nightly schedule under cloud control, with no operator on site

The E4 is a slim robot that rides along the frame of a solar module row, sweeping a microfibre brush across the glass to lift dust without water. Each unit carries its own solar panel and battery, so it needs no external power or plumbing, and cleans on a nightly schedule set from a cloud platform. Removing dust recovers energy yield that would otherwise be lost, which matters most in arid high dust regions. It works on utility scale photovoltaic plants.

Of note

  • Contracted to clean a 400 megawatt Azure Power hybrid solar site in India.
  • Selected for about 427 megawatts of Fortum solar plants in India.
  • The later H4 model adds a hybrid arm that moves the cleaning unit between module rows.

Maker: www.ecoppia.com
