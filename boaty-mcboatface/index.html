    Underwater and marine

    Boaty McBoatface

    Also known as Boaty

    n., underwater robot. National Oceanography Centre, United Kingdom, 2017. In service.

    British autonomous underwater vehicle belonging to the Autosub Long Range class, run by the National Oceanography Centre for scientific research.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNational Oceanography Centre
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    First shown2017
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • One of six vehicles in its class
    • The class was launched in 2017
    • Can be launched from the shore
