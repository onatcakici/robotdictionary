    Canadarm

    Also known as Shuttle Remote Manipulator System, SRMS, Canadarm 1

    n., robot arm. SPAR Aerospace, Canada. Retired.

    Family of robotic arms built by SPAR Aerospace of Canada that Space Shuttle orbiters carried to release, move and capture payloads in orbit.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSPAR Aerospace
    CountryCanada
    StatusRetired
    Mass410 kg
    Length15.2 m

    Of note

    • Measured 15.2 m long with a mass of 410 kg
    • After the Columbia disaster it always flew alongside the Orbiter Boom Sensor System
    • The boom was used to check the shuttle's thermal protection system for damage
