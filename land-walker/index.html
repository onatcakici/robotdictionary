    Land Walker

    n., robot.

    Bipedal vehicle 3.4 m tall, invented by Masaaki Nagumo, that moves at about 1.5 km/h on wheels concealed under its feet rather than truly walking.

    Measures and facts

    Of note

    • Its creators hope similar machines might later serve in military and law enforcement roles
