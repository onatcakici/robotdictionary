    Rovers

    Tenacious

    n., rover. ispace Europe, Luxembourg, 2025. Lost.

    Five kilogram European built micro rover with a scoop and an artwork payload, lost when the Resilience lander crashed in June 2025.

    Measures and facts
    Makerispace Europe
    CountryLuxembourg
    First shown2025
    StatusLost
    Mass5 kg
    ActuationFour wheel drive with a fixed regolith scoop
    PowerSolar panel with battery
    SensorsHigh definition forward camera and navigation cameras

    Tenacious was designed and built in Luxembourg by the European subsidiary of ispace, the first lunar rover manufactured in Europe. It carried a forward scoop to collect regolith under a sample purchase agreement with NASA, plus the Moonhouse artwork by Mikael Genberg, and was to be driven from a control centre in Luxembourg. The Resilience lander of ispace Mission 2 lost its altitude reference and struck the surface on 5 June 2025 with the rover aboard.

    Of note

    • First lunar rover built in Europe
    • Carried a contracted regolith sample purchase for NASA
    • Lost in the ispace Mission 2 landing failure
