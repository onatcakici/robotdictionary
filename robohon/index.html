    Humanoids

    RoBoHoN

    n., humanoid. Sharp, Japan, 2016. In service.

    Palm size walking humanoid that is also a working mobile phone, sold to Japanese consumers and businesses across several model revisions.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSharp
    CountryJapan
    First shown2016
    StatusIn service
    Height0.195 m
    Mass0.39 kg
    ActuationMiniature servo motors driving legs, arms and head
    PowerInternal lithium ion battery
    SensorsCamera, microphone, touchscreen and accelerometer
    AutonomyCloud voice assistant with face recognition and a smartphone app platform

    RoBoHoN is 19.5 centimetres tall and weighs about 390 grams, with small servo motors in its legs, arms and head so it can stand, walk, sit and gesture. It is a genuine phone: it takes calls, runs apps and, on the original model, projects images from a lens in its forehead. Interaction is by voice through a cloud assistant, and it recognises faces to greet its owner. Sharp launched it in Japan in May 2016 at a premium price and has since released cheaper and revised models, with units also used in hotel lobbies and shop displays.

    Of note

    • Launched in Japan in May 2016 as a walking robot that is also a certified mobile phone
    • Original model included a forehead projector for displaying images
    • Used in Japanese hotel lobbies and retail displays as well as sold to consumers
