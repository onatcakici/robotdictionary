    Rovers

    Audi Lunar Quattro

    Also known as ALQ

    n., rover. Planetary Transportation Systems and Audi AG, Germany, 2015.

    Small German lunar rover developed in 2015 by engineers of the PTScientists team, with backing from Audi and partners in several countries.

    Measures and facts
    MakerPlanetary Transportation Systems and Audi AG
    CountryGermany
    First shown2015
    Mass35 kg
