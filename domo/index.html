    Humanoids

    Domo

    Also known as Dobo

    n., humanoid. Massachusetts Institute of Technology, United States, 2004. Research platform.

    Experimental humanoid robot built at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology by Jeff Weber and Aaron Edsinger to interact with people.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMassachusetts Institute of Technology
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2004
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Named after the Japanese phrase domo arigato and the Styx song Mr. Roboto
    • Funded first by NASA and later also by Toyota
