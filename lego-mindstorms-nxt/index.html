    Lego Mindstorms NXT

    n., robot kit. The Lego Group, Denmark, 2006.

    Robot building and programming kit from Lego, launched in August 2006 as the second generation of Mindstorms after the Robotics Invention System.

    Measures and facts
    MakerThe Lego Group
    CountryDenmark
    First shown2006

    Of note

    • Released on 2 August 2006
    • NXT 2.0 followed on 1 August 2009 with a color sensor
    • Succeeded by the third generation EV3 in September 2013
