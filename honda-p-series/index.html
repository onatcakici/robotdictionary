    Humanoids

    Honda P series

    n., humanoid. Honda, 1993. Retired.

    Line of prototype humanoid robots that Honda built from 1993 to 2000, the stage between its experimental E series and the ASIMO robot.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHonda
    First shown1993
    StatusRetired

    Of note

    • Honda president Hiroyuki Yoshino tied the program to improving human mobility
