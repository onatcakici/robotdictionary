    Rovers

    FLEX

    Also known as Flexible Logistics and Exploration rover

    n., rover. Venturi Astrolab, United States. Prototype.

    Large commercial lunar rover prototype that can carry cargo or two standing astronauts, demonstrated in field trials in the California desert.

    Measures and facts
    MakerVenturi Astrolab
    CountryUnited States
    StatusPrototype
    Payload1500 kg
    ActuationFour wheel drive and steering with a payload lifting frame
    PowerBattery electric with solar recharging
    SensorsDriving cameras and lidar
    AutonomyTeleoperation with autonomous point to point driving

    Astrolab built a full scale FLEX prototype and drove it in Death Valley with former astronaut Chris Hadfield aboard, testing cargo handling and crew driving positions. The design lifts and carries modular payload pallets of up to 1,500 kilograms, runs on batteries with solar recharging, and is aimed at both uncrewed logistics and Artemis era crew mobility. Astrolab holds a contract for a lunar flight on a SpaceX Starship and is among the companies studying NASA's lunar terrain vehicle.

    Of note

    • Full scale prototype driven with an astronaut aboard in Death Valley
    • Designed to carry up to 1,500 kg of modular payload
