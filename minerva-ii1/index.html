    Rovers

    MINERVA-II1

    n., rover. JAXA, Japan, 2018. Mission completed.

    Pair of small rovers released by Hayabusa2 onto asteroid Ryugu in 2018, the first rovers to move across the surface of an asteroid, which they did by hopping.
