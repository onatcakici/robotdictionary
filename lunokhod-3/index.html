    Rovers

    Lunokhod 3

    Also known as Ye-8 No.205

    n., rover. NPO Lavochkin and VNII Transmash, Soviet Union, 1977. Retired.

    Third generation Lunokhod completed in 1977 for a mission that was never funded, now preserved as a museum exhibit near Moscow.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNPO Lavochkin and VNII Transmash
    CountrySoviet Union
    First shown1977
    StatusRetired
    ActuationEight independently driven wheels
    PowerLid mounted solar panel with radioisotope heater
    SensorsImproved television and stereoscopic camera system

    Lunokhod 3 was finished as flight hardware with improved cameras and stereoscopic imaging, intended for a Luna 25 landing. No launcher or funding was allocated once the Soviet lunar programme wound down, so the rover never left Earth. It is displayed at the NPO Lavochkin museum in Khimki.

    Of note

    • Flight ready rover that never launched
    • Preserved intact at the NPO Lavochkin museum
