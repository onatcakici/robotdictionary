    Atlas (hydraulic)

    n., humanoid. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2013. Retired.

    The hydraulic research humanoid whose parkour and backflips defined a decade of legged robotics.

    Measures and facts
    MakerBoston Dynamics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2013
    StatusRetired
    Heightapprox. 150 cm
    Massapprox. 89 kg
    Featsbackflips; parkour; gymnastics

    For a decade the hydraulic Atlas was the public face of advanced robotics: running woodland trails, vaulting boxes, and finally performing gymnastics few humans could match. Boston Dynamics retired it in April 2024 with a blooper reel, one day before revealing its electric successor.
