    Humanoids

    Forerunner K2

    Also known as K2 Bumblebee

    n., humanoid. Kepler Robotics, China, 2025. In production.

    Industrial humanoid listed from about 30,000 dollars, with 52 joints and eleven degree of freedom hands, aimed at factory material handling.

    Forerunner K2 stands 175 centimetres, weighs 75 kilograms and has 52 degrees of freedom, with each hand offering eleven degrees of freedom, 25 force sensing contact points per finger and a six axis force and torque sensor at the wrist. More than 80 onboard sensors feed compute rated at 100 trillion operations per second, and Kepler quotes millimetre level placement precision. A one hour charge supports up to eight hours of work, helped by joints that draw almost no power when holding a static pose. Kepler demonstrated an eight hour continuous livestreamed work session at the 2025 World Artificial Intelligence Conference and lists the base robot from around 30,000 dollars.

    Of note

    • Completed an eight hour continuous livestreamed work session at WAIC 2025
    • Listed from about 30,000 dollars for the base configuration
    • Each hand has eleven degrees of freedom with per finger force sensing
