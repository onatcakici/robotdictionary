    Reachy Mini

    n., robot. Pollen Robotics and Hugging Face, France, 2025. In production.

    Small open source desktop robot with an expressive head and antennas, sold as a kit for experimenting with AI models.

    Measures and facts
    MakerPollen Robotics and Hugging Face
    CountryFrance
    First shown2025
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Launched in July 2025 from $299
    • Built by Pollen Robotics after its acquisition by Hugging Face
