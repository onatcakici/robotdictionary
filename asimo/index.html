    Humanoids

    ASIMO

    n., humanoid. Honda, Japan, 2000. Retired.

    The world's most famous walking robot for two decades; retired to museum duty in 2022.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHonda
    CountryJapan
    First shown2000
    StatusRetired
    Heightapprox. 130 cm
    Paceapprox. 2.5 m/s run
    Generations2000 to 2011

    ASIMO crowned two decades of Honda's secret P series walking research and toured the world shaking hands with presidents. Honda retired the programme in 2022, folding its balance and actuation research into avatar robots and, eventually, a return announced for its 0 series era.
