    Arachno-Bot

    n., legged robot. Fraunhofer Institute of Manufacturing Engineering and Automation, Germany, 2011. Research platform.

    Eight legged spider inspired robot developed in 2011 at the Fraunhofer Institute of Manufacturing Engineering and Automation in Stuttgart to survey areas too dangerous for people.

    Measures and facts
    MakerFraunhofer Institute of Manufacturing Engineering and Automation
    CountryGermany
    First shown2011
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Each leg uses an electrohydraulic soft joint modeled on a spider's
    • Mostly plastic, produced by 3D printing with selective laser sintering
    • Able to crawl, climb and jump
