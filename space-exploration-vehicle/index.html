    Space Exploration Vehicle

    Also known as SEV, Lunar Electric Rover

    n., rover. NASA, United States, 2008. Prototype.

    NASA pressurised rover prototype on a twelve wheel chassis, tested in Arizona desert campaigns as a mobile habitat for two crew.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNASA
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2008
    StatusPrototype
    ActuationTwelve wheels in six pivoting pairs allowing omnidirectional driving
    PowerRechargeable battery electric drive
    SensorsDriving cameras and navigation displays
    AutonomyCrew driven with a remote teleoperation capability
    Height3 m
    Mass3000 kg
    Length4.5 m
    Top speed10 km/h

    The vehicle pairs a small pressurised cabin with rear mounted suit ports and the Chariot chassis, whose twelve wheels are grouped in pairs that can each pivot through a full circle so the vehicle can crab sideways or turn in place. NASA drove two units on multi day traverses at the Black Point Lava Flow in Arizona during the Desert RATS field campaigns, with crews living aboard for up to two weeks. It remains an engineering prototype, but its cabin and suit port concepts feed current pressurised rover studies.

    Of note

    • Wheel pairs pivot through a full circle for sideways driving
    • Crews lived aboard during two week desert field tests
