    Quadrupeds

    Unitree A1

    n., quadruped. Unitree Robotics, China, 2019. Discontinued.

    Twelve kilogram electric quadruped that brought dynamic legged research hardware down to a price universities could buy in quantity.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUnitree Robotics
    CountryChina
    First shown2019
    StatusDiscontinued
    Mass12 kg
    Top speed3.3 m/s (11.9 km/h)
    Degrees of freedom12
    ActuationIn house brushless motors with planetary gear reduction
    PowerOnboard lithium battery
    SensorsInertial measurement unit, foot contact sensors, optional depth camera
    AutonomyOnboard balance control with a low level developer interface
    Paceapprox. 3.3 m/s

    A1 replaced Laikago with lighter in house actuators, a top speed above three metres per second and a simpler shell. It exposes low level joint torque control through an interface that suits reinforcement learning and controller research, so many locomotion papers between 2020 and 2023 used it. Unitree sold it worldwide at a few thousand dollars before replacing it with the Go1 series.

    Of note

    • Widely used as a low cost research platform for learned locomotion controllers
    • Priced at a small fraction of comparable Western research quadrupeds
