    Ladybird of Szeged

    Also known as Ladybug of Szeged

    n., robot. University of Szeged, Hungary, 1956.

    Early Hungarian robot shaped like a red, black spotted ladybird, built at the University of Szeged to reproduce Pavlovian conditioned reflexes mechanically.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of Szeged
    CountryHungary
    First shown1956

    Of note

    • Built between 1956 and 1957 by Dániel Muszka
    • Regarded as the earliest robotic animal
    • One of the first robots built in Hungary
