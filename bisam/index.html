    Quadrupeds

    BISAM

    n., quadruped. FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik, Germany, 1997. Retired.

    Mammal like four legged walking machine from Karlsruhe, built to study adaptive gait control with an articulated trunk.

    Measures and facts
    MakerFZI Forschungszentrum Informatik
    CountryGermany
    First shown1997
    StatusRetired
    ActuationElectric motors with gear drives
    PowerExternal electrical supply
    AutonomyLayered reflex and gait selection controller

    BISAM followed the body plan of small mammals, with four legs and a jointed trunk that helped it shift weight while walking. Electric motors drove the joints and a layered controller combined reflex like reactions with higher level gait selection so the machine could cross uneven ground. It served as a testbed for biologically inspired control in the German legged locomotion community of the late 1990s.

    Of note

    • Used an actuated spine as well as four legs, unusual for the period
    • Part of the wave of biologically inspired walking machines built in Germany in the 1990s
