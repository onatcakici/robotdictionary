    Baxter

    n., workcell. Rethink Robotics, United States, 2011.

    Two armed collaborative industrial robot from Rethink Robotics, the startup founded by Rodney Brooks, that has an animated face.

    Measures and facts
    MakerRethink Robotics
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2011

    Of note

    • The first unit was completed on 22 September 2011
