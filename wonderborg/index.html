    Quadrupeds

    WonderBorg

    n., quadruped. Bandai, 2000.

    Programmable six legged robot kit shaped like a beetle, released by Bandai in 2000 for use with the WonderSwan and Windows PCs.

    Measures and facts
    MakerBandai
    First shown2000

    Of note

    • Released by Bandai in 2000
    • Has working antennae and six legs, imitating a beetle
