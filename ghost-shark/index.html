    Ghost Shark

    Also known as Ghost Shark AUV, Ghost Shark XL-AUV

    n., underwater robot. Anduril Industries, United States, 2024. In production.

    Uncrewed submarine of the extra large class, built for long range missions and developed by Anduril Industries with Australia's Department of Defence for the Royal Australian Navy.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAnduril Industries
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2024
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • First shown publicly in April 2024
    • Prototypes ordered: three for the Royal Australian Navy, one for the US Navy
    • Announcement of full rate production for Australia came in September 2025
