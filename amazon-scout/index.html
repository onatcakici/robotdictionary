    Amazon Scout

    n., wheeled machine. Amazon, United States, 2019. Retired.

    Six wheeled cooler sized sidewalk delivery robot that Amazon field tested in four United States neighbourhoods before ending the programme in 2022.

    Measures and facts
    MakerAmazon
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2019
    StatusRetired
    ActuationSix wheeled electric drive with a hinged lid cargo compartment
    PowerBattery electric drive
    SensorsCameras and satellite positioning
    AutonomyAutonomous sidewalk route following, accompanied by a human attendant during public trials

    Scout was a six wheeled electric sidewalk robot about the size of a cool box, built to carry parcels the last few hundred metres from a van or depot to a doorstep. It followed mapped sidewalk routes autonomously while a human attendant walked alongside during most public testing. Amazon expanded field tests from Washington state to California, Georgia and Tennessee. The company ended the field tests in October 2022 and reassigned the team.

    Of note

    • First field test launched in Snohomish County, Washington, in January 2019
    • Later tests ran in Irvine, California, Atlanta, Georgia, and Franklin, Tennessee
    • Amazon ended Scout field tests in October 2022, the same month FedEx cancelled its Roxo robot
