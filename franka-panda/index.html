    Arms and workcells

    Panda

    n., robot arm. Franka Emika, Germany, 2017. Discontinued.

    Seven axis arm with torque sensing in every joint, sold at a low price and widely adopted by research laboratories.

    Measures and facts
    MakerFranka Emika
    CountryGermany
    First shown2017
    StatusDiscontinued
    Axes7
    Payload3 kg
    Reach855 mm

    Of note

    • Its designers received the German Future Prize in 2017
    • Succeeded by the Franka Research 3
