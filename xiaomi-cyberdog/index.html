    Quadrupeds

    CyberDog

    n., quadruped. Xiaomi, China, 2021. Discontinued.

    Xiaomi's first quadruped, released as a limited run of one thousand units for developers at 9,999 yuan.

    Measures and facts
    MakerXiaomi
    CountryChina
    First shown2021
    StatusDiscontinued
    Top speed3.2 m/s (11.5 km/h)
    Degrees of freedom12
    ActuationIn house electric servo motors rated about 32 newton metres
    PowerOnboard lithium battery
    SensorsEleven sensors including depth and fisheye cameras, ultrasonic and inertial sensing
    AutonomyOnboard Nvidia Jetson computing with person following and voice control

    CyberDog uses twelve in house servo motors rated at about thirty two newton metres, an Nvidia Jetson Xavier NX computer and eleven sensors including a depth camera and ultra wide angle fisheye lenses. Xiaomi published a developer interface and shipped the robot to engineers and enthusiasts rather than general consumers, positioning it as an open platform. It trotted at over three metres per second, followed a person and performed backflips, and was replaced by the smaller CyberDog 2 in 2023.

    Of note

    • Sold as one thousand units at 9,999 yuan to developers in August 2021
    • Shipped with an open developer interface rather than as a finished consumer product
