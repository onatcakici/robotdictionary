    Underwater and marine

    Argo

    n., marine robot. Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution.

    Uncrewed camera sled towed deep underwater, developed by Robert Ballard at Woods Hole's Deep Submergence Laboratory, that helped find the wreck of the Titanic in 1985.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWoods Hole Oceanographic Institution

    Of note

    • Also helped locate the wreck of the Bismarck in 1989
