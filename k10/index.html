    Rovers

    K10

    Also known as K-10 robot

    n., rover. Intelligent Robotics Group, United States. Prototype.

    Family of prototype planetary rovers from NASA's Intelligent Robotics Group, with third generation units using four wheel drive and all wheel steering.

    Measures and facts
    MakerIntelligent Robotics Group
    CountryUnited States
    StatusPrototype

    Of note

    • Third generation units have a passive averaging suspension to reduce jolting on uneven ground
    • Mounting points front, back and bottom take antennas, sensors and instruments
    • Its controller runs on a Linux laptop linked by 802.11g or Tropos mesh wireless
