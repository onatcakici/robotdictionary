Robot Dictionary. Quadrupeds

Doggo

n., quadruped.

Stanford University, United States, 2019. An open-source hopping quadruped built for under $3,000 in parts. Retired.

Doggo was introduced by Stanford University of United States and first filed in this dictionary under the year 2019. It belongs to the quadruped class, four-legged machines that go where wheels cannot. Its working life has ended; it is recorded here as part of the historical register.
