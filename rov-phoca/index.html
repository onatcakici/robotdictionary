    Underwater and marine

    ROV PHOCA

    n., underwater robot. Sub-Atlantic, United Kingdom. In service.

    Compact remotely operated underwater vehicle built by Sub-Atlantic in Aberdeen and owned by the GEOMAR ocean research centre in Kiel, able to dive to 3000 m.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSub-Atlantic
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • A COMANCHE type ROV
    • Small enough to deploy from mid sized research ships
    • Designed for work class and intervention tasks
