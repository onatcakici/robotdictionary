    VolcanoBot 2

    Also known as VolcanoBot

    n., rover. NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, United States, 2015. Retired.

    Small two wheeled tethered robot built to crawl into volcanic fissures at Kilauea and map their inner shape in three dimensions.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2015
    StatusRetired
    ActuationTwo wheels with a tether for power, data and recovery
    PowerSupplied through the tether
    SensorsCamera and depth sensor for three dimensional mapping
    AutonomyTeleoperated from the surface

    The VolcanoBots are hand sized robots with two large wheels and a tether, designed to be lowered into cracks far too narrow and hot for a person. VolcanoBot 1 descended 25 metres into a fissure near the Pu'u O'o crater on Kilauea in 2014, and the smaller, lighter VolcanoBot 2 returned in 2015 with better cameras and a more capable depth sensor. The three dimensional models they produced help volcanologists understand how magma moves through the plumbing of a volcano.

    Of note

    • Descended 25 metres into a Kilauea fissure in 2014
    • Produced three dimensional maps of fissure interiors
