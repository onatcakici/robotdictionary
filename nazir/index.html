    Wheeled machines

    Nazir

    Also known as Nazir (military robot)

    n., wheeled machine. Islamic Republic of Iran Army Ground Forces, Iran, 2015.

    Remote controlled armed robot built by Iran's Army Ground Forces that carries two missiles and about 600 kg of cargo, first unveiled in September 2015.

    Measures and facts
    MakerIslamic Republic of Iran Army Ground Forces
    CountryIran
    First shown2015

    Of note

    • Unveiled on 12 September 2015
    • Carries two missiles and about 600 kg of cargo
    • Can carry out missions using instant planning and control
