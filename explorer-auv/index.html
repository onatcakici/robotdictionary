    Underwater and marine

    Explorer AUV

    n., underwater robot. China, 1994.

    Chinese autonomous underwater vehicle that entered service in November 1994 and supplied the experience behind many later Chinese designs.

    Measures and facts
    CountryChina
    First shown1994

    Of note

    • Later AUVs such as Wukong and the Sea Whale series built on its experience
    • Distinct from the American ABE and the Canadian Explorer AUV
