    El Ajedrecista

    n., automaton. Leonardo Torres Quevedo, Spain, 1912. Retired.

    Chess automaton created by the Spanish engineer Leonardo Torres Quevedo that played out a reduced endgame with three pieces entirely on its own.

    Measures and facts
    MakerLeonardo Torres Quevedo
    CountrySpain
    First shown1912
    StatusRetired

    Of note

    • Shown publicly in 1913 and exhibited in Paris in 1914
    • A smaller electromechanical second version was finished around 1920
