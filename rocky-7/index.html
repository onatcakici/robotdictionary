    Rovers

    Rocky 7

    n., rover. NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, United States, 1996. Retired.

    JPL research microrover that added a camera mast and sampling arm to the Sojourner class design, field tested in the Mojave Desert.

    Measures and facts
    MakerNASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory
    CountryUnited States
    First shown1996
    StatusRetired
    ActuationSix wheel rocker bogie drive with two steered wheels
    PowerSolar panel with battery
    SensorsDeployable camera mast, stereo cameras, sampling arm and scoop
    AutonomyWaypoint driving with onboard hazard detection

    Rocky 7 was a rocker bogie microrover used to prototype the science operations later flown on the Mars Exploration Rovers, including a deployable mast for panoramic imaging and a small arm with a scoop. Engineers ran it at Lavic Lake and other Mojave Desert sites, letting remote science teams plan each day's activity from returned images alone. The trials shaped how mission teams sequence a rover day and how much a rover can achieve between contacts.

    Of note

    • Prototyped mast based science operations later used on Mars
    • Used for remote science team field trials in the Mojave Desert
