    Leonardo

    n., robot. Massachusetts Institute of Technology, United States, 2002. Research platform.

    Social robot standing 2.5 feet tall, built by MIT's Personal Robots Group to study interaction and collaboration between humans and robots.

    Measures and facts
    MakerMassachusetts Institute of Technology
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2002
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Body built by Stan Winston Studios and completed in 2002
    • Created under the direction of Cynthia Breazeal
    • Named in Wired magazine's 50 Best Robots Ever list in 2006
