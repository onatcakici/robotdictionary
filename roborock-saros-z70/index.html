    Wheeled machines

    Saros Z70

    Also known as Roborock Saros Z70

    n., wheeled machine. Roborock, China, 2025. In production.

    Robot vacuum with a folding five axis arm that lifts small objects such as socks out of its path before it cleans.

    Measures and facts
    MakerRoborock
    CountryChina
    First shown2025
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Shown at CES in January 2025
    • The arm folds away inside the body when not in use
