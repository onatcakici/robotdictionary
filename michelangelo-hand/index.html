    Michelangelo Hand

    n., medical robot. Ottobock and Advanced Arm Dynamics, Germany, 2010. In production.

    Robotic prosthetic hand with full articulation made by the German company Ottobock with its American partner Advanced Arm Dynamics, the first prosthesis with an electronically driven thumb.

    Measures and facts
    MakerOttobock and Advanced Arm Dynamics
    CountryGermany
    First shown2010
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • First fitted to an Austrian elective amputee in July 2010
    • Used by military and civilian amputees in the United States and United Kingdom since 2011
    • Its thumb movement imitates that of a natural human hand
