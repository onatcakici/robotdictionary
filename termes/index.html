    TERMES

    n., robot. Harvard University, 2014. Prototype.

    Team of termite inspired autonomous robots from Harvard University that build structures from blocks by following simple rules, with no central controller, first shown in 2014.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHarvard University
    First shown2014
    StatusPrototype

    Of note

    • Prototype team demonstrated by Harvard researchers in 2014
    • Developed over four years
    • Coordinate through stigmergy rather than constant human supervision
