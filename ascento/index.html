Robot Dictionary. Wheeled machines

Ascento Guard

n., wheeled machine.

Ascento, Switzerland, 2023. A two-wheeled jumping security robot balancing on legs with wheels for feet. In pilot deployments.

Ascento Guard was introduced by Ascento of Switzerland and first filed in this dictionary under the year 2023. It belongs to the wheeled class, machines that trade legs for range and endurance. At the time of this volume it works in pilot deployments rather than open sale.
