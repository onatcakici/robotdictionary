    Humanoids

    iCub

    n., humanoid. Italian Institute of Technology, Italy, 2008. Research platform.

    A child sized open source humanoid distributed to dozens of labs to study embodied cognition.

    Measures and facts
    MakerItalian Institute of Technology
    CountryItaly
    First shown2008
    StatusResearch platform
    Heightapprox. 104 cm

    iCub is Europe's shared humanoid child: over fifty copies in labs worldwide study how embodied minds learn to see, grasp and speak, all on an open source body.
