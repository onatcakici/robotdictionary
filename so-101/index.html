    Arms and workcells

    SO-101

    Also known as SO ARM101, SO-100

    n., robot arm. Hugging Face and TheRobotStudio, 2025. In production.

    Low cost, 3D printable open source robot arm, built in leader and follower pairs for teaching robots by demonstration with the LeRobot software.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHugging Face and TheRobotStudio
    First shown2025
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Successor to the SO-100 arm of 2024
    • Its design files and code are published openly

    Sources

    1. Hugging Face launches the SO-101, an upgraded low cost 3D printable autonomous robot arm, Hackster, hackster.io
