    Underwater and marine

    Pluto Plus

    n., marine robot. Gaymarine, Italy.

    Battery powered unmanned underwater vehicle from Italian firm Gaymarine, used by armed forces to identify and destroy sea mines, with a top speed of 6 kt.

    Measures and facts
    MakerGaymarine
    CountryItaly

    Of note

    • Top speed of 6 kt and endurance of 2 to 6 hours
    • Can be fitted with a wireless link for counterterrorism work
    • Built under exclusive licence in the United States by the Columbia Group
