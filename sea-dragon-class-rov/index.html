    Underwater and marine

    Sea Dragon class ROV

    n., underwater robot. China. In service.

    Series of Chinese work class remotely operated vehicles for oil platform servicing, salvage and rescue, all derived from the original Sea Dragon.

    Measures and facts
    CountryChina
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • The original Sea Dragon could dive to 3,500 meters
    • Later models were adapted to different operating conditions
