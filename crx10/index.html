    Wheeled machines

    CRX10

    n., wheeled machine. CNRobot, South Korea, 2011.

    Self navigating mobile robot platform from South Korean company CNRobot, intended for teaching, training and research and first shown in May 2011.

    Measures and facts
    MakerCNRobot
    CountrySouth Korea
    First shown2011

    Of note

    • Unveiled in May 2011
    • Can be programmed with MSRDS, SPL, AVR and Android
