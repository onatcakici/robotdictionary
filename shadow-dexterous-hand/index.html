    Shadow Dexterous Hand

    n., robot hand. Shadow Robot Company, United Kingdom. In production.

    Dexterous robot hand from the Shadow Robot Company in London, built to human hand size and shape with the same full set of degrees of freedom.

    Measures and facts
    MakerShadow Robot Company
    CountryUnited Kingdom
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Sold in pneumatic and electric versions
    • Used by NASA, Bielefeld University and Carnegie Mellon University
    • Used in EU research projects including HANDLE
