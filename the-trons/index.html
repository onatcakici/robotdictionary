    The Trons

    n., robot. New Zealand, 2008.

    Robot band of four machines that play music by themselves, built from spare parts and electronics by Hamilton based musician Greg Locke.

    Measures and facts
    CountryNew Zealand
    First shown2008

    Of note

    • Based in Hamilton, New Zealand
    • Made up of four robots built from scratch
    • Named after Hamilton's nickname, "The Tron"
