    Underwater and marine

    SPURV

    n., underwater robot. University of Washington Applied Physics Laboratory, 1957. Retired.

    Autonomous underwater research vehicle that the University of Washington's Applied Physics Laboratory built in 1957, becoming the US Navy's first AUV.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of Washington Applied Physics Laboratory
    First shown1957
    StatusRetired

    Of note

    • Development was funded by the US Office of Naval Research
    • The navy used seven SPURVs until 1979
