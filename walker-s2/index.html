Walker S2

Industrial humanoid that swaps its own battery in about three minutes, sold to Chinese carmakers and electronics plants for continuous shift work.

MakerUBTECH Robotics
CountryChina
First shown2025
StatusIn production
Payload15 kg
ActuationElectric joints with a high-torque waist servo, plus autonomous battery exchange
PowerDual dynamically balanced battery packs with autonomous swap in about three minutes
SensorsBinocular stereo vision with RGB cameras
AutonomyUBTECH fleet coordination software for multiple robots on a production line

Walker S2 is UBTECH's second-generation industrial humanoid, built around a dual-battery system that keeps the robot powered while it exchanges a depleted pack, giving effectively continuous operation. It lifts 15 kilograms, works from floor level to 1.8 metres for stoop lifting, and its high-torque waist servo rotates plus or minus 162 degrees and pitches 125 degrees. Binocular stereo vision with RGB cameras handles part location, and UBTECH's fleet software coordinates groups of robots on a line. UBTECH has announced large order books from Chinese automotive and electronics manufacturers, and earlier Walker S1 fleets worked at Zeekr and Foxconn sites.

Of note

  • Swaps its own battery in about three minutes for continuous operation
  • Predecessor Walker S1 fleets deployed at Zeekr and Foxconn plants
  • UBTECH has reported some of the largest disclosed humanoid order books in China
