    Wheeled machines

    RAVON

    n., wheeled machine. University of Kaiserslautern, Germany. Research platform.

    Off road research vehicle from the Robotics Research Lab at the University of Kaiserslautern, used to test behaviour based navigation and localisation on uneven outdoor ground.

    Measures and facts
    MakerUniversity of Kaiserslautern
    CountryGermany
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Built on a base vehicle produced by Robosoft
    • Serves as a testbed for motion adaptation, localisation and navigation research
