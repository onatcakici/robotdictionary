    Rovers

    MINERVA

    n., rover. JAXA, 2005. Mission completed.

    Series of Japanese rovers built by JAXA to explore the surfaces of asteroids, carried on the Hayabusa and Hayabusa2 missions.

    Measures and facts
    MakerJAXA
    First shown2005
    StatusMission completed

    Of note

    • First MINERVA missed asteroid Itokawa in November 2005 and drifted into solar orbit
    • Two MINERVA-II rovers landed on asteroid Ryugu on 21 September 2018
    • Third MINERVA-II unit malfunctioned but was released in October 2019 for gravity measurements
