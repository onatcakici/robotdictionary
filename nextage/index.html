NEXTAGE

Dual-arm humanoid torso sold for precision cell production, working at benches alongside people in electronics and precision assembly plants.

MakerKawada Robotics
CountryJapan
First shown2011
StatusIn production
Degrees of freedom15
ActuationElectric servo joints in two six-axis arms, a two-axis head and a rotating waist
PowerMains powered controller cabinet
SensorsStereo head cameras and hand-mounted cameras for part location
AutonomyTask programming with vision-based part detection and workstation calibration

NEXTAGE is a 15-axis humanoid made up of a two-camera head, a rotating waist and two six-axis arms, mounted on a bench stand or a movable cart rather than legs. It is designed to drop into workstations built for people, so a line can be converted without re-tooling, and its head and hand cameras let it find parts whose position varies. Kawada supplies it with grippers and vision tools for electronics assembly, inspection and machine tending. It has been sold in Japan and exported since 2011 and descends from Kawada's earlier humanoid platform engineering.

Of note

  • Designed to occupy human workstations without re-tooling the production line
  • Sold for electronics assembly, inspection and machine tending in Japan and abroad
  • Grew out of Kawada's earlier humanoid platform engineering
