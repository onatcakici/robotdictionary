    Humanoids

    Furhat

    n., humanoid. Furhat Robotics, Sweden. In production.

    Conversational social robot from the Swedish company Furhat Robotics, with a humanlike face projected from behind and software for speech recognition and synthesis.

    Measures and facts
    MakerFurhat Robotics
    CountrySweden
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Used in education, healthcare, recruitment and transport
    • Employed in social robotics studies at several universities
