Robot Dictionary. Rovers

ATHLETE

n., rover.

NASA JPL, United States, 2009. A six-limbed wheel-legged lunar cargo hauler that rolls or walks as terrain demands. A research platform.

ATHLETE was introduced by NASA JPL of United States and first filed in this dictionary under the year 2009. It belongs to the small company of rovers, machines sent to drive on other worlds. It serves as a research platform rather than a commercial product.
