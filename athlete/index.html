ATHLETE

Also known as All Terrain Hex Limbed Extra Terrestrial Explorer

Six limbed JPL vehicle with a wheel on each limb, able to roll over easy ground and walk or climb over obstacles.

MakerNASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory
CountryUnited States
Launched2008
StatusResearch platform
ActuationSix limbs, each with multiple joints and a driven wheel
PowerBattery electric with external supply during trials
SensorsStereo cameras with limb force sensing
AutonomyTeleoperated with autonomous stepping and docking routines

Each of ATHLETE's six limbs has several joints and ends in a driven wheel, so the vehicle can drive like a rover, walk like a legged machine, or use a limb as a manipulator to handle cargo. JPL built successive versions with partners including Boeing and Stanford and demonstrated them carrying a mock habitat module at field sites such as Moses Lake in Washington and the Black Point Lava Flow in Arizona. The concept targeted transport of large lunar payloads and docking of pressurised modules.

Of note

  • Wheels on limbs let it drive, walk and manipulate cargo
  • Field tested carrying a habitat mock up in Washington and Arizona

Maker: www.jpl.nasa.gov
