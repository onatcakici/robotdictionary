Robot Dictionary. Humanoids

GR-2

n., humanoid.

Fourier Intelligence, China, 2024. Fourier's second generation, with greater dexterity and an opened developer platform. In production.

First recorded in 2024, GR-2 is the work of Fourier Intelligence, China. It belongs to the humanoid class, machines built to work in spaces made for people. It is offered commercially and remains in production at the time of this volume.

See also GR-1, GR-3.
