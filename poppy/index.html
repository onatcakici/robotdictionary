    Humanoids

    Poppy

    n., humanoid. Inria Flowers Laboratory, France, 2013. Research platform.

    Open source 3D printed humanoid from Inria used for developmental robotics experiments and widely adopted in schools and universities.

    Measures and facts
    MakerInria Flowers Laboratory
    CountryFrance
    First shown2013
    StatusResearch platform
    Height0.84 m
    Mass3.5 kg
    Degrees of freedom25
    ActuationServo motors with compliant control on a 3D printed skeleton
    AutonomyOpen source Python control library

    Poppy Humanoid is about 84 cm tall, weighs 3.5 kg and has 25 degrees of freedom built from servo motors and a 3D printed skeleton, including a multi part articulated spine and slightly bent thigh bones that improve walking. All mechanical files, electronics and the Python control library are published under open licences so users can print variant bodies and test how morphology changes behaviour. Inria's Flowers team introduced it in 2013 as the first complete 3D printed open source humanoid, and it has since been used in classrooms, art projects and cognitive robotics studies.

    Of note

    • First complete open source 3D printed humanoid platform
    • Used in schools, universities and art projects worldwide
