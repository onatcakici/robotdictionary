    DIY Kindle Scanner

    n., robot. 2012.

    Lego Mindstorms machine built by Peter Purgathofer that works with a computer and a Kindle reader to copy electronic books, sidestepping their copy protection.

    Measures and facts
    First shown2012

    Of note

    • Designed and built between 2012 and 2013
