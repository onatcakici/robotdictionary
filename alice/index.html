    Alice

    n., robot. École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Switzerland, 1998. Research platform.

    Tiny cube shaped mobile robot measuring 2 by 2 by 2 cm, created at the Autonomous Systems Lab of the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne.

    Measures and facts
    MakerÉcole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne
    CountrySwitzerland
    First shown1998
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Work on it ran from 1998 to 2004 in Lausanne, Switzerland
    • Has belonged to the Institute of Robotics and Intelligent Systems in Zürich since 2006
