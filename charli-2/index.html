    Humanoids

    CHARLI-2

    Also known as CHARLI

    n., humanoid. Virginia Tech, Robotics and Mechanisms Laboratory, United States, 2011. Retired.

    Lightweight full size humanoid built by students at Virginia Tech that won the adult size soccer competition at RoboCup 2011.

    Measures and facts
    MakerVirginia Tech, Robotics and Mechanisms Laboratory
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2011
    StatusRetired
    Height1.41 m
    Mass12.1 kg
    Degrees of freedom25
    Actuation32 direct current motors
    AutonomyFully autonomous during matches with onboard vision and gait control

    CHARLI-2 is 141 cm tall yet weighs only about 12 kg, built from aluminium alloy, titanium and carbon fibre with 25 degrees of freedom driven by direct current motors. It walks with a compliant gait, kicks a ball, tracks the field with an onboard camera and shifts posture to absorb pushes, working fully autonomously during matches. Dennis Hong's laboratory won the RoboCup 2011 adult size humanoid league and the Louis Vuitton Best Humanoid Award with it in Istanbul.

    Of note

    • Won the RoboCup 2011 adult size humanoid league
    • Received the Louis Vuitton Best Humanoid Award in 2011
