    Humanoids

    Actroid

    Also known as Repliee

    n., android. Kokoro, Japan, 2003. In production.

    Highly lifelike android created at Osaka University and produced by Kokoro, usually given the appearance of a young Japanese woman.

    Measures and facts
    MakerKokoro
    CountryJapan
    First shown2003
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Debuted at Tokyo's International Robot Exhibition in 2003
    • Several versions have been produced since its debut
