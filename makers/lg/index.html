    Robots by LG recorded in the dictionary: 3 entries, South Korea.

    LG Roboking n., wheeled machine. LG, 2001. Line of robotic vacuum cleaners made by LG, sold as Roboking in Korea and under the Hom-Bot name in other markets. In production.

    CLOi ServeBot n., wheeled machine. LG, South Korea, 2020. LG's shelf carrying restaurant and building courier line. In production.

    Q9 n., wheeled machine. LG, South Korea, 2025. LG's two legged wheeled home agent that emotes through a screen face. Announced, not yet in service.
