    Unitree Robotics

    Robots by Unitree Robotics recorded in the dictionary: 10 entries, China.

    Laikago n., quadruped. Unitree Robotics, China, 2017. First commercial quadruped from Unitree and the machine that opened the low cost Chinese robot dog market. Discontinued.

    Unitree A1 n., quadruped. Unitree Robotics, China, 2019. Twelve kilogram electric quadruped that brought dynamic legged research hardware down to a price universities could buy in quantity. Discontinued.

    Unitree Go1 n., quadruped. Unitree Robotics, China, 2021. Consumer priced electric quadruped that Unitree marketed as a walking companion, starting at around two thousand seven hundred dollars. Discontinued.

    Unitree B2 n., quadruped. Unitree Robotics, China, 2023. Sixty kilogram industrial quadruped from Unitree rated to walk with forty kilograms of payload for more than four hours. In production.

    Unitree Go2 n., quadruped. Unitree Robotics, China, 2023. Fifteen kilogram consumer and developer quadruped with a hemispherical lidar, sold from well under two thousand US dollars. In production.

    Unitree H1 n., humanoid. Unitree Robotics, China, 2023. Full size electric biped sold at a fraction of Western prices, notable for a fast recorded walking speed and for widespread laboratory adoption. In production.

    Unitree G1 n., humanoid. Unitree Robotics, China, 2024. Compact humanoid priced from around 13,500 dollars, sold in volume to developers, universities and companies as an affordable locomotion and manipulation platform. In production.

    Unitree H2 n., humanoid. Unitree Robotics, China, 2025. Unitree's flagship full size humanoid with more human proportions and 31 joints, aimed at performance, service and research customers. In production.

    Unitree R1 n., humanoid. Unitree Robotics, China, 2025. Humanoid robot introduced by the Chinese company Unitree Robotics in 2025, standing 123 cm tall.

    GD01 n., exoskeleton. Unitree Robotics, China. Large powered exoskeleton made by the Chinese company Unitree Robotics, standing 2.7 m tall with a mass of 500 kg.
