    ROBOTIS

    Robots by ROBOTIS recorded in the dictionary: 4 entries, South Korea.

    DARwIn-OP n., humanoid. ROBOTIS, South Korea, 2010. The open platform mini humanoid that carried university RoboCup teams for a decade. Retired.

    ROBOTIS OP3 n., humanoid. ROBOTIS, South Korea, 2017. Small open platform biped sold worldwide for education and research, built from the maker's Dynamixel smart servos and used in humanoid soccer leagues. In production.

    TurtleBot3 n., wheeled machine. ROBOTIS, South Korea, 2017. Small open source research and teaching robot with a spinning laser scanner, used in university courses and labs to learn mapping and navigation. In production.

    Robotis Bioloid n., robot kit. Robotis, South Korea. Robot kit for hobbyists and education from the South Korean company Robotis, using modular servo units that combine into wheeled, legged or humanoid machines.
