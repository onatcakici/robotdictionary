    Hanson Robotics

    Robots by Hanson Robotics recorded in the dictionary: 5 entries, Hong Kong.

    Albert HUBO n., humanoid. Hanson Robotics, Hong Kong, 2005. Walking humanoid robot that mounts a head on a full size two legged frame, based on the HUBO biped from the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology.

    Philip K. Dick Android n., android. Hanson Robotics, Hong Kong, 2005. Humanlike android modeled on the science fiction author Philip K. Dick, built by Hanson Robotics in 2005 and able to converse using a model trained on his writings.

    BINA48 n., humanoid. Hanson Robotics, Hong Kong, 2010. Humanoid robot head with chatbot software for basic conversation, built by Hanson Robotics in 2010 and made to look like Bina Aspen.

    Han n., humanoid. Hanson Robotics, Hong Kong, 2015. Social humanoid robot from the Hong Kong firm Hanson Robotics, built mostly as a head with Frubber skin that senses people and gauges their age, gender and emotional expression.

    Sophia n., humanoid. Hanson Robotics, Hong Kong, 2016. Expressive interaction humanoid known for public appearances and media work, produced in small numbers and supplied to research, education and events. In production.
