    Boston Dynamics

    Robots by Boston Dynamics recorded in the dictionary: 12 entries, United States.

    RHex n., legged robot. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2001. Six legged robot with passive stability whose compliant legs are each driven by one actuator, developed with US university partners and built by Boston Dynamics. Research platform.

    BigDog n., quadruped. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2005. The gasoline engined pack mule whose recovery from a kick announced the legged robot age. Retired.

    LittleDog n., quadruped. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2005. Small electric quadruped supplied to university teams as the shared hardware of the DARPA Learning Locomotion programme. Retired.

    PETMAN n., humanoid. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2011. An anthropomorphic test rig that marched in chemical suits to trial protective clothing. Retired.

    Cheetah n., quadruped. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2012. Tethered hydraulic quadruped built for DARPA that set the land speed record for a legged robot on a laboratory treadmill. Retired.

    Atlas (hydraulic) n., humanoid. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2013. The hydraulic research humanoid whose parkour and backflips defined a decade of legged robotics. Retired.

    WildCat n., quadruped. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2013. Untethered petrol powered quadruped built to run fast in the open, the free running successor to the treadmill bound Cheetah. Retired.

    Spot Classic n., quadruped. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2015. The hydraulic middleweight between BigDog and today's Spot. Retired.

    Spot n., quadruped. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2016. Electric inspection quadruped, the machine that turned legged robots into an off the shelf industrial product. In production.

    Handle n., humanoid. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2017. A wheel legged biped that leapt and lifted boxes; its logistics role passed to Stretch. Retired.

    Stretch n., wheeled machine. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2021. A box moving mobile arm unloading trucks one carton per second at DHL. In production.

    Atlas (electric) n., humanoid. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2024. The all electric successor to the hydraulic Atlas; unveiled for enterprise material handling at CES 2026. In pilot deployments.
