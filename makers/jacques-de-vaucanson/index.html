    Jacques de Vaucanson

    Robots by Jacques de Vaucanson recorded in the dictionary: 2 entries, France.

    Vaucanson Flute Player n., automaton. Jacques de Vaucanson, 1738. Android automaton built by Jacques de Vaucanson that played a standard transverse flute of the period, imitating a human flautist; it was first exhibited in 1738.

    Digesting Duck n., automaton. Jacques de Vaucanson, France, 1739. Mechanical duck built by Jacques de Vaucanson that seemed to eat grain and excrete it, although the droppings were stored in advance and nothing was actually digested.
