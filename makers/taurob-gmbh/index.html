    Taurob GmbH

    Robots by Taurob GmbH recorded in the dictionary: 2 entries, Austria.

    Taurob Inspector n., wheeled machine. Taurob GmbH, Austria. Mobile robot certified under ATEX rules, made by the Austrian firm Taurob for offshore oil and gas sites and derived from its Taurob Tracker.

    Taurob Tracker n., wheeled machine. Taurob GmbH, Austria. Austrian remote controlled ground robot made by Taurob, first developed for fire brigade reconnaissance and now also used by armed forces, universities and oil and gas companies. In production.
