    WowWee

    Robots by WowWee recorded in the dictionary: 13 entries, Canada.

    Roboraptor n., quadruped. WowWee, 2004. Walking toy robot sold by WowWee, invented in 2004 by Mark Tilden and Michael Bellantoni, that can roam on its own using three lifelike gaits.

    Robosapien n., humanoid. WowWee, 2004. Biomorphic toy robot created by Mark Tilden and made by WowWee, released in 2004 with preset moves and infrared remote control.

    Roboreptile n., quadruped. WowWee, 2006. Four legged reptilian toy robot from WowWee that uses infrared and sound sensors to explore on its own while steering clear of obstacles.

    Roboquad n., quadruped. WowWee, 2007. Four legged toy robot from WowWee, designed by Mark Tilden and announced at CES in January 2007, looking partly like a spider and partly like a dog.

    FemiSapien n., humanoid. WowWee, 2008. Female humanoid toy robot from WowWee that responds to what it sees, hears and feels, and accepts programmed sequences of movements.

    MiP n., wheeled machine. WowWee, Canada, 2014. Self balancing toy robot that rides on two wheels, carries a small tray and responds to hand gestures. Discontinued.

    Fingerlings n., robot. WowWee, 2017. Line of small robotic toy creatures from WowWee that cling to a finger and blink or blow kisses when they sense touch or sound.

    CHiP n., wheeled machine. WowWee, Canada. Toy robot dog that rolls on spherical wheels and follows its owner using a wristband. Discontinued.

    Roboboa n., robot. WowWee. Robotic snake made by WowWee with four angled body sections that rotate against one another so it can coil up.

    Robopanda n., robot. WowWee. Interactive robotic companion toy from WowWee that loads personalities, stories and songs from swappable cartridges and reacts to touch and sound.

    Rovio n., wheeled machine. WowWee. Wireless networked robot made by WowWee that carries a webcam, letting its user watch the camera feed and drive it remotely over the internet.

    RS Media n., humanoid. WowWee. Biomorphic robot from WowWee built on the Robosapien V2 body, running Linux based software and able to record and replay sound, photos and video.

    WowWee Alive Chimpanzee n., robot. WowWee. Animatronic chimpanzee head from the WowWee Alive division of WowWee that uses motors and sensors to copy a live chimpanzee's facial expressions.
