    Tokyo Institute of Technology

    Robots by Tokyo Institute of Technology recorded in the dictionary: 4 entries, Japan.

    ACM III n., robot. Tokyo Institute of Technology, Japan, 1972. Snake robot built by Shigeo Hirose, among the first machines to crawl by undulating its body like a real snake. Retired.

    KUMO-I n., quadruped. Tokyo Institute of Technology, Japan, 1976. Spider inspired research quadruped by Shigeo Hirose and Yoji Umetani, the first machine in the long walking robot programme at Tokyo Institute of Technology. Retired.

    TITAN VIII n., quadruped. Tokyo Institute of Technology, Japan, 1996. Compact wire driven quadruped from the Hirose laboratory, built as a general purpose walking platform for research and teaching. Retired.

    TITAN XI n., quadruped. Tokyo Institute of Technology, Japan, 2004. Seven tonne hydraulic quadruped built to drill anchor holes on steep slopes, among the largest walking machines ever completed. Retired.
