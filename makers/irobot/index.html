    iRobot

    Robots by iRobot recorded in the dictionary: 13 entries, United States.

    PackBot n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2002. Tracked man portable ground robot used by military and emergency teams, including radiation surveys inside the Fukushima Daiichi buildings. In service.

    Roomba n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2002. Disc shaped domestic vacuum robot that cleans floors on its own and returns to a dock, the product that created the home robot market. In production.

    Scooba n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2005. Floor washing robot from iRobot that sprayed, scrubbed and squeegeed hard floors. Discontinued.

    iRobot Create n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2007. Hobbyist robot made by iRobot from its Roomba vacuum platform, with the cleaning hardware replaced by a cargo bay for robotics development work.

    Braava n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2013. Roomba's quiet mopping sibling for hard floors. In production.

    Ava 500 n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2014. Autonomous office telepresence robot from iRobot that drove itself to a chosen meeting room, using video equipment from Cisco. Discontinued.

    Roomba 980 n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2015. First Roomba to map rooms with a camera and connect to a phone app. Discontinued.

    Create 3 n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2021. The Roomba stripped for parts and sold as an education research base. In production.

    Roomba j7 n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States, 2021. Roomba model with a front camera that recognises cords, socks and pet waste and steers around them. In production.

    FirstLook n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States. Throwable unmanned ground robot built by the American company iRobot and listed as military equipment.

    Looj n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States. Domestic robot made by the American consumer robot company iRobot, the firm behind the Roomba robotic vacuum cleaners.

    Root n., wheeled machine. iRobot, United States. Coding robot that drives on whiteboards, drawing and erasing as it runs a program, developed at Harvard's Wyss Institute and later sold by iRobot. In production.

    XM1216 SUGV n., wheeled machine. iRobot. Small, man portable military ground robot derived from iRobot's PackBot and built for the Future Combat Systems programme.
