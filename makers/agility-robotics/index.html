    Agility Robotics

    Robots by Agility Robotics recorded in the dictionary: 2 entries, United States.

    Cassie n., humanoid. Agility Robotics, United States, 2017. A pair of bird legs with no torso; set a bipedal 100 metre record and begat Digit. Retired.

    Digit n., humanoid. Agility Robotics, United States, 2019. A warehouse humanoid built for the handling of totes; among the first of its kind to work paid shifts, logging over one hundred thousand cycles. In production.
