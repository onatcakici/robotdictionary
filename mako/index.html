    Medical and surgical

    Mako

    n., medical robot. Stryker, United States, 2006. In production.

    Robotic arm system that guides surgeons cutting bone for knee and hip replacements according to a plan made from a CT scan.

    Measures and facts
    MakerStryker
    CountryUnited States
    First shown2006
    StatusIn production

    Of note

    • Stryker acquired the original maker, MAKO Surgical, in 2013
