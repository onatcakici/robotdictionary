    Tobor

    n., robot. Schaper Toys, United States, 1978.

    Toy robot made in 1978 by Schaper Toys that responded to loud sounds or to a control box through the company's telesonic remote system.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSchaper Toys
    CountryUnited States
    First shown1978

    Of note

    • Stood about nine inches high
    • Among the first toys with an inexpensive remote control
    • Released to cash in on the science fiction craze
