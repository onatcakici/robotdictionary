    IceMole

    n., robot. FH Aachen, Germany, 2010. Research platform.

    Autonomous, steerable ice probe developed at FH Aachen in Germany that melts and screws its way through ice for polar, glacier and space research.

    Measures and facts
    MakerFH Aachen
    CountryGermany
    First shown2010
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • Steerable while melting through ice, and retrievable once its work is done
    • Uses a driving ice screw to push through soil layers and debris in the ice
