    Sojourner

    Also known as Mars Pathfinder rover, MFEX

    n., rover. NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, United States, 1996. Mission completed.

    Six wheeled microrover of the Mars Pathfinder mission, the first wheeled vehicle to drive on another planet, operating in Ares Vallis.

    Sojourner rolled off the Pathfinder lander on 6 July 1997 and worked in Ares Vallis, an ancient flood plain on Mars. Solar panels and non rechargeable lithium batteries powered six independently driven wheels on a rocker bogie chassis, and an alpha proton X ray spectrometer measured rock chemistry. It stayed within a few tens of metres of the lander, which relayed all of its data to Earth, and it operated for 83 sols against a planned seven.

    Sojourner, named for Sojourner Truth, was the first rover on Mars, creeping a hundred metres around the Pathfinder lander in 1997 while the early web watched daily photos arrive.

    Of note

    • First wheeled robot to drive on another planet
    • Operated 83 sols against a planned seven sol mission
    • Returned 550 images and 16 rock and soil chemistry measurements
