    Wheeled machines

    Modular Advanced Armed Robotic System

    Also known as MAARS

    n., wheeled machine. QinetiQ. In development.

    Armed military ground robot developed by QinetiQ as part of the TALON family, intended to succeed the SWORDS robot on a larger chassis.

    Measures and facts
    MakerQinetiQ
    StatusIn development

    Of note

    • Member of the TALON robot family
    • Intended to replace the armed SWORDS robot
    • Shares little hardware with SWORDS and TALON because of its larger chassis
