      Robot of the day

      Digesting Duck

      n., robot. Jacques de Vaucanson, France.

      Mechanical duck built by Jacques de Vaucanson that seemed to eat grain and excrete it, although the droppings were stored in advance and nothing was actually digested.

      Grain went into one hidden container while prepared droppings came from another; Vaucanson hoped a genuinely digesting machine might be built someday
