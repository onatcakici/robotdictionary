      Robot of the day

      Stretch

      n., wheeled machine. Boston Dynamics, United States, 2021. In production.

      A box moving mobile arm unloading trucks one carton per second at DHL.

      Stretch pairs a suction arm with a mobile base to unload trucks, the job warehouse workers most want automated. DHL signed for over a thousand units, Boston Dynamics' first volume commercial contract.
