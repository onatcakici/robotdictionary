    Rovers

    MAGGIE

    Also known as Mars Automated Giant Gizmo for Integrated Engineering, MAGGIE engineering test rover, MAGGIE rover

    n., rover. Jet Propulsion Laboratory, United States.

    Engineering test rover, its name short for Mars Automated Giant Gizmo for Integrated Engineering, used by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory as an Earth testbed for NASA's Curiosity rover.
