    Underwater and marine

    Sentry

    n., underwater robot. Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution. In service.

    Autonomous underwater vehicle made by the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution that carries instruments for collecting samples, images and readings in the deep sea.

    Measures and facts
    MakerWoods Hole Oceanographic Institution
    StatusIn service

    Of note

    • Designed to dive to 6,000 metres (20,000 ft)
