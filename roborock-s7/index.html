    Wheeled machines

    Roborock S7

    n., wheeled machine. Roborock, China, 2021. In service.

    Laser navigating vacuum and mop that scrubs hard floors with a vibrating pad and lifts that pad automatically when it detects carpet.

    Measures and facts
    MakerRoborock
    CountryChina
    First shown2021
    StatusIn service
    Runtime3 h
    ActuationTwo drive wheels, rubber main brush and a sonic vibrating mop plate
    Power5,200 mAh lithium battery with a charging dock
    SensorsSpinning laser scanner, ultrasonic carpet sensor, cliff and bumper sensors
    AutonomyLaser SLAM with multi floor maps, room labels and no go zones

    The S7 combines a spinning laser scanner for mapping with a mop plate that vibrates up to 3,000 times a minute to scrub dried stains. An ultrasonic sensor recognises carpet and raises the mop about 5 mm so it can vacuum rugs on the same run. Maps are stored for several floors with room labels, no go zones and per room suction settings. It has since been succeeded by later Roborock models, though large numbers remain in use.

    Of note

    • Sonic mop plate vibrates up to 3,000 times a minute
    • Raises the mop about 5 mm when carpet is detected
    • Peak suction rated at 2,500 Pa
