    Solix

    n., wheeled machine. Solinftec, Brazil, 2022. In production.

    Solar powered four wheeled scouting and spraying robot that inspects individual plants and applies herbicide only where weeds are found in row crops.

    Measures and facts
    MakerSolinftec
    CountryBrazil
    First shown2022
    StatusIn production
    ActuationFour wheeled lightweight electric drive with a boom mounted sensing and spray head
    PowerOnboard solar panels charging a battery pack
    SensorsCameras with edge processing for plant level weed, pest and disease detection
    AutonomyAutonomous field scouting and targeted spraying coordinated by the company's ALICE decision platform

    Solix is a light four wheeled electric robot topped with solar panels, designed to work day and night in soybean, cotton, sugarcane and cereal fields. Cameras and onboard processing identify weeds, insects and disease at plant level, feeding the company's farm decision platform. A sprayer variant treats only the plants it flags, cutting chemical volumes sharply, and a self refilling workflow keeps it running without a crew. Machines operate in Brazil and in the United States Midwest.

    Of note

    • Developed in Brazil and field tested with United States land grant universities including Purdue
    • The Solix Sprayer variant reached commercial availability
    • Designed to run around the clock on solar charge with self refilling spray logistics
