    UVD Robot

    n., wheeled machine. Blue Ocean Robotics, Denmark, 2018. In production.

    Mobile ultraviolet disinfection robot that drives itself around hospital rooms and exposes surfaces to short wavelength light after manual cleaning.

    Measures and facts
    MakerBlue Ocean Robotics
    CountryDenmark
    First shown2018
    StatusIn production
    Height1.78 m
    Mass120 kg
    Top speed0.75 m/s (2.7 km/h)
    ActuationDifferential drive base carrying a vertical array of ultraviolet C lamps
    PowerRechargeable battery
    SensorsLidar, three 3D cameras and passive infrared motion sensors
    AutonomyMap based route recording with autonomous, semi manual and static modes

    The UVD Robot is a wheeled base carrying a vertical array of ultraviolet C lamps that damage the genetic material of pathogens on exposed surfaces. Lidar, three 3D cameras and passive infrared sensors let it drive a recorded route through wards and operating theatres, and shut the lamps off if a person is detected. It was developed with Odense University Hospital in Denmark and shipped in large numbers during the 2020 pandemic response, mostly to hospitals, airports and offices.

    Of note

    • Developed with Odense University Hospital in Denmark
    • Shipped in large numbers during the 2020 pandemic response
    • A third generation Model C followed in 2020
