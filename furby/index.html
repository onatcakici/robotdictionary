    Furby

    Also known as Furbies, Furbys

    n., robot. Hasbro, United States, 1998.

    Talking robot toy resembling an owl, made by Hasbro subsidiary Tiger Electronics, that became a sought after holiday gift following its October 1998 launch.

    Measures and facts
    MakerHasbro
    CountryUnited States
    First shown1998

    Of note

    • First released in October 1998
    • Over 40 million sold in its original three years of production
    • Its speech was translated into 17 languages
