    Underwater and marine

    COTSBot

    n., underwater robot. Queensland University of Technology, Australia. Research platform.

    Small autonomous underwater vehicle from Queensland University of Technology that finds crown of thorns starfish on the Great Barrier Reef and kills them by injection.

    Measures and facts
    MakerQueensland University of Technology
    CountryAustralia
    StatusResearch platform

    Of note

    • About 1.4 m long
    • Uses a neural network to spot starfish in camera images
    • Injects a bile salt solution through a needle on a folding arm
